  (`recipes/red-wine-braised-oxtail.md`, kept on file and in the rotation). **Fallback: if oxtail
  can't be found, use beef shank** (user's call). Shank is leaner with less bone, so buy roughly
  2–2.3kg instead of 2.6kg, cut into ~5cm chunks, and start testing for tenderness at 2 hours —
  it may need up to 2½. The sauce will be a little less rich and sticky without the oxtail
  gelatin; the flour thickening still helps. Those weights and times are estimates, not from the
  recipe.

  peeled prawns, 600g streaky pork mince, 3 eggs, five spice beancurd skin. **Steamed 15 minutes
  first, then cooled, coated in cornflour and deep-fried** — so it is cooked through before it
  is fried, which is good for doneness. Rolling and frying details are not in the source.
