# Fragrant Glutinous Rice 腊味糯米饭 (Rice Cooker Glutinous Rice)

> Rice-cooker glutinous rice with dried mushrooms, dried shrimp and Chinese sausage, seasoned with oyster sauce and dark soy.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (2 cups / 310g glutinous rice)

## Ingredients

### Main & aromatics

- A little oil
- 30g dried shrimp, rehydrated in 1 cup of water for about 20 minutes
- 6 dried mushrooms, rehydrated and cut into small pieces
- 80g Chinese sausage, casing removed (soak in hot water for about 1 minute), cut into small pieces — the creator notes chicken sausage can be used instead
- 310g / 2 cups glutinous rice, soaked for about 2 hours

### Seasoning & cooking liquid

- 1½ tbsp oyster sauce
- 1½ tbsp premium dark soy sauce
- A few dashes ground white pepper
- 310ml / 2 cups water

## Method

1. **Sauté the mushrooms and dried shrimp:** Set a multi-cooker or rice cooker to open-lid cooking mode and add a little oil. Add the rehydrated, chopped mushrooms, then the rehydrated dried shrimp. Stir-fry together until fragrant.
2. **Chinese sausage:** Add the cut Chinese sausage and stir-fry continuously for 1–2 minutes to render out the oil and release the aroma without burning.
3. **Rice and seasonings:** Add the soaked glutinous rice, oyster sauce, dark soy sauce and white pepper. Stir thoroughly to combine evenly.
4. **Water:** Pour in the water and push everything down so it is evenly submerged.
5. **Cook:** Turn off the open-lid cooking function, close and lock the lid, switch to rice-cooking mode and cook until complete.
6. **Finish and serve:** Release any residual steam, open the lid, fluff the rice and serve.
