# Cereal Pork Chop 麦片排骨

> Crisp-fried pork loin tossed in buttery instant cereal with curry leaves and chilli — the zi char classic, sweet-savoury and fragrant. A reunion-dinner favourite.

**Servings:** 4–6

## Ingredients

### Marinated pork

- 300g boneless pork loin, sliced & lightly pounded
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp Chinese rice wine
- A few dashes white pepper
- 4 tbsp water
- 1 egg white
- 3 tbsp cornflour
- Cooking oil, for frying

### Cereal coating

- 20g butter
- 3 cloves garlic, chopped
- 3 sprigs curry leaves
- 1 red chilli, deseeded & chopped
- 1 egg yolk
- 70g instant cereal
- 2 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt

## Method

1. **Marinate:** Mix the sliced, lightly pounded pork loin with the oyster sauce, light soy sauce, rice wine, white pepper and water until the liquid is absorbed. Work in the egg white, then the cornflour, and finish with a little cooking oil to stop the pieces sticking.
2. **Fry:** Deep-fry the pork until golden and cooked through. Drain well.
3. **Cereal base:** Melt the butter in a clean wok. Fry the chopped garlic, curry leaves and red chilli until fragrant.
4. **Egg yolk:** Add the egg yolk and stir quickly so it breaks into fine strands in the butter.
5. **Cereal:** Add the instant cereal, sugar and salt. Stir over low heat until the cereal turns golden, sandy and aromatic.
6. **Toss:** Return the fried pork to the wok and toss until every piece is coated in the cereal mixture. Serve immediately while crisp.
