# Yam Rice 芋头饭

> One-pot Hokkien-style rice cooked with chunks of yam, chicken thigh, dried mushrooms and dried shrimp. Savoury, fragrant and a meal on its own.

**Servings:** 3–4

## Ingredients

- 2 shallots, thinly sliced
- 4 cloves garlic, chopped
- 6 dried mushrooms, thinly sliced
- 30g dried shrimp, washed & rehydrated
- 240g skinless chicken thigh meat
- ½ tsp salt
- A few dashes white pepper
- 200g yam, cut into chunks
- 2 cups uncooked jasmine rice (1 cup = 159g)
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- ½ tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tsp chicken stock powder
- 2 cups water (1 cup = 175ml)
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce

## Method

1. **Aromatics:** Fry the sliced shallots until fragrant and lightly golden, then add the chopped garlic, sliced dried mushrooms and rehydrated dried shrimp. Stir-fry until aromatic.
2. **Chicken:** Add the chicken thigh meat and stir-fry until it changes colour. Season with the salt and white pepper.
3. **Yam:** Add the yam chunks and toss to coat.
4. **Season:** Stir in the 1 tbsp light soy sauce, ½ tbsp dark soy sauce and the chicken stock powder.
5. **Rice:** Add the uncooked jasmine rice and stir to coat every grain. Add the water and the final 1 tbsp dark soy sauce.
6. **Cook:** Transfer to the rice cooker (or cover and cook on low) until the rice is done and the yam is soft. Fluff and rest before serving.
