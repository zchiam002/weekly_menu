# Steamed Ribs with Silken Tofu 蒸排骨豆腐

> Pork ribs marinated with fermented bean paste, oyster sauce and garlic, steamed over a bed of silken tofu so the juices run into the tofu. Finished with garlic oil and spring onion.

**Servings:** 3–4

## Ingredients

### To clean the ribs

- 280g (9.9 oz) pork ribs, cut into smaller pieces
- 2–3 tbsp plain flour
- Water, for cleaning

### Marinated ribs

- A few dashes white pepper
- ½ tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- ½ tbsp oyster sauce
- ½ tbsp fermented bean paste 黄豆酱
- 2 cloves garlic, chopped
- 2 tbsp cornflour
- 1 tbsp sesame oil

### Other

- 300g (10.6 oz) silken / soft tofu
- 2 tbsp cooking oil
- 2 cloves garlic, chopped
- Spring onions, chopped
