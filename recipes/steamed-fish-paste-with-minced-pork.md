# Steamed Fish Paste with Minced Pork

> BoBo xi dao fish paste mixed with minced pork and a lot of garlic paste until sticky, pressed flat on
> a plate, drizzled with oil and steamed.

**Servings:** About 650–700g of mixture — one dish in a multi-dish meal (4–5 people as written)

## Ratios (by volume, not weight)

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Fish paste | the base |
| Minced pork | **⅓ of the fish paste** |
| Garlic paste (蒜泥) | **¼ to ⅓ of the fish paste** |

## Ingredients

- 2 tubs BoBo xi dao fish paste (200g each, 400g)
- Minced pork — ⅓ of the fish paste by volume: about 150g, or 2 small packets of about 75g
- Garlic paste — ¼ to ⅓ of the fish paste by volume: about 100–130ml, roughly 100–130g or 20–25
  cloves, minced to a paste *(grams and cloves are estimates)*
- White pepper, to taste *(about ¼ tsp — estimate)*
- A little cooking oil, to drizzle on top *(about 1 tbsp — estimate)*

No light soy sauce and no salt — the fish paste is already salty.

## Method

1. Put the fish paste, minced pork, garlic paste and white pepper in a bowl.
2. Mix everything until it is sticky — stir in one direction for 2–3 minutes, then slap the mixture
   against the bowl until it holds together.
3. Press it flat on a plate, about 2cm thick (a plate of about 22–24cm).
4. Drizzle a little cooking oil over the top.
5. Steam for 15 minutes over a rolling boil. *(The message said "七蒸15" — see the note below.)*
6. Check the centre: no pink, hot all through. Cut into it to look, then serve as it is.
