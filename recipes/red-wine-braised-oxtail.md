# Red Wine Braised Oxtail (4 Hour Oxtail Stew)

> Classic red-wine oxtail braise — seasoned oxtail browned hard, then braised four to five hours in red wine, light chicken stock and bay over a roasted soffritto, and served over creamed mashed potatoes. The West Country Kitchen.

**Servings:** 3–4 (oxtail weight not stated — "standard oxtail portions in a standard Dutch oven")

## Ingredients

### Oxtail

- Fresh oxtail pieces *(weight not stated)*
- Sea salt and coarsely ground black pepper, generous amount
- Olive oil, a generous splash for browning

### Soffritto base

- 2–3 carrots, cut into chunky pieces
- 2–3 stalks celery, chopped chunky to match the carrots
- 2 onions, skinned and cut into large chunks

### Sauce & cooking liquid

- About 1 tbsp tomato paste
- About ⅓ bottle good quality red wine
- Light chicken stock, enough to cover the meat *(quantity depends on the pot)*
- 2–3 bay leaves

### To serve

- Creamed mashed potatoes

## Method

1. **Chop the vegetables:** Wash and chop the carrots, celery and onions into chunky, uniform pieces. Combine in one bowl and set aside.
2. **Season and brown the oxtail:** Season the oxtail generously with sea salt and coarse black pepper. Heat a generous splash of olive oil in a Dutch oven over high heat. Brown the oxtail in batches until golden brown and caramelized on all sides. Remove and set aside in a bowl.
3. **Sauté the vegetables and tomato paste:** Lower the heat to medium and add the vegetables to the remaining oil. Season with salt and pepper and sauté until roasted and golden brown. Stir in the tomato paste and cook 3–5 minutes, stirring constantly.
4. **Deglaze with red wine:** Pour in about ⅓ bottle of red wine. Simmer a few minutes to reduce the alcohol and concentrate the flavour.
5. **Add stock and braise:** Return the browned oxtail and any juices to the pot. Cover with light chicken stock and add the bay leaves. Bring to a boil on high heat, cover, and transfer to a preheated oven at 140–150°C for 4–5 hours.
6. **Serve:** Remove the pot from the oven. Serve the tender oxtail and rich sauce hot over creamed mashed potatoes.
