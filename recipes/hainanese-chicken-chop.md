# Hainanese Chicken Chop 海南炸鸡排

> Butterflied boneless chicken legs marinated in soy, coated in flour, egg and crushed cream crackers and double-fried, served with twice-fried potato wedges and a sweet-and-sour tomato gravy with peas.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (2 boneless chicken legs, 4–5 potatoes)

## Ingredients

### Chicken & marinade

- 2 pieces boneless chicken leg, butterflied
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp salt
- A few dashes crushed white pepper

### Breading & coating

- Cream crackers (quantity not specified)
- Plain flour (quantity not specified)
- Egg wash (quantity not specified)

### Potato wedges

- 4–5 raw potatoes, cut into pieces
- Oil, for deep frying (quantity not specified)
- 1 tsp salt

### Gravy / sauce

- A little oil
- 1 medium red onion, thinly sliced
- 4 tbsp tomato sauce
- 2 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
- 200ml water
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 2 tbsp white vinegar
- Cornstarch solution (~1 tbsp cornflour + 2 tbsp water)
- Canned green peas, blanched (quantity not specified)

## Method

1. **Marinate the chicken:** Butterfly the chicken legs. Combine with the light soy sauce, salt and crushed white pepper, mix well and marinate for at least 2 hours.
2. **Cracker crumbs:** Put the cream crackers in a ziplock bag and smash as fine as possible, then tip onto a large plate.
3. **Coat the chicken:** Dredge the marinated chicken in plain flour (pressing down and shaking off the excess), dip it in the egg wash, then coat thoroughly in the cracker powder, pressing down slightly. Let it rest.
4. **First fry — potatoes:** Heat oil in a pot over medium heat. Add the potatoes and fry until they float and are slightly golden, then lift out with a strainer (keep the heat on).
5. **Second fry — potatoes:** Heat the oil until very hot (test with a bamboo chopstick until bubbles rise rapidly). Return the potatoes and fry on high heat for 10–15 seconds until crisp on the outside. Take out and toss immediately with the 1 tsp salt.
6. **First fry — chicken:** Heat oil over medium heat. Place the chicken skin-side down and leave for about 1 minute so the crumbs set, then flip and fry until golden brown. Remove.
7. **Second fry — chicken:** Heat the oil to medium-high or high until bubbles rise rapidly. Return the chicken for 10–15 seconds to crisp the outside and stop oil lodging in the crumb. Remove and drain.
8. **Gravy:** Heat a pan over medium-low heat with a little oil and fry the sliced red onion until softened. Add the tomato sauce and sweet chilli sauce, then the water, and turn the heat up slightly to bring it to a boil. Add the sugar, light soy sauce and white vinegar, mix thoroughly and slowly stir in the cornstarch solution until thickened. Stir in the blanched canned peas.
9. **Serve:** Turn off the heat and pour the sauce over the fried chicken chop.
