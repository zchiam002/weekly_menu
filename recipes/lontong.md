# Lontong (Curry Vegetables & Compressed Rice 咖喱什锦菜饭团)

> Lontong — compressed rice cakes served with a vegetable curry (sayur lodeh) of carrot, long beans, tofu and cabbage in a spiced coconut gravy, topped with toasted grated coconut.

**Servings:** Not specified in source

## Ingredients

### Compressed rice (nasi impit)

- Not captured in the source — add from the video

### Spice paste (rempah)

- 2 red chillies
- 1 big red onion
- 3 cloves garlic
- ~10 shallots
- 30g dried shrimp, rehydrated
- 10g lengkuas or blue ginger
- 10g young ginger
- 3 candlenuts — the creator notes cashew nuts can be used instead
- 10 dried chillies, rehydrated, boiled and deseeded
- 2 stalks lemongrass, white part only, cut
- 1 cup water (the water used to hydrate the dried shrimp)

### Curry & vegetables base

- 10g belacan (fermented prawn paste)
- A little oil
- 1 tbsp turmeric powder
- 1 tbsp curry powder (meat curry powder)
- 800ml water
- 1 chicken stock cube
- 170g carrot, cut into strips
- 140g long beans
- 2 pieces firm tofu (tau kwa), cut in half and pan-fried until browned
- 250g cabbage
- 400ml coconut milk
- 1 tsp + ¼ tsp salt
- ½ tsp sugar

### Toasted grated coconut topping (serundeng)

- 80g grated coconut
- ¼ tsp salt

## Method

### Vegetable curry (sayur lodeh) & toasted coconut

1. **Blend the spice paste:** In a food processor blend the red chillies, red onion, garlic, shallots, dried shrimp, lengkuas, young ginger, candlenuts, dried chillies, lemongrass and the 1 cup of shrimp-soaking water until finely minced.
2. **Dry-fry the belacan:** Heat a Dutch oven or pot over low heat without oil. Add the belacan and dry-fry, breaking it up with a spatula, until it crumbles easily and is fragrant.
3. **Sauté the paste:** Push the belacan to one side, add a little oil and turn the heat up to medium. Add the blended rempah and stir-fry until dry.
4. **Spices:** Add the turmeric and curry powder and stir-fry with the paste until dry.
5. **Boil the vegetables:** Pour in the water and add the chicken stock cube, carrot, long beans, tofu (halved and pre-fried) and cabbage. Cover and bring to a boil for 10 minutes.
6. **Coconut milk and seasoning:** Pour in the coconut milk and turn the heat down to low so it doesn't over-boil. Add 1 tsp salt and the sugar, stir well, taste, and add the extra ¼ tsp salt.
7. **Steep:** Cover, turn off the heat and let the curry sit for 20 minutes before serving.
8. **Toast the coconut:** Heat a pan over medium-low (or low) heat without oil. Add the grated coconut and ¼ tsp salt and stir constantly until golden brown and fragrant. Remove and set aside to top the lontong.
