# Braised Pork Belly with Potatoes (土豆炖红烧肉 / 马铃薯炖五花肉)

> Red-braised pork belly cubes caramelised in rock sugar and simmered with star anise and cinnamon, with potatoes added to soak up the glossy gravy.

**Servings:** Not specified in source

## Ingredients

### Main ingredients

- 500g pork belly, cut into bite-sized cubes
- 2 medium potatoes, peeled and cut into medium chunks
- 1–2 tbsp cooking oil
- Boiling water, enough to submerge the meat

### Aromatics & spices

- 3–4 slices ginger
- 2 stalks spring onions / scallions, cut into sections
- 2 cloves garlic, smacked
- 1–2 star anise
- 1 small cinnamon stick
- 1–2 dried chillies (optional)

### Seasoning & sauce

- 20g rock sugar (or 1 tbsp granulated sugar)
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce (for deep rich colour)
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- ½ tsp salt (adjust to taste)

### Garnish

- Chopped spring onions or sesame seeds

## Method

1. **Blanch the pork belly:** Place the cubes in a pot with cold water and a few slices of ginger and spring onion sections. Bring to the boil over medium-high heat and simmer 3–5 minutes to remove impurities and scum. Drain, rinse with warm water and pat dry.
2. **Caramelise the sugar (糖色):** Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok or heavy-bottomed pot over low heat. Add the rock sugar and stir gently until it melts into an amber-red foam.
3. **Brown the pork and add aromatics:** Immediately add the blanched pork and toss rapidly to coat each piece in the caramel. Add the ginger, garlic, star anise, cinnamon stick and dried chillies. Stir-fry 2–3 minutes until fragrant and the fat starts rendering out.
4. **Season and simmer:** Pour the Shaoxing wine around the edge of the wok, followed by the light soy sauce, dark soy sauce and oyster sauce. Toss well. Pour in enough boiling water to fully cover the pork. Bring to the boil, then reduce to low, cover and simmer gently 35–40 minutes until the meat is tender.
5. **Add the potatoes:** Add the potato chunks, pushing them down into the braising liquid. Cover and continue simmering over low-medium heat 15–20 minutes until the potatoes are fork-tender and have absorbed the gravy.
6. **Thicken and serve:** Remove the lid, taste and add salt if needed. Turn the heat up to medium-high and reduce the sauce until thick and glossy and clinging to the pork and potatoes. Garnish with spring onions and serve hot with steamed rice.
