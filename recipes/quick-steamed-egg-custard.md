# Quick Chinese Steamed Egg Custard (水蒸蛋 / 芙蓉蛋)

> Silky, mirror-smooth steamed egg custard made with strained eggs and warm water, finished with a light soy and sesame oil drizzle and spring onion.

**Servings:** Not specified in source

## Ingredients

### Egg custard

- 3 large eggs, at room temperature
- Warm water, approx. 1.5 times the volume of the eggs (around 220–250ml)
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp cooking oil or sesame oil (optional, for smoother texture)

### Seasoning & garnish

- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1–2 tbsp warm water, to dilute the soy sauce mixture slightly
- 1 stalk spring onion / scallion, finely chopped

## Method

1. **Whisk the eggs:** Crack the eggs into a bowl, add the ½ tsp salt and whisk gently until completely blended, without creating too many bubbles.
2. **Add warm water and strain:** Measure the egg mixture volume and add warm water (around 40°C / 104°F) at a ratio of 1 part egg to 1.5 parts water. Whisk gently to incorporate. Pass through a fine-mesh sieve into a shallow heat-proof steaming bowl or dish. Skim off any remaining surface bubbles with a spoon for a silky, mirror-like top.
3. **Steam:** Cover the bowl tightly with cling wrap or a heat-proof plate/lid, so condensation doesn't drip onto the custard. Bring the steamer water to a gentle boil over medium-low heat. Place the covered bowl in, cover the steamer and steam on medium-low heat 10–12 minutes. Turn off the heat and rest inside the covered steamer another 3–5 minutes.
4. **Season and serve:** Carefully remove the bowl and the wrap or lid. Cut a shallow grid or criss-cross into the top with a butter knife so the sauce can penetrate. Mix the light soy sauce, sesame oil and warm water, and drizzle evenly over the custard. Garnish with spring onions and serve immediately.
