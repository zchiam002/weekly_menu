# Malaysian Chicken Curry with Potato (Kari Ayam) 咖喱鸡

> The most common Malaysian home curry — chicken and potato simmered in a spiced coconut gravy with lemongrass, star anise and cinnamon. Potato soaks up the flavour and thickens the sauce.

**Servings:** 4–6

## Ingredients

### Curry paste
- 8 shallots
- 3 cloves garlic *(reduced)*
- 1 inch ginger
- 2 stalks lemongrass, white part
- 6–8 dried chillies, soaked *(moderate for less heat)*
- 3 tbsp meat curry powder
- 1 tsp turmeric

### Other
- 1kg chicken (bone-in), cut into pieces
- 400g starchy potatoes, peeled and cubed
- 400ml coconut milk
- 300ml water or chicken stock
- 2 sprigs curry leaves
- 1 star anise, 1 cinnamon stick
- Salt & a little sugar to taste

## Method

1. Blend the curry paste. Heat oil and fry the paste with the curry leaves, star anise and cinnamon until fragrant and the oil splits.
2. Add the chicken and stir to coat; brown lightly.
3. Add the potato, coconut milk and water/stock. Bring to a gentle simmer.
4. Cover and simmer ~40–60 min until the chicken is tender and the potato soft and the gravy thickened. Season with salt and a touch of sugar.
