# Hot and Sour Soup

> Classic Chinese hot and sour soup — pork, mushrooms, black fungus and tofu in a peppery, tangy broth thickened and finished with egg ribbons and a homemade Sichuan chilli oil.

**Servings:** 4–6

## Ingredients

### Chilli oil

- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns
- 2 tsp chilli powder

### Soup

- Cooking oil
- 100g pork loin, cut into strips
- 60g shredded carrot
- 8 dried mushrooms, cut into strips
- 60g black fungus, cut into strips
- 80g ham, cut into strips
- 1.5L water
- 1 cube chicken bouillon
- 1 piece soft tofu (soup-suitable)
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp black vinegar
- ½ tbsp white pepper
- 1 tsp salt
- Cornstarch solution
- 2 beaten eggs

## Method

1. **Chilli oil:** Gently heat the 2 tbsp oil with the Sichuan peppercorns to infuse, then pour over the chilli powder (remove the peppercorns). Set aside.
2. **Prep:** Rehydrate the dried mushrooms and black fungus, then cut into strips along with the pork, carrot and ham. Cut the tofu into strips.
3. **Build the broth:** Heat a little cooking oil in a pot and lightly fry the pork until it changes colour. Add the water and chicken bouillon and bring to a boil.
4. **Add solids:** Add the mushrooms, black fungus, carrot and ham. Simmer until everything is tender, then add the tofu strips.
5. **Season:** Stir in the soy sauce, black vinegar, white pepper and salt.
6. **Thicken:** Stir in the cornstarch solution until the soup thickens to a silky consistency.
7. **Egg ribbons:** With the soup at a gentle simmer, slowly drizzle in the beaten eggs while stirring in one direction to form fine ribbons.
8. **Serve:** Ladle into bowls and top with the Sichuan chilli oil to taste.
