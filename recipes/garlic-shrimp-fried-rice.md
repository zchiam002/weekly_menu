# Grandma's Garlic Shrimp Fried Rice 阿嬷蒜香虾仁蛋炒饭

> Egg fried rice with a whole bulb of garlic and semi-cooked prawns tossed back in at the end, seasoned with fish sauce and plenty of dark soya sauce for colour.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (rice cooked from 380g raw rice, about 3 small cups)

## Ingredients

### Main & aromatics

- Oil (quantity not specified)
- 160g prawns / shrimp, deshelled and deveined (from about 250g in the shell)
- 1 whole bulb garlic, chopped
- Cooked, refrigerated rice from 380g raw rice (3 small cups)
- 6 eggs, beaten
- 1 big bunch spring onions, chopped (about 3 stalks)

### Seasoning & sauce

- 1½ tsp chicken stock powder — the creator notes MSG can be used instead
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp fish sauce — the creator notes soy sauce can be used instead
- 3–5 tbsp premium dark soya sauce (the creator added 5 tbsp in total)

## Method

1. **Semi-cook the prawns:** Heat a suitable amount of oil in a wok or pan over medium heat. Stir-fry the prawns briefly until they change colour and are semi-cooked. Remove and set aside.
2. **Sauté the garlic:** In the same pan add a little more oil and the chopped garlic. Stir-fry, without burning it, until golden brown and fragrant.
3. **Rice:** Add the cooked, refrigerated rice. Press gently with a spatula to break up lumps and mix well with the garlic.
4. **Season and dry out:** Add the chicken stock powder, black pepper and salt. Mix thoroughly to dry out the moisture in the rice.
5. **Eggs:** Push the rice to the side, add a little more oil into the space, pour in the beaten eggs and stir from the sides to semi-cook.
6. **Combine:** Push the rice over the semi-cooked eggs and mix together thoroughly. Turn the heat up to high and stir-fry until the rice dries up and starts to pop and jump around in the pan.
7. **Fish sauce and prawns:** Spread the fish sauce around the pan and mix well. Add the semi-cooked prawns back and toss to combine.
8. **Dark soya sauce:** Add the dark soya sauce, 3–5 tbsp (the creator added 5), mixing thoroughly until it reaches the colour you want.
9. **Finish:** Add the spring onions, toss well, turn off the heat and serve.
