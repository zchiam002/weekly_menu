# Sesame Crispy Chicken 芝麻香鸡

> Deboned skin-on chicken marinated with five-spice, ginger juice and aromatics, brushed with egg wash, crusted in sesame seeds and fried, finished with a hot-oil bath.

**Servings:** Not specified in source

## Ingredients

### Marinade

- 2 tbsp Knorr SavorRich chicken concentrated seasoning
- 1 tbsp young ginger juice
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1½ tbsp Chinese cooking wine or Shaoxing wine
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp five-spice powder
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 3 cloves garlic, smashed
- 4 shallots, smashed
- 2 stalks spring onion, smashed and tied into a knot

### Main, coating & frying

- Deboned chicken, skin on (quantity not specified)
- 1 egg, beaten well, for the egg wash
- Sesame seeds, a generous amount
- Cooking oil, for frying (quantity not specified)

## Method

1. **Mix the marinade:** In a casserole or ziplock bag combine the chicken concentrated seasoning, ginger juice, light soy sauce, Chinese cooking wine, salt, five-spice powder, sesame oil, smashed garlic, smashed shallots and the spring onion knot. Mix thoroughly.
2. **Marinate the chicken:** Place the deboned chicken skin-side down in the marinade. Rub the spring onion all over the chicken, then push the garlic, shallots and spring onion underneath it. Cover with cling wrap and marinate in the fridge for at least 2 hours (or overnight).
3. **Before frying:** Take the chicken out of the fridge at least 30 minutes before frying. Pick off and discard all the garlic and shallot pieces.
4. **Egg wash and sesame seeds:** Lay the chicken skin-side up on a flat tray. Spread the egg wash evenly over the skin, then sprinkle a generous amount of sesame seeds across it.
5. **Fry the first side:** Heat a wok of oil over medium heat until hot (test with a wooden skewer until bubbles rise rapidly). Carefully place the chicken in the oil and fry for 3 minutes.
6. **Flip and fry the second side:** Carefully turn the chicken over and gently fry the other side until cooked through and golden brown.
7. **High-heat oil bath:** Turn the heat up to high and spoon the hot oil over the chicken to render out excess oil and brown the skin further.
8. **Rest and serve:** Remove the chicken from the oil and let it rest for 10–15 minutes before cutting and serving.
