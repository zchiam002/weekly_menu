# Hakka Yam Pork Belly 芋头扣肉

> Hakka pork belly with taro — sliced pork belly marinated in oyster sauce, soy, five spice and
> fermented bean curd, pan-fried with taro, then packed into a bowl and steamed for 1½ hours.

**Servings:** 6–8

## Ingredients

### Pork & marinade (marinate at least 1 hour)

- 280g sliced pork belly
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp premium dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp five spice powder
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 piece fermented bean curd
- 1 tbsp fermented bean curd liquid
- 1 tbsp Chinese white rice wine

### Pan-fry with the pork

- 250g taro (yam)
- 5g sakura prawns (optional)
- 3 cloves garlic, chopped
- 6 shallots, chopped

## Method

1. **Marinate:** Combine the pork belly with the oyster sauce, light soy, dark soy, five spice,
   sugar, fermented bean curd, fermented bean curd liquid and rice wine. Marinate at least 1 hour.
2. **Pan-fry:** Pan-fry the marinated pork, along with the taro, sakura prawns (if using), garlic
   and shallots.
3. **Steam:** Pack everything tightly into a steaming bowl and steam for 1½ hours in a rice cooker.
