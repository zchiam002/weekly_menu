**Servings:** ~6–7 as a main dish, scaled for 6.5 pax *(estimated — his own file gives no number)*

## Ingredients

### Rice base

- 5–6 cups cooked jasmine rice (from ~2½ cups raw), a day old if possible — dry, not sticky
- 3 tbsp butter
- 4–5 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 medium onion, diced
- ½ tsp salt, to taste

### Seafood & mushroom

- 300g salmon, skin off, cut into 2cm cubes
- 300g prawns, peeled and deveined
- 200g scallops
- 200g mushrooms, sliced

### White sauce

- 3 tbsp butter
- 3 tbsp plain flour
- 500ml milk
- 100ml heavy cream (optional, for a richer sauce)
- 1 tsp chicken stock powder
- Salt and white pepper, to taste

### Cheese topping

- 150g cheddar, shredded
- 200g mozzarella, shredded

## Method

1. **Rice:** Melt the butter in a large pan, sauté the garlic and onion until fragrant. Add the
   cooked rice, toss to coat evenly with the butter and aromatics, season lightly with salt. Set
   aside, spread across a large baking dish (roughly 30×25cm or two smaller dishes).
2. **Seafood:** In the same pan, sear the salmon, prawns and scallops briefly — just until the
   surface changes colour, not cooked through, since they'll finish in the oven. Remove and set
   aside. Sauté the mushrooms in the same pan until they release their liquid and start to brown.
3. **White sauce:** Melt the butter in a saucepan, whisk in the flour, cook 1 minute stirring
   constantly. Gradually whisk in the milk to avoid lumps, then the cream. Simmer, stirring, until
   thickened enough to coat the back of a spoon. Stir in the chicken stock powder, season with
   salt and white pepper.
4. **Assemble:** Scatter the seared seafood and mushrooms over the rice. Pour the white sauce
   evenly over the top. Cover generously with the shredded cheddar and mozzarella.
5. **Bake:** 200°C (390°F), 15–20 minutes, until the cheese is fully melted, golden in patches
   and bubbling at the edges. If the top isn't browning enough, finish under the broiler/grill for
   1–2 minutes, watching closely.
6. **Doneness check:** Before serving, check a piece of salmon and a scallop from the centre of
   the dish — the seafood should be opaque all the way through, no translucent centre. The bake
   time above assumes 2cm salmon cubes; thicker pieces need longer.
