# Black Pepper Chicken Udon 黑椒乌冬鸡面

> Udon stir-fried in a butter, garlic and black pepper sauce with strips of chicken thigh, scrambled egg and choy sum.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (400g udon, 250g chicken thigh)

## Ingredients

### Scrambled eggs

- Oil, a suitable amount
- 3 eggs, beaten

### Chicken & seasoning

- A little more oil
- 250g boneless chicken thigh, skin removed, cut into thin strips
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ⅓ tsp salt

### Sauce base & aromatics

- A little oil
- 15g butter
- 5 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1½ tbsp black pepper (reduce if you prefer it less spicy)
- 330ml water
- 1½ tbsp oyster sauce
- ½ tsp sugar

### Noodles, greens & finishing

- 400g udon noodles
- 1 bunch favourite greens (choy sum used in the video)
- ~2 tbsp premium dark soy sauce

## Method

1. **Scramble the eggs:** Heat a wok over medium heat with a suitable amount of oil. Pour in the beaten eggs and scramble until set. Chop up the eggs, remove and set aside.
2. **Fry the chicken:** In the same wok over medium heat add a little more oil and the chicken strips. Season with the ½ tsp black pepper and ⅓ tsp salt. Fry until no longer red and about 80% done. Remove and set aside.
3. **Sauce base:** Keeping the heat at medium, add a little oil and the butter to the wok. Add the garlic and 1½ tbsp black pepper and fry until fragrant. Pour in the water, oyster sauce and sugar, stir well and bring to a boil.
4. **Cook the udon:** Add the udon to the boiling sauce over medium heat and move it around gently to separate the noodles (about 1 minute).
5. **Combine and finish:** Add the scrambled eggs, fried chicken and greens, then the dark soy sauce, and mix well. Turn the heat up to medium-high to evaporate and thicken the sauce slightly. Turn off the heat and serve.
