# Black Pepper Chicken Udon (黑椒乌冬鸡面)

> 15-minute stir-fried udon with marinated chicken, onion and bell peppers in a glossy coarse black pepper and oyster sauce.

**Servings:** Not specified in source

## Ingredients

### Chicken & marinade

- 300g boneless chicken thigh or breast, sliced into bite-sized strips
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine
- ½ tsp cornstarch
- A dash of white pepper

### Stir-fry & aromatics

- 2 packs fresh udon noodles (approx. 400g total)
- 3–4 cloves garlic, minced
- ½ medium yellow onion, sliced
- ½ red bell pepper, sliced
- ½ green bell pepper, sliced
- 2–3 tbsp cooking oil

### Black pepper sauce mixture

- 1½ tbsp coarsely ground black pepper (adjust to taste)
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce (for colour)
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 3–4 tbsp water or chicken stock

## Method

1. **Marinate the chicken:** Combine the sliced chicken with the light soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, cornstarch and white pepper. Mix well and marinate about 10 minutes.
2. **Sauce:** In a small bowl, whisk together the black pepper, oyster sauce, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil and water until well combined. Set aside.
3. **Udon:** Blanch the udon in boiling water 1–2 minutes just to loosen it. Drain immediately and rinse with cool water to stop the cooking and prevent sticking.
4. **Sear the chicken:** Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat. Stir-fry the chicken 3–4 minutes until browned and cooked through. Remove and set aside.
5. **Vegetables and noodles:** In the same wok, add another 1 tbsp oil. Stir-fry the garlic and onion until fragrant and slightly softened. Add the red and green bell peppers and toss about 1 minute. Add the blanched udon, then the cooked chicken.
6. **Final toss:** Pour the sauce evenly over everything. Toss vigorously over high heat 2–3 minutes until the udon absorbs the sauce, turns glossy and everything is piping hot. Serve immediately.
