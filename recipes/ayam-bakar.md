# Ayam Bakar 印尼烤鸡 (Indonesian Grilled Chicken)

> Indonesian grilled chicken marinated in calamansi juice, simmered in a fried lemongrass, kaffir lime, turmeric and tamarind rempah with kecap manis, then baked briefly to finish.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (1 whole chicken, ~1kg)

## Ingredients

### Chicken & marinade

- 1 whole chicken (~1kg), cut into big pieces
- Juice of 10 calamansi — the creator notes lemon or lime juice can be used instead

### Spice paste (rempah)

- 4 kaffir lime leaves
- 10g lengkuas / blue ginger
- 2 stalks lemongrass, white stem only
- 10g young ginger
- 4 cloves garlic
- 3 shallots
- Big red chilli (quantity not specified)
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- ½ tsp coriander seed powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp tamarind paste
- 125ml water, to help the blending

### Cooking base & liquids

- A little oil
- 2 bay leaves
- 100ml kecap manis (sweet black sauce)
- 250ml water

## Method

1. **Marinate the chicken:** Cut the chicken into big pieces. Pour over the calamansi juice, mix well and set aside.
2. **Blend the rempah:** In a blender combine the kaffir lime leaves, lengkuas, lemongrass, young ginger, garlic, shallots, big red chilli, turmeric, coriander seed powder, salt, tamarind paste and the 125ml water. Blend thoroughly into a smooth paste.
3. **Fry the rempah:** Heat a pan over medium heat with a little oil. Add the blended rempah and the bay leaves and fry for about 10 minutes, stirring continuously, until thickened and dry.
4. **Kecap manis and coat the chicken:** Reduce the heat to medium-low or low. Add the kecap manis and stir well. Add the marinated chicken (leaving out the calamansi juice) and turn the heat back up to medium. Coat all the pieces thoroughly in the paste.
5. **Simmer:** Add the 250ml water and mix well. Cover and cook over medium heat for 10 minutes. Flip the chicken, reduce the heat to low, put the lid back on and cook for another 10 minutes.
6. **Reduce the sauce:** Remove the lid and turn the heat up to medium-high to reduce the sauce until it clings to the chicken.
7. **Bake and serve:** Preheat the oven to 200°C (top and bottom fan). Place the chicken skin-side up on a baking tray lined with baking paper and spoon some sauce over the top. Bake at 200°C for about 6–10 minutes (the creator baked for 6; if using an air fryer, reduce to 180°C). Serve with the remaining sauce poured over rice.
