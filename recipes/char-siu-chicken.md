# Char Siu Chicken 叉烧鸡

> Whole chicken marinated in a char siu-style mix of soy, honey, five-spice and red fermented tofu sauce, roasted over potatoes, carrot and onion, and served with a gravy made from the reserved marinade.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (1 whole chicken, ~1kg, with roast vegetables)

## Ingredients

### Char siu marinade

- 6 tbsp light soy sauce
- 2 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp young ginger juice
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ tsp five-spice powder
- 4 cloves garlic, smashed
- 3 tbsp brown sugar
- 3 tbsp sauce from red fermented tofu 南乳
- A few dashes white pepper

### Main

- 1 fresh whole chicken (~1kg)

### Vegetable bed

- 1 onion, quartered (yellow or red)
- 4 potatoes, halved
- 1 carrot, cut into chunks
- 1 tsp salt
- A splash of oil

### Gravy / dipping sauce

- Reserved chicken marinade, strained to remove the garlic
- 80ml water
- Cornstarch solution (quantity not specified)

## Method

1. **Marinade:** In a ziplock bag combine the light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, oyster sauce, ginger juice, honey, salt, five-spice powder, smashed garlic, brown sugar, red fermented tofu sauce and white pepper. Mix well.
2. **Marinate the chicken:** Put the chicken in the bag, press out the excess air and seal tightly. Massage the marinade into the chicken, including inside the cavity. Marinate in the fridge for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight.
3. **Vegetables:** In a bowl toss the quartered onion, halved potatoes and carrot chunks with the salt and a splash of oil.
4. **Preheat and set up the pot:** Preheat the oven to 180°C. Line the bottom of a Dutch oven with baking paper and spread the seasoned vegetables evenly over it.
5. **Prep the chicken:** Rest the chicken at room temperature for 1 hour. Place it breast-side up on the bed of vegetables. Tie the drumsticks together with butcher's string and wrap the wing tips in aluminium foil so they don't burn.
6. **First roast:** Roast at 180°C for 40 minutes, basting generously with the marinade every 10–15 minutes.
7. **Flip and second roast:** Flip the chicken over and roast at 180°C for another 15–20 minutes (about 1 hour in total) until fully cooked and browned.
8. **Dipping sauce:** Pour the remaining marinade into a saucepan, add the 80ml water and bring to a boil over medium-low heat. Stir in the cornstarch solution until thickened to your liking. Serve alongside the chicken and roasted vegetables.
