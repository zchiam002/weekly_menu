# Sichuan Veg & Bitter Gourd Soup 四川菜苦瓜排骨汤 (Pork Rib Soup with Bitter Gourd & Sichuan Vegetables)

> Pork ribs and chicken feet simmered with bitter gourd, tomato, garlic and preserved Sichuan vegetables into a clear soup — on the stove or in a thermal cooker.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (3L water, 600g pork ribs, 500g chicken feet)

## Ingredients

### Soup base & main

- 3L water
- 600g pork ribs, blanched
- 500g chicken feet, blanched
- 350g bitter gourd, inner white layer scraped off
- 1 thumb-size piece ginger, sliced
- 1 tomato, cut into wedges
- 150g Sichuan vegetables 四川菜, cut and rinsed twice
- 10 cloves garlic, skin on, washed

### Seasoning

- 1 tsp sea salt

## Method

1. **Combine:** In a pot combine the water, blanched pork ribs, blanched chicken feet, bitter gourd, ginger, tomato wedges, rinsed Sichuan vegetables and the garlic cloves (skin on).
2. **Initial boil:** Bring to a boil and boil for 10 minutes.
3. **Simmer:**
   - **Thermal cooker (as used in the video):** Transfer the pot into a thermal / simmer pot, cover and leave for 8 hours.
   - **Standard pot (suggested in the video):** Simmer on the stove for 1½–2 hours.
4. **Season and serve:** Taste and stir in the sea salt (or to taste). Serve hot.
