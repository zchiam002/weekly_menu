# Black Pepper Beef 黑胡椒牛肉

> Tender beef marinated with baking soda and cornflour, then stir-fried with red onion in a buttery
> coarse black pepper and oyster sauce glaze.

**Servings:** 4–6 (as stated by the user, 4 Oct 2026 — fits 5 as written, no scaling)

## Ingredients

### Beef marinade (20 minutes)

- 350g chuck tender beef
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- ½ tsp baking soda
- 2 tbsp cornflour
- A few dashes white pepper
- 60ml (¼ cup) water

### Stir-fry

- 1 tbsp coarsely pounded black peppercorns
- 40g butter
- Cooking oil
- 1 medium red onion
- 1½ tbsp oyster sauce
- 120ml (½ cup) water
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp sugar
- 4 stalks spring onion
- 1 tbsp premium dark soy sauce
- Cornstarch solution
- 2 tbsp Chinese cooking wine (Shaoxing Huatiao)

> **If black pepper beef is the main dish**, 350g across 5 people is light (about 70g each).
> Consider 400–450g beef with the sauce as written *(estimate, not from the source)*.

## Method

1. **Marinate:** Mix the beef with the oyster sauce, light soy sauce, baking soda, cornflour, white
   pepper and water until the liquid is absorbed. Marinate for 20 minutes.
2. **Prep:** Slice the red onion, cut the spring onion into 4–5cm lengths and mix the cornstarch
   solution.
3. **Sear the beef:** Stir-fry in hot oil until browned and no pink is left, then set aside.
4. **Pepper base:** Melt the butter in the wok. Fry the black pepper and red onion until fragrant.
5. **Sauce:** Add the oyster sauce, water, salt, sugar and dark soy sauce. Bring to a simmer.
6. **Finish:** Return the beef, add the spring onion and Shaoxing wine, and thicken with the
   cornstarch solution. Toss until glossy and serve hot.
