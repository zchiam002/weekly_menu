# Dry Mee Siam Goreng 干炒米暹

> Spice N' Pans dry-fried mee siam — rice vermicelli tossed in a tamarind-tomato rempah with prawns, beancurd and chives, topped with egg strips and a squeeze of calamansi.

**Servings:** 3–4

## Ingredients

### Condiments
- 3 eggs — fried into a thin omelette, cooled and cut into strips
- 200g firm beancurd

### Rempah paste
- 6 shallots
- 4 cloves garlic
- 10 dried chillies, rehydrated & deseeded
- 50g dried shrimp, soaked in ½ cup (125ml) water *(keep the soaking liquid)*
- 25g belacan, dry-fried until it crumbles
- Some cooking oil

### Other
- Some cooking oil
- 1 tbsp tau cheo (fermented soybean paste)
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- Tamarind water (50g tamarind paste + 1 cup hot water)
- 1½ tsp sugar
- 300g prawns, deshelled & deveined
- Dried shrimp soaking liquid (from the rempah)
- 250g rice vermicelli, soaked in room-temp water 1 hour
- Some water
- 70g chives
- Juice from 5 calamansi

## Method

1. Blend the rempah. Fry in oil until fragrant and darkened.
2. Stir in the tau cheo and tomato paste, then the tamarind water, sugar, dried-shrimp soaking liquid and a little water; simmer to a gravy.
3. Add the beancurd and prawns; cook until the prawns are done.
4. Add the soaked vermicelli; toss until coated and heated through, adding water if needed.
5. Toss through the chives near the end. Finish with the calamansi juice; top with egg strips.
