# Easy Stir-Fried Garlic Beef Broccoli (蒜香牛肉炒西兰花)

> Quick stir-fry of tender marinated sliced beef and blanched broccoli in a glossy garlic oyster sauce.

**Servings:** Not specified in source

## Ingredients

### Beef & marinade

- 300g flank steak or tenderloin, thinly sliced across the grain
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine
- ½ tsp baking soda (optional, for tenderising the beef)
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- 1 tsp cooking oil (to seal in moisture)

### Broccoli & stir-fry aromatics

- 1 medium head broccoli, cut into small bite-sized florets
- 4–5 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, minced
- 2–3 tbsp cooking oil

### Garlic stir-fry sauce

- 1½ tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- ½ tsp dark soy sauce (for colour)
- ½ tsp sugar
- ½ tsp sesame oil
- ¼ cup water or beef stock
- 1 tsp cornstarch

## Method

1. **Marinate the beef:** In a bowl, combine the sliced beef with the light soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, baking soda and cornstarch. Mix well until the liquid is absorbed. Mix in the 1 tsp oil to coat and set aside 15–20 minutes.
2. **Stir-fry sauce:** In a small bowl, whisk together the oyster sauce, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil, water and 1 tsp cornstarch until smooth. Set aside.
3. **Blanch the broccoli:** Bring a pot of salted water to the boil. Blanch the florets 1–2 minutes until vibrant green and tender-crisp. Drain immediately and submerge in ice water (or rinse under cold running water) to stop the cooking and keep them bright green. Drain well.
4. **Sear the beef:** Heat 1–2 tbsp oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat until smoking. Add the beef in a single layer. Sear untouched for 1 minute, then stir-fry another 1–2 minutes until browned but slightly underdone in the centre. Remove and set aside.
5. **Stir-fry and combine:** Add another 1 tbsp oil to the same wok over medium heat. Stir-fry the garlic and ginger about 30 seconds until fragrant. Add the blanched broccoli and the beef back into the wok. Give the sauce a quick stir and pour it in.
6. **Finish:** Toss rapidly over high heat 1–2 minutes until the sauce thickens, turns glossy and coats every piece evenly. Serve immediately with steamed white rice.
