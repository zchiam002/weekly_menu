# Homemade Egg Tofu with Broccoli & Shiitake Mushrooms 自制鸡蛋豆腐和西兰花与香菇

> Egg tofu made from eggs and unsweetened soy milk, steamed in a tray, cut and shallow-fried, then topped with broccoli, shiitake and carrot in an oyster sauce.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (the egg tofu makes 4 portions; 1 portion is used for this dish)

## Ingredients

### Part 1: Homemade egg tofu

- 7 eggs
- ½ tsp salt
- ½L (500ml) unsweetened soy bean milk
- A little water (for lining the steaming tray with baking paper)
- Oil, for shallow frying (quantity not specified)

### Part 2: Broccoli & shiitake mushroom dish

- A little oil
- 3 slices young ginger
- 1 clove garlic, chopped
- 1 cup water
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- A few broccoli chunks
- A few fresh shiitake mushrooms
- A few slices of carrot
- ¼ tsp sugar
- ⅓ tsp salt
- Cornstarch solution (quantity not specified)
- 1 tbsp Chinese cooking wine
- 1 tsp sesame oil

## Method

### Part 1: Making & frying the egg tofu

1. **Egg base:** In a bowl combine the eggs and salt and mix well. Add the soy milk and stir together thoroughly.
2. **Pour and strain:** Line a steaming tray with baking paper (a little water under the paper helps it stick to the tray). Pour the egg mixture through a sieve into the tray, pressing down with a spoon, and remove any surface bubbles.
3. **Cover and steam:** Cover the tray tightly with aluminium foil (shiny side facing down). Place in a steamer over boiling water and steam for 30 minutes, or until firm.
4. **Test and cool:** Insert a bamboo satay stick into the centre; if it comes out clean, turn off the heat, take the tray out of the steamer and let it cool down completely.
5. **Unmould and cut:** Invert the tray onto a flat surface, carefully peel off the baking paper and pat the egg tofu dry with a kitchen towel. Divide into 4 equal portions (1 portion is used for this recipe).
6. **Shallow-fry:** Heat a pan of oil over medium-to-low heat. Gently slide in the portioned egg tofu and shallow-fry for 1–2 minutes on the first side until golden brown, flip carefully with a spatula and chopstick, and fry the second side for another minute. Remove and set aside.

### Part 2: Broccoli & shiitake with sauce

1. **Aromatics:** Heat a little oil in a pan. Fry the ginger and garlic until fragrant.
2. **Boil and steam the vegetables:** Pour in the water and oyster sauce and mix well. Once boiling, add the broccoli, shiitake and carrot, cover and steam for about 10–15 seconds.
3. **Season:** Uncover and stir in the sugar and salt.
4. **Thicken:** Slowly stir in the cornstarch solution until the sauce thickens to your liking.
5. **Finish:** Drizzle the Chinese cooking wine around the side of the pan to evaporate slightly. Turn off the flame and stir in the sesame oil.
6. **Assemble and serve:** Pour the cooked vegetables and sauce over the fried egg tofu.
