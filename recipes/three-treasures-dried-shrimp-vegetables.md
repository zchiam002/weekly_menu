# Stir-Fried Vegetables with Dried Shrimp / "Three Treasures" (虾米炒三宝)

> Shallow-fried eggplant, okra and long beans tossed with garlic, dried shrimp and a light oyster sauce, topped with crispy dried shrimp.

**Servings:** Not specified in source

## Ingredients

### Vegetables ("Three Treasures")

- 1 eggplant / brinjal (approx. 285g), cut into batons
- 5 pieces okra / ladies' fingers (approx. 66g), halved
- Long beans (approx. 75g), cut into pinky-finger length
- 2–3 tbsp cornstarch, for coating the eggplant
- Salted water, for soaking the eggplant

### Aromatics & dried shrimp

- 30g dried shrimp, soaked in 60ml water for 15 minutes and roughly chopped (reserve the soaking liquid)
- 4 cloves garlic, chopped
- Cooking oil, for shallow frying

### Sauce & seasoning

- Reserved dried shrimp soaking liquid (60ml)
- ½ tbsp oyster sauce
- ⅓ tsp sugar

## Method

1. **Prepare the eggplant and okra:** Cut the eggplant into thick batons and immediately soak in salted water to prevent oxidation. Remove from the water and drain (no need to dry completely), then toss thoroughly with 2–3 tbsp cornstarch to prevent excess oil absorption during frying. Trim the ends of the okra and slice each piece in half lengthwise.
2. **Shallow-fry the vegetables:** Heat oil in a wok over medium heat until chopsticks produce bubbles. Shallow-fry the long beans about 1 minute until the skin blisters and softens; remove and drain. Shallow-fry the cornstarch-coated eggplant about 1 minute until lightly golden; remove and drain. Shallow-fry the okra about 1 minute until blistered; remove and drain.
3. **Crisp half the dried shrimp:** Lower the oil heat and add half of the chopped dried shrimp. Fry gently until golden, crispy and fragrant. Strain and set aside on paper towels as a topping. Remove most of the oil from the wok, leaving about 1–2 tbsp.
4. **Build the sauce:** Over medium heat, add the remaining dried shrimp and fry until fragrant. Add the garlic and stir-fry until aromatic and golden. Pour in the reserved soaking liquid, oyster sauce and sugar, and stir to combine.
5. **Toss and serve:** Add all the pre-fried vegetables back to the wok and toss quickly over high heat so they absorb the sauce evenly. Transfer to a serving dish and top with the reserved crispy dried shrimp. Serve hot.
