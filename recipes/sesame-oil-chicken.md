# Sesame Oil Chicken 麻油鸡

> Small whole chicken marinated in oyster sauce and ginger juice, browned with sesame oil, ginger and garlic in a clay pot and stewed with button mushrooms in stock and rice wine, finished with a generous pour of Shaoxing wine.

**Servings:** 2

## Ingredients

### Main & aromatics

- 1 small whole chicken
- ~3 thumb-size pieces ginger, smashed
- 1 thumb-size piece ginger, grated (squeeze for the juice, keep the pulp)
- ~3–4 cloves garlic, chopped
- 2 cans button mushrooms (bigger ones cut up, smaller ones kept whole), liquid from the cans reserved
- CP Smart Soup / chicken stock (quantity not specified)

### Marinade

- ~2 tbsp oyster sauce
- Ginger juice, squeezed from the grated ginger
- ~1 tbsp light soy sauce

### Cooking liquids & finishing

- ~2 tbsp sesame seed oil, for frying the aromatics, plus a few dashes to finish
- ~2 tbsp Chinese rice wine (mijiu)
- Dark soy sauce (chicken rice dark soy sauce) (quantity not specified)
- ½ tsp sugar
- ~2 cupfuls Shaoxing wine
- Cornstarch solution (quantity not specified)

## Method

1. **Marinate the chicken:** Put the chicken in a bowl with the oyster sauce, the ginger juice (keep the pulp) and the light soy sauce. Mix well and set aside to marinate.
2. **Sauté the aromatics:** Heat a clay pot and add ~2 tbsp sesame seed oil. Add the garlic, smashed ginger and the reserved grated ginger pulp and sauté until fragrant.
3. **Brown the chicken:** Add the marinated chicken to the clay pot and brown for about half a minute.
4. **Rice wine:** Add the Chinese rice wine (mijiu) and scrape up any chicken bits sticking to the bottom of the pot.
5. **Liquids and first simmer:** Pour in the reserved liquid from the 2 cans of button mushrooms and the CP Smart Soup (chicken stock). Bring to a boil and simmer for 10 minutes.
6. **Mushrooms and second simmer:** Add the button mushrooms (bigger ones cut up, smaller ones whole). Cover and simmer for another 10 minutes until the chicken is tender.
7. **Colour and season:** Add dark soy sauce for the colour you want and stir in the sugar.
8. **Shaoxing wine and thicken:** Pour in the Shaoxing wine. Slowly add the cornstarch solution bit by bit, stirring, until the gravy thickens.
9. **Finish:** Drizzle a few dashes of sesame seed oil on top for fragrance, dish up and serve.
