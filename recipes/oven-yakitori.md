# Oven Yakitori

> Japanese chicken and scallion skewers with a sticky soy-mirin glaze, baked in the oven — 焼き鳥

**Servings:** 7 skewers (approx. 2-3 servings)

## Ingredients

### Glaze (basting sauce)
- 1/3 cup (~85ml) water
- 1/2 cup (~125ml) sake
- 1/3 cup (~85ml) black soy sauce
- 1/3 cup (~85ml) mirin
- 1/2 onion, cut in half
- Green part of 1 scallion
- 1 tbsp brown sugar

### Skewers
- 2 boneless chicken thighs (~210g each, 420g total)
- 2 scallion stems, cut into bite-sized lengths
- Bamboo skewers, soaked in water for ~30 minutes
- Cooking oil for the grill rack (amount not specified)

## Preparation

1. Soak the bamboo skewers in water for about 30 minutes so they don't burn.
2. Make the glaze: in a non-stick saucepan over low heat, combine the water, sake, black soy sauce, mirin, brown sugar, onion and scallion green. Stir constantly until thick and sticky enough to coat the back of a spoon.
3. Remove from heat and cool. Split the sauce: 2/3 for basting during cooking, 1/3 kept for brushing after.
4. Cut each chicken thigh in half, then into bite-sized pieces (skin on keeps it juicier). Cut the scallions to the same length.
5. Thread each skewer, alternating scallion and chicken: 4 scallion pieces and 3 chicken pieces per skewer. Pierce the skin first and fold the chicken over to secure. Makes about 7 skewers.
6. Preheat the oven to 220°C (428°F). Lightly oil the grill rack and lay the skewers on it.
7. Bake on the middle rack for 10 minutes, until about 80% cooked.
8. Brush both sides of each skewer with the 2/3 basting sauce. Return to the top shelf and bake another 10 minutes to char slightly and cook through.
9. Brush with the remaining 1/3 sauce for a glossy finish and serve.
