# Udon Noodle Soup (Kake Udon) かけうどん

> Thick udon noodles in a light, clear Japanese broth. 15 minutes. Great with yasai tempura on the side.

**Servings:** 4

## Ingredients

### Broth
- 4 cups dashi — *use a kombu (kelp) dashi or chicken broth to keep it seafood-free*
- 3–4 tbsp light soy sauce
- 2–3 tbsp mirin
- *(Sake — substitute with a splash of water)*
- Pinch of salt & sugar, to taste

### Noodles & toppings
- 4 portions udon noodles (fresh or frozen)
- Spring onion, sliced
- Optional: a soft-cooked egg (fully set), or serve the yasai tempura on the side
- *(Skip kamaboko / fish cake)*

## Method

1. Gently warm the dashi/broth with the soy, mirin, salt and sugar; taste and adjust.
2. Cook the udon in boiling water per packet, then drain into bowls.
3. Pour the hot broth over the noodles. Top with spring onion (and egg / tempura if using).
