# Balinese Style Braised Beef 巴厘风味焖牛肉

> Beef shank slow-braised with lemongrass, kaffir lime, turmeric and kecap manis until fork-tender, then thickened into a sweet-savoury Southeast Asian gravy.

**Servings:** 3–4

## Ingredients

- 700g (24.7 oz) beef shank, knuckles, brisket or short ribs, cut into large chunks
- 30g (1.1 oz) young ginger, sliced
- 5 shallots, finely chopped
- 3 stalks lemongrass, white parts only, lightly smashed
- 4–5 kaffir lime leaves, lightly bruised
- 5 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 1 tsp coriander seed powder
- ½ tsp ground nutmeg
- 2 tsp turmeric powder
- ½ tsp chilli flakes
- 2 tsp salt
- 120ml (4.2 fl oz) kecap manis (Indonesian sweet soy sauce) — if using a very thick brand like ABC, reduce to 80ml (2.8 fl oz) and adjust to taste at the end of cooking
- Water, enough to completely submerge the beef
- A little cornflour solution, to thicken
