# Tomato Soup

> Rich, silky tomato soup — whole peeled tomatoes simmered with sweated onion and garlic, blended smooth and finished with cream and fresh basil.

**Servings:** ~4–6 | **Total time:** ~50 min

## Ingredients

- 2 cans (28 oz each) whole peeled tomatoes
- 1 large yellow onion (plus a little extra), roughly chopped
- 6 cloves garlic (or more), minced
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter
- 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 3 cups vegetable stock (or chicken stock / water)
- 1 tbsp granulated sugar (balances the acidity)
- ½–1 cup heavy cream (to taste, at the end)
- Pinch dried thyme and dried oregano
- Fresh basil leaves (to finish)
- Salt and black pepper (at least ¼ tsp pepper to start)

## Method

1. **Prep (5 min):** Chop the onion. Smash the garlic cloves to loosen the skins, then finely mince.
2. **Sweat the onions (8 min):** Set a large Dutch oven over medium heat. Melt the butter with the olive oil. Add the onions and sauté, stirring occasionally, ~8 min until translucent and completely softened.
3. **Infuse the garlic (5 min):** Add the minced garlic and stir constantly ~5 min until highly aromatic. Don't burn it — burnt garlic turns the whole soup bitter.
4. **Add liquids & simmer (30 min):** Pour in both cans of tomatoes with their juices. Add the stock, sugar, a generous pinch of salt, ¼ tsp black pepper, thyme and oregano. Bring to a rolling boil, then reduce to low and simmer uncovered 30 min to reduce and intensify.
5. **Blend (3 min):** Off the heat, use an immersion blender in the pot until smooth and silky. Warning: don't over-blend in a high-speed countertop blender when using extra virgin olive oil — over-processing can turn it intensely bitter.
6. **Finish with cream (2 min):** Stir in the heavy cream for a velvety richness that cuts the acidity. Taste and adjust salt and pepper.
7. **Garnish & serve (1 min):** Ladle into bowls, top with torn basil, and serve with croutons or a slice of grilled cheese bread.
