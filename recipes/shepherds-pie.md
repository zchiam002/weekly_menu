# Shepherd's Pie

> Baked beef and pork mince with onion, carrot, celery and corn in a savoury gravy, topped with buttery mash, Pecorino and panko, baked golden and crisp. (Technically a cottage pie; the original lamb version is noted below.)

**Servings:** 5.5 (about 520g per portion; ~2.85kg cooked total)

## Ingredients

### Filling
- 400g (14 oz) beef mince
- 400g (14 oz) pork mince
- 1½ brown onions, finely diced
- 2 carrots, finely diced
- 1½ sticks celery, finely diced
- 1½ corn cobs, kernels removed (about 1 cup)
- 3–4 cloves garlic, finely diced
- Small bunch thyme, finely chopped
- Small sprig rosemary, finely chopped
- 1½ tbsp olive oil
- 1½ tbsp tomato paste
- 8 dashes Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tbsp flour
- 600ml (2½ cups) chicken stock
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Mash topping
- 1.4kg (3 lbs) potatoes, suitable for mashing (e.g. Sebago)
- 70g (2.5 oz) butter
- 70ml (2.4 fl oz) milk
- Small handful panko breadcrumbs
- 20g (0.7 oz) Pecorino cheese, grated

## Method

1. Heat a large heavy pot over medium-high with a drizzle of oil. Brown the beef and pork mince in two batches (pinch of salt each), keeping the heat high and not crowding the pot, and removing each batch as it browns.
2. Return the pan to high heat. Sauté onions and carrots 2–3 min. Add garlic, rosemary, thyme and celery; stir. Add tomato paste, Worcestershire and flour; cook 2–3 min, stirring.
3. Return the meat with the chicken stock. Season and simmer on medium-low 20–25 min until the gravy coats a spoon. Pork must be fully cooked through. Stir corn kernels through in the last 2 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, boil peeled potatoes in cold salted water until fork-tender (25–30 min). Mash with butter and milk until smooth.
5. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F). Transfer the meat to an oven dish (~25×20cm). Top with mash, peaking it for crispy edges. Sprinkle Pecorino and panko.
6. Bake 30–40 min until golden and crisp on top and steaming hot throughout.

## Portions and nutrition (estimate)

- Yield ~2.85kg cooked: ~1.4kg filling + ~1.45kg mash.
- Full portion ~520g (~250g filling + ~270g mash); half portion ~260g.
- Per full portion: ~730–780 kcal, 33–36g protein, ~40g fat, ~55g carbs.
- Keeps 3 days in the fridge; freezes up to 3 months. Bake from frozen at 180°C for ~50 min.

## Original lamb version (8 servings)

Swap the meat for 1.5kg lamb mince, use 2 onions, 3 carrots, 2 celery sticks, 2 corn cobs, 5 garlic cloves, a small bunch each of rosemary and thyme, 2 tbsp olive oil, 2 tbsp tomato paste, 12 dashes Worcestershire, 3 tbsp flour, 1 litre stock, 2kg potatoes, 100g butter, 100ml milk and 30g Pecorino. Brown the lamb in thirds and bake in a ~30×25cm dish for 35–40 min.
