# Pasta Arrabbiata

> Classic Italian penne all'arrabbiata — a rustic, fiery sauce of hand-crushed peeled tomatoes, garlic, chilli and olive oil, finished with parsley and Pecorino. 15 minutes.

**Servings:** 3

## Ingredients

- 300g penne pasta
- 2 small cans Italian peeled tomatoes (approx. 800g total)
- 3 cloves garlic *(1 per person — reduce for a milder, gentler sauce)*
- Fresh red chillies, to taste *(reduce/omit for less heat)*
- 4–5 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 bunch fresh parsley, finely chopped
- Salt & black pepper
- Pecorino Romano, grated (to serve)

## Method

1. **Prep the sauce base:** Empty the peeled tomatoes into a bowl and gently crush by hand or fork into a chunky, rustic texture. Finely chop the chillies so they melt into the sauce.
2. **Sauté aromatics safely:** Warm a large pan over medium heat with a generous pour of olive oil. Stir 1 tbsp water into the oil right before the garlic (stops it burning). Add crushed garlic + chopped chilli; sauté gently ~30 sec to infuse.
3. **Simmer the sauce:** Pour in the crushed tomatoes. Lower to medium and simmer 15 min. Around 5 min in, squash any large chunks with a wooden spoon to release the juices.
4. **Cook the pasta:** Meanwhile, cook the penne in well-salted boiling water until strictly al dente (~10 min), stirring early so it doesn't stick.
5. **Season the sauce:** 2 min before the pasta is done, season the sauce with salt, plenty of black pepper and a healthy handful of chopped parsley. Stir.
6. **Combine & toss:** Transfer the penne straight from the water into the sauce (don't drain). Add a splash of starchy pasta water to emulsify. Turn up the heat slightly and toss vigorously 1–2 min to marry.
7. **Garnish & serve:** Plate, top with extra parsley and grated Pecorino Romano. Serve immediately.
