# Grilled Cheese & Sheet Tray Tomato Soup

> Nick's perfect grilled cheese — a three-cheese sourdough sandwich with a butter-mayo-Parmesan crust and a fused Parmesan cheese skirt — paired with an easy roasted sheet-tray tomato soup.

**Servings:** 1–2 | **Total time:** ~40–45 min

## Ingredients

### Sheet tray tomato soup

- 1 bunch vine-on tomatoes
- 1 large yellow onion, quartered
- 1 large clove garlic, smashed
- Olive oil (for drizzling)
- Heavy cream (a small splash at the end)
- Salt and fresh cracked black pepper
- Boiling water (backup — to adjust thickness)

### Perfect grilled cheese

- 2 thick slices sourdough bread
- Grated cheese mix: equal blend of Fontina, soft Gouda and sharp Cheddar
- Unsalted butter, softened (for spreading, plus extra for the pan)
- Mayonnaise
- Parmesan, finely grated (for the exterior crust)

## Method

### Part 1: Sheet tray tomato soup

1. **Prep & season (5 min):** Preheat oven to 425°F (218°C). Lay the vine-on tomatoes on a sheet tray. Add the quartered onion wedges and the smashed garlic clove. Drizzle generously with olive oil, salt and plenty of black pepper.
2. **Roast (25–30 min):** Roast at 425°F until the tomatoes and onions are softened, slightly charred and juicy.
3. **Blend (5 min):** Cool a minute, then pull off and discard the tomato vines. Scrape all the roasted vegetables and juices into a blender. Blend low to high until smooth. If too thick, pour a splash of boiling water onto the tray to lift stuck-on flavour and add it in. Finish with a small splash of heavy cream, more pepper and a pinch of salt; quick final blend. Keep warm on low heat.

### Part 2: Perfect grilled cheese

1. **Grate & mix (5 min):** Grate the Fontina, Gouda and Cheddar along the long face of a box grater for long strands. Toss gently by hand so they combine without packing.
2. **Build the outer crust (3 min):** Spread a thin layer of softened butter on the outside faces of both slices. Paint a light layer of mayonnaise over the butter (acts like an egg wash for a golden crust). Press a light dusting of grated Parmesan into the butter-mayo layer.
3. **Stuff (2 min):** Turn the bread crust-side down. Pile the cheese mix thickly onto one slice, filling to the edges, then close the sandwich.
4. **Fry & melt (5 min):** Melt a little butter in a pan over medium heat. Lay the sandwich down — it should sizzle gently. Press lightly for even contact. When the bottom is golden, flip. Cover with a lid to trap steam and melt the core. Trick: tilt the pan, drop a splash of water into the empty side away from the bread, and cover instantly — the steam melts the cheese without sogging the crust.
5. **Fused Parmesan finale (2 min):** Once the inside is gooey, lift the sandwich out briefly. Wipe the pan centre clean, grate a fresh layer of Parmesan directly onto the hot pan the size of the bread, set the sandwich on top, cover 90 sec, and fry until it forms a crisp laced cheese skirt.
6. **Plate & serve (1 min):** Ladle the soup into a bowl; finish with olive oil, black pepper and parsley. Pull the sandwich to reveal a plain crust on one side and a crispy Parmesan crust on the other. Tear open and serve.
