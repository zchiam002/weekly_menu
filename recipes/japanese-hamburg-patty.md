# Japanese Hamburg Patty (Hambāgu) ハンバーグ

> Champ's juicy Japanese hamburg steak — a beef-and-pork patty bound with a soy-milk-panko tsunagi (no egg) and studded with carrot, onion and shiitake, finished in a glossy red wine demi-glace.

**Servings:** 4 (4 thick patties)

## Ingredients

### Meat & aromatics
- 400g ground meat (6:4 beef to pork, or 100% beef)
- ½ medium carrot, finely cubed
- ½ medium onion, finely diced
- 2 shiitake mushrooms, stems removed, finely cubed
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- A few generous shakes ground nutmeg
- Plain flour, for a light dusting
- 1 tsp olive oil, for pan-frying

### Tsunagi (binder paste)
- 120ml soy milk
- 20g panko breadcrumbs

### Red wine demi-glace sauce
- 70ml red wine
- 35g ketchup
- 20g Worcestershire sauce
- 4–5 cherry tomatoes, halved
- 2–3 white button / cremini mushrooms, sliced
- 2 tsp honey
- 15g unsalted butter

## Method

1. **Tsunagi:** Whisk the soy milk + panko; rest ~10 min until it becomes a paste. Finely cube the carrot, onion and shiitake.
2. **Mix:** Pat the meat dry; in a chilled bowl mix briefly until sticky. Add salt, pepper, nutmeg; mix. Fold in the carrot, onion, shiitake and the tsunagi until uniform (work fast to keep the fat cold).
3. **Shape:** Divide into 4; slap each between your hands to knock out air pockets; shape thick ovals. Chill 10 min, then lightly flour both sides.
4. **Cook:** Sear in olive oil ~1 min to a golden crust. Flip, cover, drop heat to low; after a minute add 1 tbsp water, cover and steam 2–3 min until cooked through (60°C / for pregnancy cook fully, no pink). Rest.
5. **Sauce:** In the same pan, deglaze with the red wine and reduce a couple of minutes to cook off the alcohol. Stir in ketchup + Worcestershire, add mushrooms and cherry tomatoes, simmer 2 min. Finish with honey + butter until glossy. Spoon over the patties.
