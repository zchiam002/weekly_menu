# Chicken Rendang (仁当鸡)

> Authentic Southeast Asian chicken rendang — chicken slow-cooked in a fragrant rempah spice paste with coconut milk and toasted grated coconut (kerisik) into a thick, rich, savoury sauce. Spice n' Pans.

**Servings:** 5–6

## Ingredients

### Rempah paste (spice paste)

- 6 cloves garlic, peeled
- 4 stalks lemongrass, white part only, cut into pieces
- 10 shallots, peeled
- 15g galangal, peeled
- 20g young ginger, peeled
- 15 dried chillies, rehydrated and deseeded
- 2–3 tbsp cooking oil (or more, for easier blending)

### For frying the rempah

- 1 cinnamon stick
- 3 star anise
- 5 cloves
- 1½ tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 5 cardamom pods
- 6 kaffir lime leaves, torn from the spine and bruised

### Other

- 1 kg chicken, cut into small pieces
- 1 tbsp tamarind paste, dissolved in 2–3 tbsp hot water
- 3½ tsp salt (add 1½ tsp initially, adjust to taste later)
- 500ml coconut milk
- 100g grated coconut, tossed with a pinch of salt and toasted until lightly golden (kerisik)

## Method

1. **Blend the rempah:** Blend the garlic, lemongrass, shallots, galangal, ginger and rehydrated dried chillies with the cooking oil into a smooth paste.
2. **Fry the rempah:** Heat a wok and fry the rempah with the cinnamon, star anise, cloves, turmeric, coriander powder, cardamom pods and kaffir lime leaves. Cook patiently over medium-low heat until fragrant and the oil separates.
3. **Add chicken:** Add the chicken pieces and stir to coat well in the paste. Add the tamarind solution and 1½ tsp of the salt.
4. **Coconut milk:** Pour in the coconut milk and bring to a gentle simmer. Cook over low heat, stirring occasionally, until the chicken is tender and the sauce reduces and thickens.
5. **Kerisik:** Stir in the toasted grated coconut to deepen the flavour and thicken further. Continue simmering until the sauce is thick and clings to the chicken.
6. **Season & serve:** Adjust with the remaining salt to taste. Serve with steamed rice.
