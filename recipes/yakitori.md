# Yakitori 焼き鳥 (Japanese Satay)

> Skewers of chicken thigh and scallion baked in a hot oven and glazed with a homemade sake, soy and mirin tare.

**Servings:** Not specified in source — yields about 7–8 skewers (420g chicken thigh)

## Ingredients

### Sauce (tare)

- ⅓ cup (~85ml) water
- ½ cup (~125ml) sake
- ⅓ cup (~85ml) black soy sauce
- ⅓ cup (~85ml) mirin
- ½ onion
- Green part of 1 scallion
- 1 tbsp brown sugar

### Skewers

- 2 pieces boneless chicken thigh with skin (~210g each, ~420g total)
- 2 stems scallions
- Bamboo skewers (quantity not specified; about 7–8 for this batch)

## Method

1. **Sauce:** In a nonstick saucepan combine the water, sake, black soy sauce, mirin, onion, scallion green and brown sugar.
2. **Reduce the sauce:** Cook over low heat, stirring, until sticky and thick enough to coat the back of a spoon. Turn off the heat and let cool. Divide the sauce into ⅔ for basting during baking and ⅓ for the final coating.
3. **Soak the skewers:** Soak the bamboo skewers in water for about half an hour.
4. **Prep:** Cut the chicken thigh into bite-size pieces and cut the 2 scallion stems into matching lengths.
5. **Assemble:** Thread a piece of scallion onto a skewer, then a piece of chicken (pierce through the skin first to secure it), alternating until each skewer has 4 pieces of scallion and 3 of chicken.
6. **Preheat:** Preheat the oven to 220°C. Oil a grill rack to stop the skewers sticking and set them on top.
7. **First bake:** Bake at 220°C for 10 minutes.
8. **Baste and second bake:** Take the skewers out and baste both sides with the ⅔ basting portion. Return to the top shelf at 220°C and bake for another 10 minutes.
9. **Finish:** Remove from the oven, brush with the reserved ⅓ sauce for a glossy finish, and serve.
