# Hong Kong Swiss Wings 香港瑞士鸡翼

> Chicken wings blanched, then poached briefly in a sweet soy gravy of dark and light soy, rock sugar, star anise and Shaoxing wine, steeped off the heat and glazed in the reduced gravy.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (8 wings, cut in half)

## Ingredients

### Main & blanching

- 8 chicken wings, cut in half
- Ice-cold water, for the ice bath (quantity not specified)

### Gravy / poaching liquid & aromatics

- ~1.1L water (plus a little more if needed to cover the chicken)
- 1 piece cinnamon bark
- ~1–2 star anise
- 1 piece rock sugar (about twice the size of a thumb)
- 1 stalk spring onion, halved
- ~5 pieces ginger, thinly sliced
- 2 bay leaves
- ~5–6 shallots, halved
- 1 tsp concentrated chicken stock — the creator notes chicken stock powder or bouillon can be used instead
- ½ tsp salt
- 3 tbsp light soy sauce
- 2 tbsp dark soy sauce (+ 1 extra tbsp added later if you prefer a darker colour)
- 2 tbsp Shaoxing wine

## Method

1. **Blanch the wings:** Put the wings (cut in half) in a pot of cold water, bring to a boil and cook for about 1 minute once boiling to remove scum and impurities.
2. **Ice bath and wash:** Transfer the wings to ice-cold water to stop the cooking, then wash off any remaining scum.
3. **Boil the aromatics:** In an empty pot combine the water, cinnamon bark, star anise, rock sugar, spring onion, ginger, bay leaves, shallots, concentrated chicken stock and salt. Bring to a boil for about 10 minutes.
4. **Remove the solids and add liquids:** Scoop out and discard all the spice and aromatic solids (add a little more water if needed so the wings will be submerged). Add the light soy sauce, dark soy sauce and Shaoxing wine.
5. **Initial poach:** Add the blanched wings and boil for about 4 minutes.
6. **Steep off the heat:** Cover the pot, turn the heat off completely and let the wings steep in the hot gravy for about 20–25 minutes.
7. **Remove the wings:** Scoop the wings out and set aside (test doneness with a sharp stick — clear juices with no blood means done).
8. **Reduce the gravy:** Boil and simmer the gravy until reduced and thick enough to coat a spoon (add the optional extra 1 tbsp dark soy sauce for a darker colour).
9. **Coat and finish:** Return the wings to the reduced gravy, stir to coat every piece evenly and cook for about 2 minutes until the sauce is very thick, then dish up and serve.
