# Spicy Garlic Butter Prawns (蒜香奶油辣炒虾)

> Seared large prawns tossed in a garlic butter sauce with chilli padi, light soy and oyster sauce, finished with spring onions and herbs.

**Servings:** Not specified in source

## Ingredients

### Prawns & prep

- 500g large prawns / shrimp, veins removed, shelled or shell-on according to preference
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp white pepper
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 2–3 tbsp cooking oil, for searing

### Sauce & aromatics

- 40g unsalted butter
- 6–8 cloves garlic, minced
- 2–3 chilli padi / bird's eye chillies, chopped (adjust for heat)
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2 tbsp spring onions / scallions, chopped, for garnish
- Fresh parsley or coriander, chopped (optional)

## Method

1. **Prepare the prawns:** Pat the prawns dry with paper towels. Toss with the salt, white pepper and cornstarch until lightly coated.
2. **Sear:** Heat the oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add the prawns in a single layer and sear about 1–2 minutes per side until pink and slightly charred. Remove and set aside (do not overcook at this stage).
3. **Garlic butter sauce:** In the same pan, lower the heat to medium and add the butter. Once melted, add the garlic and chilli padi and stir-fry 1–2 minutes until fragrant and the garlic turns light golden brown. Stir in the light soy sauce, oyster sauce and sugar until well combined.
4. **Toss and finish:** Return the prawns to the pan and toss over high heat for 1 minute so the sauce coats each prawn evenly. Turn off the heat and garnish with the spring onions and parsley/coriander.
5. Serve hot with steamed rice or crusty bread.
