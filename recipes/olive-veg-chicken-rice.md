# Savoury Olive Veg Chicken Rice (电饭锅榄菜鸡饭)

> One-pot rice cooker chicken rice with preserved olive vegetables, carrots, oyster sauce and fish sauce, finished with spring onions.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (3 cups rice, about 3–4)

## Ingredients

### Main ingredients

- 3 cups jasmine rice, washed and drained (approx. 390g)
- 200g boneless chicken thigh / leg, skin removed, cut into small bite-sized pieces
- 80g preserved olive vegetables (approx. 3 tbsp)
- 200g carrots, diced into small pieces
- 390ml water

### Chicken seasoning

- ½ tsp salt
- A few dashes of white pepper

### Rice seasoning

- 1½ tbsp oyster sauce
- 1½ tbsp fish sauce (or replace with light soy sauce)
- A few dashes of white pepper

### Garnish

- Chopped spring onions, to taste

## Method

1. **Season the chicken:** In a small bowl, combine the chicken with the ½ tsp salt and white pepper. Mix well and set aside.
2. **Assemble in the rice cooker:** Add the washed and drained rice to the inner pot. Add the seasoned chicken, preserved olive vegetables and diced carrots on top of the rice.
3. **Season the rice mixture:** Add the oyster sauce, fish sauce and another few dashes of white pepper. Mix everything thoroughly, breaking up the clumped olive vegetables and dispersing all the ingredients evenly.
4. **Add water:** Pour in 390ml water. Push down any rice grains sticking to the sides of the pot and level out the top surface smoothly.
5. **Cook:** Cover and start the normal rice cooking function.
6. **Fluff and serve:** Once cooked, sprinkle chopped spring onions over the rice. Gently fluff and mix with a paddle to combine evenly. Serve hot as a complete one-pot meal.
