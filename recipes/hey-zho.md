# Hey Zho (Prawn Rolls) 虾枣

> Prawn and streaky-pork rolls wrapped in five-spice beancurd skin, steamed, cooled, coated in cornflour and deep-fried. Cut each roll into three bite-size pieces.

**Servings:** Makes 13 rolls (39 small bite-size pieces)

## Ingredients

### Filling

- 1 kg prawns, deshelled and deveined (cut ⅓ into bigger chunks, mince the rest)
- 600g minced pork (use streaky pork)
- 3 eggs
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp five spice powder
- 1 tbsp sole fish powder
- A bunch of chopped Chinese coriander leaves (or Chinese parsley)
- 7 tbsp cornflour
- 8 pieces water chestnut, diced

### Wrapping & cooking

- 2 big pieces five spice beancurd skin
- Cooking oil, to brush on the tray and on the prawn rolls
- Water, to stick the beancurd skin together when rolling
- Cornflour, to coat the steamed rolls before frying
- Oil, for deep frying

## Method

1. **Make the filling and roll:** Combine the prawns, minced pork, eggs, light soy sauce, oyster sauce, five spice powder, sole fish powder, coriander, cornflour and water chestnut. Roll the filling in the five spice beancurd skin, sticking the skin together with a little water. Brush the tray and the rolls with oil. *(The source lists these steps only implicitly — the mixing order, roll size and how the skin is cut are not written down.)*
2. **Steam:** Steam the prawn rolls for 15 minutes.
3. **Cool and coat:** Let the steamed rolls cool down first, then coat them in cornflour.
4. **Deep-fry:** Deep-fry the coated rolls. *(Oil temperature and frying time are not stated.)*
