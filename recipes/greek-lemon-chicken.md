# Greek Lemon Chicken

> Bone-in chicken thighs baked in a lemon, oregano, thyme and Dijon marinade, served with lemon herb rice and garlic air-fryer green beans.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (8 bone-in chicken thighs)

## Ingredients

### Greek lemon marinade & chicken

- ⅓ cup extra virgin olive oil
- ¼ cup fresh lemon juice, freshly squeezed
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp dried oregano
- 2 tsp dried thyme
- 2 tsp Dijon mustard
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp ground black pepper
- 8 bone-in chicken thighs

### Lemon herb rice (side)

- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- ½ medium onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- Zest of 1 lemon (~1 tbsp)
- 1½ cups basmati rice, rinsed and drained
- 2½ cups low-sodium chicken broth — the creator notes vegetable broth can be used instead
- Juice of 1 lemon (~3 tbsp)
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- ¼ cup finely chopped fresh herbs (a mix of parsley, oregano and dill; or thyme, tarragon and basil)

### Air fryer green beans (side)

- 1 pound (~450g) green beans
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp ground black pepper

## Method

### Greek lemon chicken

1. **Marinade:** In a bowl whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, thyme, Dijon mustard, salt and pepper until emulsified like a dressing.
2. **Marinate the chicken:** Put the chicken thighs in a bowl or plastic bag, pour over the marinade and toss / squish to coat fully. Marinate in the fridge for at least 1–2 hours.
3. **Bake:** Arrange the thighs side by side in a 9 × 13 inch casserole dish and spread the remaining marinade over the top. Bake at 350°F (180°C) for about 40–45 minutes, until an instant-read thermometer registers 175°F (80°C) in the thickest part (optionally baste a few times, and turn on the top oven broiler for the last 2–3 minutes for extra crispy skin).

### Lemon herb rice

1. **Sauté the aromatics and rice:** Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Sauté the onion for 3–4 minutes until softened and translucent. Add the garlic, lemon zest and rinsed basmati rice and sauté for another minute.
2. **Simmer the rice:** Pour in the chicken broth, lemon juice and salt. Stir, bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover and simmer for 12–15 minutes.
3. **Rest and finish:** Remove from the heat and let it sit covered for 4–5 minutes. Stir in the chopped herbs before serving.

### Air fryer green beans

1. **Season:** Toss the green beans with the olive oil, garlic, salt and pepper.
2. **Air-fry:** Transfer to the air fryer basket and cook at 375°F (~190°C) for 7–8 minutes, shaking the basket halfway through.
