# Nyonya Ayam Pongteh 娘惹豆酱鸡 (Braised Chicken & Potato Stew)

> Nyonya braised chicken and potato stew built on pounded garlic and shallots with fermented soy bean paste and dark soy, simmered briefly and reduced.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (1 spring chicken, ~800g)

## Ingredients

### Main & coating

- 1 whole spring chicken (~800g), cut into pieces
- 1 tsp tapioca flour
- 2 potatoes, sliced

### Aromatics

- ~8–9 cloves garlic
- ~20 shallots

### Sauce & seasoning

- ~2½–3 tbsp oil
- 1½ cups water
- 2 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp fermented soy bean paste
- 1 tbsp sugar
- A generous dash of pepper
- ~½ tsp salt

## Method

1. **Coat the chicken:** Coat the chicken evenly with the tapioca flour by hand, to give it a protective layer for tenderness.
2. **Pound the aromatics:** Coarsely pound the garlic and shallots together in a mortar and pestle (or blender).
3. **Sauté the aromatics:** Heat the oil in a pan and fry the pounded shallot and garlic until fragrant.
4. **Fry the chicken:** Add the coated chicken pieces and fry briefly together with the garlic and shallots.
5. **Potatoes and seasonings:** Add the sliced potatoes, fermented soy bean paste, dark soy sauce, sugar, water and pepper. Mix well.
6. **Simmer:** Cover, turn the heat down and simmer for about 10–15 minutes (15 in the video) until the chicken and potatoes are cooked.
7. **Reduce and finish:** Remove the lid, turn the heat up and stir to reduce the sauce to the consistency you like. Add the salt, give a final stir and serve.
