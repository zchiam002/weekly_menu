# Prosperity Yam Ring 佛钵飘香 (Yam Ring with Chicken and Seafood Stir Fry)

> Fried yam ring on a nest of puffed vermicelli, filled with a chicken, prawn and scallop stir-fry in a light oyster sauce.

**Servings:** Not specified in source

## Ingredients

### Part 1: Fried yam ring & vermicelli nest

- 300g yam, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp five-spice powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp sugar
- A few dashes white pepper
- 60g wheat flour (+ extra for dusting)
- 90ml hot water
- 30g lard — the creator notes margarine, butter or vegetable shortening can be used instead
- ½ tsp baking powder
- Oil, for deep frying (quantity not specified)
- ~35g vermicelli

### Part 2: Seafood & chicken stir-fry filling

- A little oil
- 2 cloves garlic, chopped
- 20g carrot, thinly sliced
- 135ml water
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- ½ tsp sugar
- Dashes of white pepper
- 100g chicken, thinly sliced
- 120g prawns, deshelled and deveined
- 6 scallops (~80g)
- 3 young corn, cut in half
- A few sweet peas / snow peas
- Cornstarch solution (quantity not specified)
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine / Chinese cooking wine

## Method

### Part 1: Fried yam ring & vermicelli nest

1. **Steam the yam:** Steam the thinly sliced yam over boiling water, covered, for 20 minutes until soft enough to poke easily with a chopstick.
2. **Mash and season:** Drain off any excess water from the hot steamed yam. Add the five-spice powder, salt, sugar and white pepper and mash thoroughly with a potato masher.
3. **Flour base:** In a separate bowl stir the hot water into the wheat flour, mixing thoroughly into a paste.
4. **Combine:** Add the hot flour mixture, lard and baking powder to the mashed yam. Mix until smooth and well combined.
5. **Chill the dough:** Wrap the yam dough tightly in cling wrap and freeze for 1 hour until semi-solid.
6. **Shape the ring:** Dust a tray / board and your fingers with wheat flour. Put the chilled yam paste on the board, press a thumb into the centre and work outwards to shape a ring of even thickness.
7. **Fry the vermicelli:** Heat a pot of oil over medium heat until hot (bubbles rise around a bamboo skewer). Add the vermicelli and deep-fry until puffed up. Remove immediately, drain and turn off the heat.
8. **Deep-fry the yam ring:** Reheat the oil over medium heat. Place the shaped ring on a strainer and lower it into the oil. Ladle hot oil over the top to bathe and set the ring, frying until golden brown and crispy. Remove and set aside.

### Part 2: Seafood & chicken stir-fry filling

1. **Aromatics and sauce base:** Heat a little oil in a pan. Add the garlic and carrot. Pour in the water, oyster sauce, sugar and white pepper and stir to combine.
2. **Proteins and vegetables:** Add the chicken, prawns, scallops, young corn and peas.
3. **Simmer:** Cover and cook for about 2 minutes.
4. **Thicken and finish:** Turn the heat up to high. Slowly pour in the cornstarch solution, stirring, until the sauce thickens and coats the ingredients. Stir in the sesame oil and Shaoxing wine, then turn off the heat.
5. **Assemble and serve:** Put the fried vermicelli on a serving dish, top with the fried yam ring, ladle the filling into the centre of the ring and serve.
