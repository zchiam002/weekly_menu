# Chinese Baked Chicken 电饭锅药材鸡

> Whole chicken marinated with ginseng, angelica root and sand ginger, then cooked in a rice cooker (not the oven) on a bed of ginger and spring onion, and served with a sauce thickened from the pot juices.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (1 whole chicken, ~1kg)

## Ingredients

### Herbal soak & marinade

- 5g ginseng roots
- 5g angelica root
- ½ cup hot water
- ½ tbsp sand ginger powder (sha jiang fen 沙姜粉)
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (Chinese cooking wine)
- 1 stalk spring onion, lightly smashed

### Main

- 1 whole chicken (~1kg)

### Rice cooker pot base

- Spring onion stalks (quantity not specified)
- 80g young ginger, thinly sliced

### Sauce / finishing

- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce (optional, for colour and sauce)
- Cornstarch solution (quantity not specified)
- 1 handful spring onion, chopped

## Method

1. **Steep the herbs:** Soak the ginseng and angelica root in the ½ cup of hot water until it cools down.
2. **Marinade:** In a ziplock bag combine the cooled herbal water (with the roots), sand ginger powder, oyster sauce, light soy sauce, salt, Shaoxing wine and the lightly smashed spring onion stalk.
3. **Marinate the chicken:** Cut off the tail end of the chicken. Put the chicken in the bag, rub the marinade all over, including inside the cavity, and squeeze the spring onion into the cavity. Seal and marinate in the fridge for at least 2 hours (the creator did 3 hours).
4. **Before cooking:** Take the chicken out of the fridge 30–45 minutes ahead so it comes to room temperature. Lift it out of the bag (keep the marinade) and stuff the ginseng, angelica root and spring onion inside the cavity. Rub a little dark soy sauce over the skin and tie the drumsticks together with butcher's string.
5. **Set up the rice cooker:** Lay a bed of spring onion stalks and the 80g of sliced young ginger in the rice cooker pot. Set the chicken on top breast-side up, tuck in the wings, and pour the reserved marinade over it.
6. **Cook:** Close the lid and run the normal rice-cooking function for 2 cycles (about 28–30 minutes in total) until cooked through — test with a skewer in the thickest part; the juices should run clear.
7. **Thicken and serve:** Remove the chicken and discard the cooked ginger and spring onion. Bring the remaining pot juices to a boil on the rice-cooking function, stir in the 1 tbsp dark soy sauce (optional), then slowly stir in the cornstarch solution until thickened to your liking. Turn off, stir in the chopped spring onion and pour the sauce over the chicken.
