# Steamed Tofu in Minced Pork Sauce

> Silken tofu steamed under a savoury minced pork sauce of black bean paste, oyster sauce and rice wine, finished with spring onion.

**Servings:** 3–4

## Ingredients

### Pork marinade

- 150g minced pork
- 1 tbsp black bean paste
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp Chinese white rice wine
- 2–3g young ginger, julienned
- 1 tbsp cornflour
- ⅓ cup (85ml) water
- 1 tsp sesame oil

### Other

- 300g silken tofu (can be slightly more or less)
- Chopped spring onion
