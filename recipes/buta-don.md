# Buta Don 豚丼

> Thinly sliced pork belly marinated in sake, soy, mirin and grated garlic, seared in its own fat and served over rice and shredded nori, finished with the marinade reduced to a glossy glaze.

**Servings:** 6.5 (source recipe serves 4 — quantities below are pre-scaled ×1.625)

## Ingredients

### Main

- ~1.3kg thinly sliced pork belly (pick pork with good fat layers)
- ~30g shredded nori seaweed
- 6.5 portions steamed white rice

### Marinade & sauce

- 13 cloves garlic, fine-grated
- 13 tbsp (~195ml) Japanese sake
- 13 tbsp (~195ml) soy sauce
- 13 tbsp (~195ml) mirin
- 6½ tsp (~2 tbsp + ½ tsp) sugar

### Optional toppings

- 6–7 raw egg yolks (1 per bowl)
- Japanese mayonnaise, to taste

## Method

1. **Marinade:** In a large mixing bowl combine the sake, soy sauce, mirin and sugar. Fine-grate the garlic straight into the bowl and whisk lightly until combined.
2. **Season the pork:** Lay the pork belly slices into the bowl piece by piece so each one is evenly coated, then gently massage the pork by hand so the marinade absorbs into the meat.
3. **Marinate:** Cover with plastic wrap, pressing the film directly onto the surface of the meat to eliminate air pockets. Leave at room temperature for 4–5 minutes.
4. **Sear:** Heat a large skillet over medium heat — no oil, the pork belly renders its own fat. Lay the slices flat in a single layer without overlapping, working in batches. Sear the first side for 3 minutes until browned and aromatic, flip, and cook the second side for 1 minute. Turn off the heat and remove the pork.
5. **Build the bowls:** Scoop warm steamed rice into 6–7 bowls and cover the rice generously with shredded nori. Arrange the seared pork over the seaweed.
6. **Reduce the glaze:** Pour the remaining marinade from the bowl into the hot skillet. Turn the heat to low so the sugar and soy don't burn, and simmer gently, stirring, until the sauce thickens to a glossy glaze.
7. **Serve:** Drizzle the warm glaze over the pork in each bowl. Optionally top each with a raw egg yolk in the centre and serve with Japanese mayonnaise on the side.
