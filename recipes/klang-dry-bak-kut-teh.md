# Klang Dry Bak Kut Teh 巴生鸡肉干肉骨茶

> Klang-style dry bak kut teh with chicken drumsticks pan-fried in cuttlefish-infused oil with dried chillies and garlic, deglazed with Shaoxing wine, oyster sauce and dark soy, and finished with okra.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (450g chicken drumsticks)

## Ingredients

### Main & aromatics

- 20g cuttlefish floss, rinsed
- 450g chicken drumsticks, cut into smaller pieces
- 10 dried chillies, rehydrated and deseeded
- 4 cloves garlic, chopped
- 4 lady's fingers (okra), sliced
- Oil (quantity not specified)

### Sauce & seasoning

- 4 tbsp Chinese wine (Shaoxing wine)
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 2 tbsp dark soy sauce
- ~50ml water (optional, if extra sauce is wanted)

## Method

1. **Pan-fry the cuttlefish floss:** Heat oil in a wok over medium or medium-low heat. Add the rinsed cuttlefish floss and pan-fry slowly until fragrant and browned, so its umami flavour goes into the oil. Remove the cuttlefish floss and set aside.
2. **Brown the chicken:** In the same oil over medium heat, pan-fry the chicken, browning it on all sides until no redness remains and the meat is semi-cooked.
3. **Chillies and garlic:** Add the dried chillies and chopped garlic. Stir-fry until fragrant and smoky.
4. **Deglaze and season:** Deglaze the wok with the Chinese wine. Add the oyster sauce and dark soy sauce.
5. **Okra and cuttlefish floss:** Add the sliced lady's fingers and the reserved fried cuttlefish floss back into the wok. Mix well to coat everything.
6. **Finish:** Add about 50ml water if you want extra sauce, stir thoroughly and serve.
