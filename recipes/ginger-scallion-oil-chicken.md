# Ginger Scallion Oil Chicken 葱油手撕鸡 (Chinese Chicken in Scallion Sauce)

> Chicken legs poached gently with ginger and scallion, shocked in ice water, shredded and unmoulded, with a choice of two ginger-scallion oil and light soy sauces.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (3 chicken legs, ~770g)

## Ingredients

### Poaching the chicken

- 3 chicken legs (~770g in total)
- Water, enough to cover the chicken legs
- ~1 tsp salt
- 2 stalks spring onion / scallion stem, lightly crushed
- ~5g young ginger, thinly sliced
- Ice water, for shocking the chicken

### Scallion oil sauce — method 1 (clean oil sauce)

- 4 tbsp cooking oil
- ~2 pieces spring onion / scallion stem, slightly smashed and cut in half
- ~15g ginger, thinly sliced
- ½ tsp sugar
- 5 tbsp light soy sauce

### Scallion oil sauce — method 2 (grated ginger & scallion sauce)

- 4 tbsp cooking oil (smoking hot)
- 5g grated ginger
- ~2 stalks spring onion (the rest after using the stems)
- ½ tsp sugar
- 5 tbsp light soy sauce

### Plating & garnish

- Cucumber, sliced
- Spring onions, for garnish

## Method

### Poaching the chicken & shocking

1. Boil enough water in a wok to cover the 3 chicken legs. Add the salt, the lightly crushed spring onion stems and the thinly sliced young ginger. Cover and bring to a boil.
2. Add the chicken legs, making sure they are completely submerged. Put the lid back on and bring back to a boil.
3. Once boiling, turn the heat to low, cover and cook slowly for 20 minutes.
4. Check doneness by inserting a skewer or chopstick into the thickest part of a leg; if the juices run clear, take the chicken out.
5. Immediately transfer the chicken to ice water and soak for about 20 minutes to stop the cooking and keep it tender.

### Sauce method 1: clean scallion oil sauce

1. In a washed wok, heat the 4 tbsp oil over medium-low heat (or low if it gets too hot).
2. Add the smashed spring onion stems and sliced ginger. Fry slowly until the ginger and spring onion are slightly browned and fragrant.
3. Turn off the flame and remove and discard the spring onion and ginger from the oil.
4. Without turning the flame back on, add the sugar and light soy sauce to the warm oil. Stir well to melt the sugar, then set the sauce aside.

### Sauce method 2: grated ginger & scallion sauce

1. In a bowl put the grated ginger, spring onions and sugar.
2. Heat the 4 tbsp oil until smoking hot, turn off the heat and pour the hot oil directly over the ginger and spring onions.
3. Add the light soy sauce and stir well to combine.

### Deboning, plating & serving

1. Take the chicken out of the ice water after 20 minutes. Debone and shred or slice the meat into smaller pieces.
2. Place the pieces skin-side down, compactly, in a bowl (larger skin-on pieces at the bottom first).
3. Put a serving plate over the bowl, hold it firmly and flip over to unmould the chicken neatly onto the plate.
4. Garnish with sliced cucumber, pour the prepared sauce over the chicken, top with fresh spring onions and serve.
