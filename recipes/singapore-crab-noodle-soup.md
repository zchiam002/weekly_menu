# Singapore Crab Noodle Soup 螃蟹米粉 (Crab with Bee Hoon in Soup)

> Anchovy and salted-fish stock cooked with live crabs and ladled over bee hoon, with evaporated milk and Chinese cooking wine stirred in at the end.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (3 live crabs, 2–2.5L of stock)

## Ingredients

### Stock base

- Oil (quantity not specified)
- 2 pieces dried salted fish, cut into 4 pieces
- 100g dried anchovies / ikan bilis, washed
- 2L water (+ ½L extra added later)
- A few slices of ginger
- 5 chicken stock cubes

### Main & finishing

- 3 live crabs, cleaned and prepared
- Bee hoon / rice vermicelli (quantity not specified)
- ½ cup evaporated milk
- ¼ cup Chinese cooking wine
- Spring onions, for garnish (quantity not specified)
- Coriander leaf, for garnish (quantity not specified)

## Method

1. **Pan-fry the salted fish and anchovies:** Heat oil in a pan. Pan-fry the salted fish pieces for a few minutes until golden brown and fragrant, then set aside. In the same pan fry the washed anchovies until dry and fragrant.
2. **Simmer the stock:** Bring the 2L water to a boil in a pot. Add the ginger slices, fried salted fish, fried anchovies and the chicken stock cubes. Cover, turn the heat to low and simmer for 30 minutes.
3. **Strain and adjust:** Add ½L more water if you want extra soup and bring back to a boil. Strain out and discard the salted fish and anchovies, leaving the ginger slices in the stock.
4. **Cook the crabs:** Make sure the stock is boiling, then add the cleaned live crabs. Cover and cook for 8–10 minutes until done.
5. **Blanch the bee hoon:** In a separate pot of boiling water blanch the bee hoon for about 1 minute, then drain and divide between serving bowls.
6. **Finish the soup:** Add the evaporated milk and Chinese cooking wine to the pot with the cooked crabs and soup and stir to combine.
7. **Assemble and serve:** Put the cooked crabs on the blanched bee hoon in the bowls, ladle over the hot soup, garnish with spring onions and coriander and serve.
