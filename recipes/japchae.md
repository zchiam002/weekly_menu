# Japchae 잡채

> Korean stir-fried sweet potato glass noodles with beef, spinach, carrot, onion and shiitake in a
> sweet soy and sesame sauce.

**Servings:** 3–4

## Ingredients

### Sauce

- 4 tbsp light soy sauce
- ½ tbsp light brown sugar
- 3 cloves garlic, chopped
- ½ tbsp premium dark soy sauce
- 2 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
- 1 tbsp sesame oil

### For stir-frying

- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- ½ red onion, thinly sliced
- ½ carrot, cut into thin strips
- 3 spring onion stalks (white stems go in first, green tops at the end)
- 2 dried shiitake mushrooms, rehydrated and thinly sliced
- A pinch of salt
- 50g beef (shabu shabu or thinly sliced steak)
- 50g spinach
- 100g sweet potato glass noodles (dangmyeon)

## Method

1. **Noodles:** Soak the sweet potato glass noodles in hot water for 30 minutes, then rinse under
   cold running water.
2. **Sauce:** Mix the light soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, dark soy sauce, sesame seeds and sesame
   oil.
3. **Stir-fry:** Heat the sesame oil and stir-fry the onion, carrot, spring onion whites and
   shiitake with a pinch of salt. Add the beef, then the spinach, then the noodles and the sauce,
   tossing to coat. Finish with the spring onion greens.
