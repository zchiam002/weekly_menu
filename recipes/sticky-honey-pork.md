# Sticky Honey Pork

> Twice-fried pork loin tossed in a glossy honey-soy glaze with ginger and garlic. Crisp outside, tender inside, sticky all over.

**Servings:** 6

## Ingredients

### Marinade (at least 2 hours)

- 600g pork loin with bones
- 1.5 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp Chinese white rice wine
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- ½ tsp baking powder
- A generous dash of white pepper
- 3 tbsp cornflour

### Sauce

- ½ thumb-size ginger, julienned
- 4 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1 cup water
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce

## Method

1. **Marinate:** Mix the pork with the light soy sauce, rice wine, oyster sauce, baking powder, white pepper and cornflour. Marinate at least 2 hours.
2. **Deep-fry twice:** First fry over medium heat to cook the pork through. Rest, then fry a second time over high heat to crisp the outside. Drain.
3. **Build the sauce:** In a wok, fry the julienned ginger and chopped garlic until fragrant. Add the water, light soy sauce, honey and dark soy sauce; bring to a simmer.
4. **Finish:** Add the fried pork and toss over the heat until the sauce reduces and glazes the pork.
