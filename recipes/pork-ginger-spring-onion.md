# Pork with Ginger and Spring Onion

> Homely Chinese stir-fry — velveted pork in a savoury dark soy and oyster sauce gravy fragrant with ginger, garlic and spring onion.

**Servings:** 4

## Ingredients

### Pork marinade

- 500g pork
- ½ tsp baking soda
- 2 tsp tapioca flour (or corn flour)
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- Ginger juice from 2-inch grated ginger (spread the grated ginger over the pork after squeezing out the juice)
- A few dashes of white pepper

### Gravy

- 1 thumb-size ginger, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, chopped
- 3 spring onions / scallions
- 2 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 cup water
- ½ tsp sugar
- Tapioca starch solution (or cornstarch solution)
- ½ tbsp sesame seed oil
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing Hua Tiao wine / Chinese cooking wine

## Method

1. **Marinate:** Slice the pork and mix with the baking soda, tapioca flour, light soy sauce, ginger juice (and the grated ginger) and white pepper. Leave to marinate and tenderise, ~20–30 min.
2. **Sear the pork:** Heat oil in a wok over high heat and stir-fry the marinated pork until it changes colour and is lightly browned. Remove and set aside.
3. **Aromatics:** In the same wok, fry the sliced ginger and chopped garlic until fragrant. Add the white parts of the spring onion.
4. **Gravy:** Add the dark soy sauce, oyster sauce, water and sugar. Splash in the Shaoxing wine. Bring to a simmer.
5. **Combine:** Return the pork to the wok and toss to coat. Thicken with the starch solution to a glossy gravy.
6. **Finish:** Stir in the sesame oil and the green parts of the spring onion. Serve with rice.
