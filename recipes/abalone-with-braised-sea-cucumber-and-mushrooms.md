# Abalone with Braised Sea Cucumber & Mushrooms

> Pressure-cooker stock of chicken, Jinhua ham, dried scallop and mushroom used to braise sea cucumber, served with canned baby abalone warmed in a darkened, thickened gravy.

**Servings:** Not specified in source

## Ingredients

### Stock & main

- 2 cans baby abalone (keep the abalone liquid from the cans)
- Sea cucumber (quantity not specified)
- Mushrooms (quantity not specified), soaked in water and hydrated
- 3 small pieces Chinese ham / Jinhua ham, fat and skin removed
- Small dried scallops (quantity not specified)
- ~1kg chicken legs
- Water, to fill the pot (quantity not specified)

### Sauce & seasoning

- 3 tbsp oyster sauce
- Chinese cooking wine / Hua Tiao wine — 1 capful for the stock, plus 2 small capfuls to finish
- Pepper (quantity not specified)
- Dark soy sauce (quantity not specified)
- Cornstarch solution (quantity not specified)

## Method

1. **Stock base:** Fill a pressure cooker pot with water. Add the chicken legs, Chinese ham, dried scallops, hydrated mushrooms, oyster sauce and 1 capful of Hua Tiao wine.
2. **Boil and pressurise:** Cover with the pressure cooker lid and bring to a boil over heat until steam releases and the pressure indicator rises. Turn off the heat and let the indicator drop. Repeat the boil-and-depressurise cycle 2 more times (3 cycles in total).
3. **Strain the chicken:** Open the lid and lift the chicken legs out of the broth with a strainer.
4. **Braise the sea cucumber:** Add the whole sea cucumbers to the broth, cover and do one last boil-and-pressurise cycle over heat.
5. **Warm the abalone and darken the sauce:** Scoop a portion of the brewed stock into a separate pan. Add the 2 cans of baby abalone together with their canned liquid. Add dark soy sauce for the colour you want. Bring to a boil and heat for about 5 minutes to warm through.
6. **Thicken the gravy and finish:** Add the cornstarch solution to thicken the sauce. Stir in the 2 small capfuls of Hua Tiao wine.
7. **Plate and serve:** Arrange the sea cucumbers, mushrooms and abalone on a dish, pour the gravy over the top and serve.
