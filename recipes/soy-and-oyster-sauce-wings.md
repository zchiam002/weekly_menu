# Soy & Oyster Sauce Wings 酱烧蚝油鸡翅

> Chicken wings marinated in light soy, oyster sauce, garlic and spring onion, then cooked one of three ways — oven-baked, air-fried or pan-fried.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (2kg chicken wings, ~18 wings)

## Ingredients

### Main & shared marinade

- 2kg chicken wings (~18 wings), cut into drumlets, mid-wings and wing tips
- 3 tbsp light soy sauce
- 3 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 4 cloves garlic, slightly smashed
- 2–3 pieces spring onion, lightly smashed
- A few dashes crushed white pepper

### Cooking essentials

- A little oil, for pan-frying
- Baking paper, for oven baking

## Method

### Shared marinade & pre-cooking setup

1. Cut the wings into three parts: mini drumstick / drumlet, centre piece / mid-wing and wing tip.
2. In a bowl with the wings add the light soy sauce, oyster sauce, sugar, salt, smashed garlic, smashed spring onion and crushed white pepper. Massage the marinade thoroughly into the wings.
3. Cover and marinate in the fridge for at least 2 hours.
4. Rest the marinated wings at room temperature before cooking.

### Method 1: Oven baked

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
2. Line a baking tray with baking paper and arrange the wings (drumlets and mid-wings), leaving space between them.
3. Bake at 200°C, top and bottom fan, for 20 minutes.
4. Increase the oven to 220°C and bake for another 10 minutes to brown the skin further.

### Method 2: Air fried

1. Put the wings (drumlets and mid-wings) in the air fryer basket, spaced out.
2. Air-fry at 180°C for 15 minutes.
3. Increase to 200°C and air-fry for another 5 minutes to brown the skin further.

### Method 3: Pan fried

1. Heat a pan over medium heat and add a little oil.
2. Add the wings and turn the heat down to medium-low. Pan-fry one side for 1–2 minutes until lightly charred.
3. Flip the wings, put the lid on and cook for 3 minutes to steam the chicken through.
4. Remove the lid, flip again, put the lid back on and cook for another 3 minutes.
5. Remove the lid and brown all sides in the pan until fully cooked through.
