# Braised Winter Melon with Bamboo Shoot Soup 冬瓜笋干火腿汤

> A clear, savoury Jiangnan-style soup — Chinese ham and dried bamboo shoots braised until the broth is deeply flavoured, then winter melon simmered in until translucent and soft.

**Servings:** Not specified

## Ingredients

- 80–100g Chinese ham (Jinhua ham 金华火腿)
- 250g dried bamboo shoots (笋干), soaked 2 hrs minimum, preferably overnight
- 1 scallion
- 3–4 slices ginger
- 300g winter melon (seeded weight)

## Method

1. **Bamboo shoots:** Rinse and soak the dried bamboo shoots in advance — 2 hours minimum, preferably overnight. Tear them into slimmer pieces and cut into 2-inch sections.
2. **Ham:** Cut the Chinese ham into ¾-inch thick pieces.
3. **Ginger:** Prepare 3–4 thin slices.
4. **Scallion:** Tie into a knot.
5. **Winter melon:** Remove the hard skin and the seeds, then cut into 1.5-inch chunks.
6. **Build the broth:** In a casserole, heat 1 tbsp cooking oil. Sauté the ginger slices and ham until fragrant. Add the scallion knot and bamboo shoots, then pour in enough water to cover everything. Bring to a boil, lower to medium-high, cover and cook 15 minutes.
7. **Winter melon:** Add the winter melon chunks and simmer over low heat a further 15 minutes. Taste and adjust with salt or more water before serving.
