# Hayashi Rice ハヤシライス (Japanese Beef Stew)

> Japanese beef and onion stew built on browned mushrooms, red wine, tomato paste and a brown roux, simmered about 2 hours and served over short grain rice.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (550g stew meat, 700g onions)

## Ingredients

### Mushrooms

- 300g shimeji mushrooms
- 200g cremini mushrooms
- 200g enoki mushrooms
- ½ tsp salt
- 3 tbsp cultured unsalted butter

### Beef & main aromatics

- 550g stew meat (beef shanks, short ribs or chuck), cut into 1½-inch cubes
- 1 tsp salt
- Black pepper (quantity not specified)
- ¼ cup water
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 25g garlic, minced
- 700g onions, halved and cut into ½-inch slices

### Stew base & liquid

- 2 cups beef stock
- 1 cup red wine
- 3 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 tbsp ketchup
- 2 tbsp chuno sauce
- 1 tbsp mild honey
- 2 tsp porcini powder

### Brown roux

- 1 tbsp flour
- 1 tbsp butter

### Serving & garnish

- Japanese short grain rice (quantity not specified)
- Chopped parsley (quantity not specified)

## Method

1. **Prep:** Wipe the cremini caps and stems clean with a damp paper towel and halve them. Trim the growing medium off the shimeji and separate into clusters. Trim the enoki and shred into small clusters by hand. Halve the onions and cut into ½-inch slices. Mince the garlic finely. Cut the stew meat into 1½-inch cubes, lay them in a single layer on a tray and season two sides with the 1 tsp salt and black pepper.
2. **Steam the mushrooms:** In a large heavy-bottomed pot melt the 3 tbsp butter over medium-low heat. Add all the mushrooms and the ½ tsp salt. Cover and steam for 10 minutes.
3. **Brown the mushrooms:** Remove the lid, turn the heat up to boil off the mushroom juices quickly, and sauté from time to time for 10–15 minutes until browned. Transfer to a bowl and set aside.
4. **Brown the beef:** In the same pot add the olive oil and the seasoned beef in a single layer with space between the pieces. Fry undisturbed for 3–4 minutes until a brown crust forms, flip and fry the other side for 3–4 minutes. Transfer the beef out of the pot.
5. **Steam and sauté the onions and garlic:** Add the onions, garlic and ¼ cup water to the same pot. Cover and steam for 10 minutes until wilted. Uncover and sauté for another 10 minutes until starting to brown.
6. **Simmer the stew:** Add the red wine, beef stock, tomato paste, ketchup, chuno sauce, honey and porcini powder. Return the browned beef to the pot, stir and bring to a full boil. Skim off the floating foam and coagulated protein scum. Reduce the heat to low, cover and stew for about 2 hours, checking and stirring every 30 minutes (skim off excess rendered fat if needed).
7. **Add the mushrooms back:** Stir the sautéed mushrooms back into the stew.
8. **Brown roux:** In a separate frying pan over medium heat melt the 1 tbsp butter with the 1 tbsp flour and fry for about 3 minutes until golden brown. Scoop a few ladles of hot stew liquid into the roux pan and whisk until smooth, then pour it back into the stew pot and stir quickly until thickened.
9. **Serve:** Serve the hot stew alongside Japanese short grain rice and sprinkle with chopped parsley.
