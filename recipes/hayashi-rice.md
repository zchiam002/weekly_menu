# Hayashi Rice

> Japanese beef and mushroom stew in a red wine and tomato sauce, thickened with a brown roux and served over short-grain rice — ハヤシライス

**Servings:** 6

## Ingredients

### Beef
- 550g beef chuck (or shank), cut into 1½-inch cubes
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp ground black pepper
- 1 tbsp olive oil

### Mushrooms
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter
- 200g crimini mushrooms, trimmed and halved
- 300g shimeji mushrooms, trimmed and separated
- 200g enoki mushrooms, trimmed and separated
- ½ tsp salt

### Aromatics
- 700g onions (about 3 large), halved and sliced ½ inch thick
- 25g garlic (about 4 large cloves), minced
- ¼ cup water

### Stew
- 2 cups beef stock
- 1 cup red wine
- 3 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 tbsp ketchup
- 2 tbsp chunou sauce
- 1 tbsp mild honey
- 2 tsp porcini powder

### Roux
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter
- 1 tbsp all-purpose flour

### To serve
- Japanese short-grain rice

## Preparation

1. Season the beef with the salt and pepper.
2. Melt the butter in a large heavy pot over medium-low heat. Add the mushrooms and ½ tsp salt, cover and steam for 10 minutes.
3. Uncover, raise the heat and sauté 10–15 minutes until golden brown. Transfer to a bowl.
4. Add the olive oil and brown the beef in a single layer, about 3 minutes per side. Remove.
5. Add the onions, garlic and ¼ cup water. Cover, reduce to low and steam for 10 minutes.
6. Uncover, raise the heat to boil off excess liquid and sauté about 10 minutes until the onion edges brown.
7. Add the red wine, beef stock, tomato paste, ketchup, chunou sauce, honey and porcini powder. Return the beef, bring to a boil and skim off the foam.
8. Reduce to the lowest heat, cover and simmer about 2 hours until the beef flakes easily, stirring every 20–30 minutes. Add a little water if it gets too low and skim excess fat.
9. Return the mushrooms to the pot.
10. Make the roux: cook the butter and flour in a small pan over medium heat until golden brown. Off the heat, whisk in a few ladles of the stew liquid until smooth, then stir it into the pot until thickened.
11. Serve over rice.
