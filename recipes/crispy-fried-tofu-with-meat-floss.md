# Crispy Fried Tofu with Meat Floss 肉松脆豆腐

> Cornstarch-coated tofu cubes fried until golden and crisp, drizzled with a savoury soy sauce and piled with pork floss and scallions. About 20 minutes start to finish.

**Servings:** Not specified — 1 block of tofu, roughly 3–4 as a side dish

## Ingredients

### Main

- 1 block tofu (firm or semi-firm)
- 3–4 tbsp cornstarch, for coating
- Pork floss or chicken floss, to taste
- 1 stalk scallion / green onion, sliced
- Cooking oil, enough for deep or shallow frying

### Sauce

- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- ¼ tsp sugar
- ¼ tsp mushroom granules / seasoning
- 2 tbsp hot water

## Method

1. **Prep and dry the tofu:** Pat the block thoroughly dry with kitchen paper to remove surface moisture. Cut into bite-sized cubes.
2. **Coat:** Spread the cornstarch on a plate. Lightly coat every side of each cube, shaking off the excess.
3. **Fry:** Heat the oil in a pan or pot over medium heat. Add the tofu and fry until all sides are lightly golden and crisp. Remove and drain on paper towels.
4. **Sauce:** In a small bowl, combine the soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, sugar, mushroom granules and hot water. Stir until the granules and sugar dissolve completely.
5. **Assemble:** Plate the crispy tofu. Drizzle 3–4 tsp of the sauce over the top, keeping the rest as a dipping sauce on the side. Top generously with pork floss and garnish with chopped scallion.
