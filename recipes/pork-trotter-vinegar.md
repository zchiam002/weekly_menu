# Pork Trotter Vinegar 猪脚醋

> Front pork trotter blanched, then simmered 1½ hours in a full bottle of black vinegar with fried old ginger and hard-boiled eggs, sweetened at the end with gula melaka.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (1.9kg front trotter, 6 eggs)

## Ingredients

### Main

- ~1.9kg front pork trotter
- Cold water, for blanching (quantity not specified)
- 6 hard-boiled eggs

### Aromatics & liquid base

- 2 tbsp sesame oil
- 200g old ginger, lightly smashed
- 1 full bottle (750ml) black vinegar — the brand is named in the video but transcribed inconsistently ("Cheong Chan" in one transcript, "Cheung Kong Thai Seung Mew" in another), so check the bottle shown in the video
- 250ml water

### Seasoning

- 150g palm sugar (gula melaka)
- 1 tsp salt

## Method

1. **Blanch the trotter:** Put the trotter in a pot of cold water, bring to a boil over medium to medium-high heat and boil for 5–10 minutes to draw out the impurities. Lift out and wash thoroughly.
2. **Fry the ginger:** Heat a pot, add the sesame oil and immediately add the smashed ginger (don't wait for the oil to heat up). Fry for a few minutes until fragrant and starting to brown.
3. **Add the liquids, trotter and eggs:** Pour in the black vinegar and water, then add the blanched trotter and the hard-boiled eggs.
4. **Bring to a boil:** Cover and bring to a boil.
5. **Simmer:** Once boiling, put the lid back on, turn the heat down to low and simmer for 1½ hours.
6. **Season and finish:** Add the palm sugar and salt and stir well. Cover and boil for another 5 minutes to melt the palm sugar completely, then serve.
