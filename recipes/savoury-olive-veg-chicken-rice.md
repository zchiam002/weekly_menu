# Savoury Olive Veg Chicken Rice 电饭锅榄菜鸡饭

> One-pot rice-cooker chicken rice with preserved olive vegetables, carrot, oyster sauce and fish sauce, finished with spring onions.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (3 cups jasmine rice, ~390g)

## Ingredients

### Chicken & seasoning

- 200g boneless chicken leg, skin removed, cut into small pieces
- ½ tsp salt
- A few dashes white pepper

### Rice cooker main & sauce

- 3 cups (~390g) jasmine rice, washed and drained dry
- 80g (~3 big tbsp) preserved olive vegetables 榄菜
- ~200g carrots, diced small
- 1½ tbsp oyster sauce
- 1½ tbsp fish sauce — the creator notes soy sauce can be used instead
- A few dashes white pepper
- 390ml water

### Finishing / garnish

- Spring onions (quantity not specified)

## Method

1. **Season the chicken:** Put the chicken in a bowl with the salt and white pepper. Mix well and set aside.
2. **Assemble in the rice cooker:** In the rice cooker pot add the washed, drained rice, the seasoned chicken, preserved olive vegetables, diced carrots, oyster sauce, fish sauce and white pepper.
3. **Mix and add water:** Mix everything together thoroughly, breaking up the olive vegetables. Pour in the water and push down any rice stuck to the sides of the pot to even it out.
4. **Cook:** Close the lid, switch on rice-cooking mode and cook until done.
5. **Garnish and serve:** Open the lid, sprinkle over the spring onions, stir thoroughly to distribute the ingredients and serve.
