# Chinese Roast Chicken 五香烤鸡

> Five-spice spring chicken marinated in rice wine, oyster sauce and soy, air-fried, and served with a dipping sauce made by boiling down the marinade.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (1 spring chicken, ~700g)

## Ingredients

### Marinade

- 2 tbsp Chinese rice wine
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 4 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp five-spice powder
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 4 cloves garlic, smashed
- 2 stalks spring onion, lightly smashed and tied into a knot
- A few dashes white pepper — the creator notes black pepper can be used instead

### Main

- 1 whole spring chicken (~700g)

### Dipping sauce

- Reserved chicken marinade, strained
- ⅓ cup water
- ½ tsp salt
- Cornstarch solution (quantity not specified)

## Method

1. **Marinade:** In a bowl combine the rice wine, oyster sauce, light soy sauce, five-spice powder, brown sugar, smashed garlic, spring onion knot and white pepper. Mix well.
2. **Marinate the chicken:** Use the spring onion knot, dipped in the marinade, to rub the marinade all over the chicken, inside and out. Stuff the spring onion knot and smashed garlic into the cavity. Put the chicken in a ziplock bag, pour in the remaining marinade, seal and marinate in the fridge for at least 2–3 hours (or 3–4 hours), turning every hour.
3. **Rest:** Take the chicken out of the fridge and let it come to room temperature before cooking.
4. **First air-fry (breast-side up):** Place the chicken breast-side up in the air fryer basket and air-fry at 180°C for 10 minutes.
5. **Second air-fry (turned over):** Turn the chicken over and air-fry at 180°C for another 10 minutes until browned and cooked through. Remove and let it rest.
6. **Dipping sauce:** Heat a small saucepan and strain the reserved marinade into it. Bring to a boil, then add the water and salt. Slowly pour in the cornstarch solution, stirring, until thickened to your liking. Serve alongside the chicken.
