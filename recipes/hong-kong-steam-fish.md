# Hong Kong Style Steam Fish (港式蒸鱼)

> Whole fish steamed on a bed of ginger and spring onion, topped with fresh julienned aromatics and chilli, then finished with a light soy sauce and a sizzle of smoking hot oil.

**Servings:** Not specified in source

## Ingredients

### Fish & aromatics for steaming

- 1 whole fish (e.g. sea bass, snapper or grouper), approx. 600–800g, cleaned and gutted
- 3–4 slices ginger
- 2–3 stalks spring onions / scallions, cut into long sections
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (optional, for rubbing on the fish)

### Garnish

- 2–3 stalks spring onions / scallions, finely julienned into thin curls
- 1 piece ginger, finely julienned
- 1 red chilli, seeded and finely julienned, for colour

### Seasoning & oil

- 3 tbsp cooking oil (neutral oil like canola or peanut)
- ½ tsp sesame oil

### Hong Kong style soy sauce mixture

- 3 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp water (or chicken stock)
- 1 tsp rock sugar or white sugar, to taste
- ½ tsp seasoned soy sauce for seafood (optional, or extra light soy sauce)

## Method

1. **Prep the fish and plate:** Clean the fish thoroughly and pat dry. Make 2–3 shallow diagonal slashes on both sides for even cooking. Rub a little Shaoxing wine over the fish. On a heat-proof steaming plate, lay a bed of the spring onion sections and ginger slices, and place the fish on top (raising the fish slightly off the plate helps steam circulate evenly underneath).
2. **Steam:** Bring water in a steamer or wok to a rolling boil over high heat. Place the plate in, cover tightly and steam on high 8–10 minutes, depending on the size and thickness of the fish. It is done when a chopstick easily pierces the thickest part behind the head.
3. **Discard the steaming liquid:** Carefully remove the plate. Discard the cooked spring onions, ginger and all the fish water on the plate (it carries a fishy odour and dilutes the sauce).
4. **Garnish and soy sauce:** Place the fresh julienned spring onions, ginger and red chilli on top of the fish. In a small saucepan over medium heat, combine the light soy sauce, water and sugar, stirring until the sugar dissolves and it comes to a simmer. Pour around the base of the fish (not directly over the garnish).
5. **Hot oil sizzle:** Heat the cooking oil and sesame oil in a small pan until smoking hot. Carefully pour over the julienned spring onions, ginger and chilli on top of the fish to scald them and release their aroma.
6. Serve immediately with warm rice.
