# Korean Spicy Seafood Stir Fry 韩式香炒海鲜

> Quick stir-fry of prawns and squid with red onion in a gochujang and soy sauce, finished with sesame oil and sesame seeds.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (~150g prawns, 100g squid)

## Ingredients

### Main & aromatics

- Oil (quantity not specified)
- 10g young ginger, minced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 3 stalks spring onion, white stems and green parts separated
- ~150g prawns, deshelled and deveined
- 100g squid, sliced and cleaned

### Sauce & seasoning

- ¼ cup water
- 1 tbsp gochujang sauce
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- ½ tsp chilli powder (optional)
- ½ tbsp sesame oil
- A pinch of sugar
- White sesame seeds (quantity not specified)

## Method

1. **Sauté the aromatics:** Heat oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the ginger and garlic and sauté until fragrant.
2. **Onion and spring onion stems:** Add the sliced red onion and stir-fry for 1–2 minutes until slightly softened. Add the white stems of the spring onions and stir-fry to release the fragrance.
3. **Liquid and sauces:** Add the water, gochujang and light soy sauce. Stir well to mix into the pan.
4. **Prawns:** Add the prawns and stir-fry until about 50% cooked.
5. **Squid:** Add the squid and stir-fry until the seafood turns white and the squid curls up.
6. **Greens and seasoning:** Add the green parts of the spring onions, the chilli powder (optional), sesame oil and a pinch of sugar. Stir well to combine.
7. **Garnish and finish:** Sprinkle over the white sesame seeds, turn off the heat and serve.
