# Century Egg Pork Congee

> Classic Cantonese comfort congee — jasmine and glutinous rice simmered smooth with marinated lean pork and diced century eggs.

**Servings:** 6

## Ingredients

### Congee

- ¾ cup jasmine rice
- ¼ cup glutinous rice
- 10 cups water

### Other

- 4 whole century eggs, diced
- 400g lean pork (can replace with chicken)

### Pork marinade

- 2 tbsp glutinous rice flour
- 2 capfuls rice wine (to remove the porky smell)
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 2 tsp light soy sauce

## Method

1. **Marinate the pork:** Slice the lean pork and mix with the glutinous rice flour, rice wine, sesame oil and light soy sauce. Set aside while the congee cooks.
2. **Cook the congee:** Rinse the jasmine and glutinous rice. Add to a pot with the 10 cups water. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a low simmer and cook, stirring occasionally so it doesn't stick, until the rice breaks down into a smooth, thick congee (~45 min–1 hr).
3. **Add century eggs:** Stir in the diced century eggs and let them warm through and partly melt into the congee.
4. **Add the pork:** Add the marinated pork and simmer until cooked through, stirring so it separates.
5. **Serve:** Season to taste and serve hot. Garnish as you like (spring onion, white pepper, fried shallots, ginger).
