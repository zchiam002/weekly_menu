# Super Easy Braised Pork Belly + Hainanese Curry Sauce

> Small-batch pork belly braised in rice wine, light soy and five-spice, served with a Hainanese-style curry sauce thickened with cornstarch.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (250g pork belly)

## Ingredients

### Part 1: Braised pork belly

- A little oil
- 1 star anise
- 1 cinnamon stick, broken in two
- 1 whole bulb garlic, washed, skin on
- 250g pork belly, washed
- ½ tbsp dark soy sauce (low sodium)
- 1 cup Chinese rice wine — the creator notes water can be used instead
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- ½ tsp five-spice powder
- 2 tsp sugar
- 1 cup water
- Cornstarch solution (quantity not specified)

### Part 2: Hainanese curry sauce

- A little oil
- 1 cinnamon stick, broken in two
- 1 star anise
- 1½ tbsp curry powder, mixed with water to form a paste
- ½ tbsp chilli powder, mixed with water to form a paste (optional)
- 500ml water
- 1 tbsp concentrated chicken stock — the creator notes chicken powder can be used instead
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp sugar
- Cornstarch solution (quantity not specified)

## Method

### Part 1: Braised pork belly

1. **Spices and garlic:** Heat a little oil in a small pot over medium heat. Add the star anise, cinnamon stick and garlic bulb (skin on) and fry for a minute or two until fragrant.
2. **Sear the pork belly:** Add the pork belly and fry over medium heat to render out some of the fat.
3. **Liquids and seasonings:** Add the dark soy sauce, rice wine, light soy sauce, five-spice powder, sugar and water. Mix well and bring to a boil.
4. **Simmer:** Cover, turn the heat down to low and simmer for 45 minutes to 1 hour.
5. **Thicken the gravy:** Uncover and slowly add the cornstarch solution, stirring, until the sauce thickens to your liking.

### Part 2: Hainanese curry sauce

1. **Fry the spices:** Heat a little oil in a pan. Add the cinnamon stick and star anise and fry until fragrant.
2. **Curry paste and liquids:** Add the curry powder paste and the chilli powder paste (optional). Add the water and concentrated chicken stock. Stir well and bring to a boil.
3. **Season:** Stir in the salt and sugar.
4. **Thicken and serve:** Slowly stir in the cornstarch solution until the sauce reaches the consistency you want, then turn off the heat.
