# Korean Spicy Beef Soup (Yukgaejang) 韩式辣牛肉汤

> Beef shank simmered with onion and dried shiitake, torn into strips and returned to the broth with a chilli-powder, garlic and sesame oil-seasoned mix of soybean sprouts and scallions, finished with glass noodles.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (500g beef shank, 2L water)

## Ingredients

### Beef & stock base

- 500g beef shin shank (brisket or flank steak can also be used)
- 2L water, for the stock
- 1 onion, cut into halves
- 8 dried shiitake mushrooms, washed
- 1 beef stock cube / bouillon

### Spice mix & vegetables

- 3 tbsp Korean chilli powder
- 1 tbsp black pepper
- 2 tsp salt (+ 1½ tsp added later when seasoning)
- 4–5 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp soy sauce (or 2 tbsp if the fish sauce is left out)
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 2 tbsp sesame oil
- 200g soybean sprouts
- 2 scallions, stems cut in half

### Noodles & finishing

- 30g Korean glass noodles (dry weight), pre-soaked in water for about 40 minutes

## Method

1. **Prep and soak the beef:** Cut the beef shank along the grain into halves. Soak in a basin of tap water for about 20 minutes to remove excess blood.
2. **Stock base:** Bring the 2L water to a boil in a pot over medium-high heat. Add the rinsed beef, onion halves, washed dried shiitake and the beef stock cube.
3. **Skim and first simmer:** As it comes to a boil, skim off as much scum as you can. Cover, reduce the heat to low and simmer for 1 hour (or 1 hour 10–20 minutes for extra tenderness).
4. **Spiced vegetables:** In a bowl combine the chilli powder, black pepper, 2 tsp salt, garlic, soy sauce, fish sauce and sesame oil. Add the soybean sprouts and scallions and mix gently by hand.
5. **Add the vegetables to the stock:** Remove the cooked beef, onion halves and shiitake and set aside to cool. Add the spiced vegetables and spice mixture to the pot (rinse the bowl with a little soup).
6. **Second simmer:** Bring back to a boil over medium-high heat, cover, reduce to low and simmer for 15 minutes.
7. **Slice and shred:** Slice the cooked shiitake thinly (stems included), tear the cooled beef along the grain into thin strips by hand and roughly chop the cooked onion.
8. **Third simmer:** Return the sliced mushrooms, shredded beef and chopped onion to the pot. Cover and simmer over low heat for another 15 minutes.
9. **Season and add the noodles:** Uncover, taste, and stir in 1½ tsp salt. Bring back to a boil, then add the pre-soaked glass noodles.
10. **Final boil and serve:** Cook the glass noodles in the boiling soup for 3–5 minutes until soft, then turn off the heat and serve.
