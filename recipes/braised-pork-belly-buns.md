# Braised Pork Belly Buns (Kong Bak Pau)

> Melt-in-the-mouth braised pork belly — slow-cooked in dark soy, rice wine and warm spices, then tucked into soft steamed buns. Big-batch recipe.

**Servings:** 25

## Ingredients

### Marinade

- 3 kg pork belly
- 1 cup dark soy sauce (less-sodium type)

### Braise / stir-fry

- 2 pieces star anise
- 5 stalks cinnamon bark
- A handful white peppercorns
- 15 cloves
- 3 bulbs garlic
- 320ml Chinese rice wine
- 240ml dark soy sauce (less-sodium type)
- 50g rock sugar
- Water, to cover the meat
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce (or to taste)
- Cornstarch solution, to thicken the gravy

## Method

1. **Marinate:** Cut the pork belly into large pieces and coat with the 1 cup dark soy sauce. Let it marinate to colour and flavour the meat.
2. **Aromatics:** Heat a large pot. Add the star anise, cinnamon bark, white peppercorns, cloves and garlic; stir-fry until fragrant.
3. **Sear & deglaze:** Add the marinated pork and stir-fry to colour. Pour in the Chinese rice wine and the 240ml dark soy sauce, then add the rock sugar.
4. **Braise:** Add enough water to cover the meat. Bring to a boil, then reduce to low and simmer until the pork belly is tender and the fat is soft, ~1.5–2 hr.
5. **Season & thicken:** Add the light soy sauce to taste. Stir in the cornstarch solution to thicken the gravy.
6. **Serve:** Serve the braised pork belly in soft steamed buns (kong bak pau), spooning over some gravy.
