# Steamed Ribs with Silken Tofu (蒸排骨豆腐)

> Marinated pork spare rib pieces with garlic and fermented black beans, steamed on a bed of silken tofu and finished with chilli and spring onion.

**Servings:** Not specified in source

## Ingredients

### Main ingredients

- 400g pork spare ribs, cut into small bite-sized pieces
- 1 box silken tofu (approx. 300g), sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fermented black beans (salted black beans), rinsed and lightly mashed
- 1 red chilli or chilli padi, sliced, for heat and colour
- 1–2 stalks spring onion, chopped, for garnish

### Pork rib marinade

- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine
- ½ tsp sugar
- ¼ tsp white pepper
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 tbsp cooking oil

## Method

1. **Marinate the ribs:** Rinse the rib pieces in cool water and pat thoroughly dry. In a bowl, combine the ribs with the light soy sauce, oyster sauce, Shaoxing wine, sugar, white pepper, sesame oil and cornstarch; mix well. Add the garlic, black beans and cooking oil, toss to coat evenly and marinate at least 20–30 minutes.
2. **Tofu layer:** Carefully remove the silken tofu from its packaging and cut into slabs or bite-sized blocks. Arrange in an even layer across the bottom of a shallow, heat-proof steaming plate.
3. **Assemble:** Lay the marinated ribs in a single layer on top of the tofu, so they cook evenly. Top with the sliced chilli.
4. **Steam:** Bring the steamer water to a rolling boil over high heat. Place the plate in, cover tightly and steam on high 15–18 minutes, until the ribs are tender and fully cooked through.
5. **Garnish and serve:** Carefully remove the plate, sprinkle with the spring onions and serve hot with steamed white rice.
