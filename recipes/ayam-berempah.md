# Ayam Berempah 马来香料炸鸡 (Malaysian Style Spiced Fried Chicken)

> Chicken legs marinated in a lemongrass, shallot and garlic paste with curry, coriander and cumin powders and cornflour, deep-fried with curry leaves and finished with crispy spice bits.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (3 whole chicken legs)

## Ingredients

### Blended paste (rempah)

- 6 lemongrass, white stem / tender part only
- ~10–11 shallots
- 4–5 cloves garlic
- 20g young ginger
- 100ml water

### Chicken & marinade

- 3 whole chicken legs, cut in half into thighs and drumsticks, with small cuts made in the meat
- The blended paste (all of it)
- 1 tbsp coriander powder
- 1½ tbsp curry powder (meat curry powder)
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- ½ tsp chilli powder
- ½ tsp turmeric powder
- 1 egg
- Leaves of 2 sprigs of curry leaves
- 2 tsp salt
- ~6–7 tbsp cornflour

### Frying & garnishing

- Oil, for deep frying (quantity not specified; enough to cover at least half the chicken)
- 3 sprigs curry leaves, for garnishing and flavouring the oil
- A little water, added to the remaining marinade for the crispy bits

## Method

1. **Blend the paste:** Blend the lemongrass, shallots, garlic, young ginger and water into a smooth paste in a blender or chopper.
2. **Marinate the chicken:** Cut the chicken legs into thighs and drumsticks and make small cuts in the meat. Add the blended paste, coriander powder, curry powder, cumin powder, chilli powder, turmeric powder, egg, curry leaves and salt. Mix thoroughly, then add the cornflour and mix again.
3. **Chill and rest:** Marinate in the fridge for at least 2 hours (preferably overnight; the creator did 4 hours, including 1 hour at room temperature before frying).
4. **Fry the curry leaves:** Heat oil in a pan over medium heat. Fry the 3 sprigs of curry leaves briefly to flavour the oil and make a garnish, then remove and drain.
5. **Deep-fry the chicken:** Mix the chicken slightly and push off the excess spices before frying. Place the chicken skin-side down, 2–3 pieces at a time, into the oil over medium heat. Fry for 12–15 minutes in total, turning every 1–2 minutes, until cooked through and dark golden brown. Remove and drain.
6. **Crispy spice bits:** Add a little water to the remaining marinade to loosen it. Pour it slowly into the oil over medium heat, stirring constantly, and fry until dark brown, crispy and the bubbling subsides. Remove from the oil before turning off the heat, drain, and serve with the fried chicken and curry leaves.
