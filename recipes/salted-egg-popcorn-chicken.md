# Salted Egg Popcorn Chicken 咸蛋黄金鸡丁

> Marinated chicken leg pieces coated in potato starch and double-fried until crisp, then tossed in a foaming butter, curry leaf and mashed salted egg yolk sauce.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (330g boneless chicken leg)

## Ingredients

### Main & marinade

- 330g boneless chicken leg, cut into small pieces
- ~½ tsp salt
- A few dashes white pepper
- 1 egg
- 1 tbsp corn flour

### Coating

- Potato starch (quantity not specified) — the creator notes corn starch, tapioca starch or sweet potato starch can be used instead

### Frying & sauce base

- Oil, for shallow frying (quantity not specified)
- 10g butter
- 2 sprigs curry leaves
- 2 salted egg yolks, cooked, mashed and finely chopped
- ~½ tsp salt, or a little more
- ½ tsp sugar

## Method

1. **Marinate the chicken:** Put the chicken in a bowl with the salt, white pepper and egg. Mix well with clean hands, add the corn flour and mix thoroughly again. Marinate for about half an hour (up to 1 hour).
2. **Coat the chicken:** Coat the marinated pieces evenly with potato starch, pressing down slightly to form a crust. Set aside.
3. **First shallow-fry:** Heat a wok of oil over medium heat until hot (test with a bamboo chopstick until bubbles rise rapidly). Add the chicken in batches without overcrowding, and don't move it at first so the crust can set. Fry for about 4–5 minutes, turning regularly, until light golden brown. Remove and drain.
4. **Second fry (double fry):** Turn the heat up to medium-high until the oil is very hot. Return all the chicken to the wok and fry for about 20–30 seconds until dark golden brown and extra crispy. Remove and set aside.
5. **Salted egg yolk sauce:** Leave about ½ tbsp of oil in the wok over the lowest (or medium-low) heat. Add the butter, curry leaves and the mashed, finely chopped salted egg yolks. Increase the heat to medium-low and fry until the yolk foams and forms lots of bubbles.
6. **Season and toss:** Season the sauce with a little more than ½ tsp salt and the ½ tsp sugar and mix well. Add the fried chicken to the wok over medium or medium-low heat and toss until every piece is coated in the salted egg sauce, then serve.
