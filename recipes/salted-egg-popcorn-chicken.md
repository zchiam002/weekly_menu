# Salted Egg Popcorn Chicken (咸蛋黄金鸡丁)

> 9-ingredient salted egg popcorn chicken — marinated chicken thigh bites fried crisp, then tossed in a foamy salted egg yolk butter sauce with curry leaves and chilli padi.

**Servings:** Not specified in source

## Ingredients

### Chicken marinade & coating

- 500g boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized popcorn chicken pieces
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (or cooking wine)
- ½ tsp white pepper
- 1 egg, beaten
- 4 tbsp cornstarch (or tapioca starch), for coating
- Cooking oil, for deep-frying

### Salted egg sauce

- 4–5 salted egg yolks (steamed 8–10 min and mashed)
- 30g unsalted butter
- 2 sprigs curry leaves
- 2–3 chilli padi / bird's eye chillies, sliced (optional, for heat)
- 1 tbsp evaporated milk (or full cream milk)
- ½ tsp sugar (to taste)

## Method

1. **Marinate:** In a bowl, combine the chicken with the light soy sauce, Shaoxing wine and white pepper. Mix well and marinate at least 15–20 minutes.
2. **Coat and fry:** Mix the beaten egg into the marinated chicken. Dredge each piece thoroughly in cornstarch, shaking off any excess. Heat oil in a pan or wok over medium-high heat. Fry in batches 4–5 minutes until golden brown and crispy. Drain on paper towels and set aside.
3. **Salted egg yolk paste:** Steam the salted egg yolks 8–10 minutes until cooked through, then mash smooth with a fork.
4. **Sauce:** In a clean wok or skillet over medium-low heat, melt the butter. Add the mashed yolks and stir continuously until a rich, foamy lather forms. Add the curry leaves and chilli padi and sauté about 30 seconds until fragrant. Pour in the evaporated milk and add the sugar to balance the saltiness; stir until smooth and creamy.
5. **Combine:** Turn off the heat (or lower to minimum) and immediately toss the fried chicken through the sauce until evenly coated. Serve hot.
