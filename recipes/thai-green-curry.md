# Thai Green Curry แกงเขียวหวาน

> Aromatic Thai green curry with chicken thighs, Thai eggplant, peppers, and basil. Built on a store-bought paste (Mae Ploy or Maesri recommended) enhanced with extra herbs.

**Servings:** 4

## Ingredients

### Paste enhancement (optional but recommended)
- Store-bought green curry paste (Mae Ploy or Maesri — avoid Thai Kitchen)
- Thai basil leaves, finely julienned
- Extra krachai (fingerroot)
- Fermented shrimp paste (kapi)

### Curry
- Coconut milk
- Chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces (preferred over breast — see notes)
- Chicken stock (or water)
- Palm sugar
- Fish sauce
- Makrut (kaffir) lime leaves, centre stem removed, leaves twisted to release aroma
- Thai eggplant, stem removed, cut into bite-sized pieces
- Red and orange bell peppers, chopped
- Thai basil leaves

## Method

1. **Enhance the paste (optional):** Pound julienned Thai basil, extra krachai, and shrimp paste into the store-bought paste. Boosts colour, herbal aroma, and umami.
2. **Fry the paste:** Bring a portion of the coconut milk to a full boil. Add the paste and sauté a few minutes, stirring continuously, until the coconut oil separates and floats to the top.
3. **Cook the chicken:** Cook another minute after oil separates, then stir in chicken thigh pieces and coat in the paste.
4. **Add liquids and season:** Pour in the remaining coconut milk and chicken stock. Season early with palm sugar and fish sauce so the chicken absorbs flavour. Add the bruised lime leaves.
5. **Simmer:** ~10 minutes until chicken is fork-tender.
6. **Check the flavour:** Taste for aroma and depth (don't check for salt yet). If it needs more paste, sauté extra paste in oil separately, dissolve with liquid, then stir in.
7. **Add eggplant:** Once chicken is tender, add Thai eggplant. Push it into the liquid for the first minute to prevent browning. Cook 2–3 minutes until a fork meets slight resistance.
8. **Finish:** Turn off the heat. Stir in chopped peppers and Thai basil — residual heat will wilt them.
9. **Final season:** Taste and adjust saltiness with more fish sauce if needed.

### Notes
- Chicken thighs tenderise beautifully and release juices into the broth. If you must use breast, marinate it in fish sauce first and add at the very end — cook only 1–2 minutes to prevent drying out.
- Serve with rice, rice vermicelli (khanom chin), roti paratha, or even spaghetti.

## The household's version with the Dancing Chef packet (the user, 17–18 Oct 2026)

For 3 people. Eggplant, long beans and carrot instead of the Thai eggplant and bell peppers. No fish
sauce and no Thai basil — the packet covers them.

- 3 chicken thighs
- 1 eggplant
- 1 small carrot
- 80g long beans
- 1 packet Dancing Chef green curry paste (amount as the packet says)
- 100ml water
- 200ml Kara coconut milk
- 2 kaffir lime leaves
- ½ tbsp sugar

1. Cut the ends off the long beans and throw them away. Blanch the beans in hot water first.
2. Sear the chicken first.
3. Add the vegetables and stir-fry briefly, then take the vegetables out.
4. Fry the curry paste with the chicken.
5. Add the water, then the coconut milk, the sugar and the kaffir lime leaves.
6. Simmer for 15 minutes.
7. Put the vegetables back in for the last 10 minutes of the simmer only.
8. Cut the biggest piece of chicken open: no pink.
