# Soy Sauce Steamed Fish with Preserved Radish 菜圃蒸鱼

> Whole fish steamed on ginger and spring onion, dressed with a light soy and chicken-seasoning sauce and topped with pan-fried preserved radish (chai poh), garlic and chilli.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (1 whole fish)

## Ingredients

### Preserved radish topping (chai poh)

- A little oil, plus a little more
- 100g preserved radish (salty type), rinsed, washed and soaked in water for about 20 minutes
- 4 cloves garlic, chopped
- 2 chillies, chopped (optional)
- 1 tsp sugar

### Steaming base & fish

- 1 thumb-size piece ginger, cut into several pieces
- 2 spring onion stems
- 1 whole fish
- Water, for the steamer (quantity not specified)

### Sauce

- 160ml water
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tsp Knorr SavorRich chicken concentrated seasoning
- 1 small piece young ginger, sliced
- 1 tsp sugar

### Garnish

- Spring onion (quantity not specified)

## Method

1. **Prepare and fry the preserved radish:** Rinse, wash and soak the preserved radish in water for about 20 minutes. Heat a pan over medium-low heat with a little oil. Add the radish and pan-fry, stirring constantly, for about 10–13 minutes until it turns white and starts popping. Push the radish to the side of the pan, add a little more oil, the chopped garlic and chopped chilli and sauté until fragrant. Mix everything together, stir in the 1 tsp sugar, turn off the flame and set aside.
2. **Set up the fish for steaming:** Spread the ginger pieces across a steaming dish. Lay the 2 spring onion stems flat on top and place the whole fish over the spring onions.
3. **Steam the fish:** Bring the water in a steamer to a boil over medium-high to high heat. Put in the fish dish, cover and steam for about 9–10 minutes (up to 12 minutes for a larger fish) until cooked through and the eye pops up.
4. **Sauce:** In a separate pan (off the heat to start) combine the water, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, chicken concentrated seasoning, sliced young ginger and sugar. Turn the heat to medium and bring to a boil to melt the sugar and infuse the ginger, then turn off the flame and set aside.
5. **Plate and finish:** Take the fish out of the steamer and pour away the liquid that has collected. Transfer the fish to a serving dish, pour the sauce over, spread the fried preserved radish over the top, garnish with spring onions and serve.
