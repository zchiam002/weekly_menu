# Soy Sauce Chicken 豉油鸡

> Whole chicken marinated in premium dark soya sauce and poached in a caramelised rock sugar, star anise and cinnamon liquid — 20 minutes on a low simmer, then 20 minutes off the heat — brushed with sesame oil and served with a thickened poaching-liquid sauce.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (1 whole chicken, ~1kg)

## Ingredients

### Chicken & marinade

- 1 whole fresh chicken (~1kg)
- 2 tsp salt, for the cavity
- 10 tbsp (~200ml) premium dark soya sauce
- ~15g young ginger, thinly sliced
- 1 stalk spring onion, tied into a small knot

### Poaching base & aromatics

- A little oil
- 60g rock sugar — the creator notes cane sugar or brown sugar can be used instead
- 1.5L hot water
- 1L normal water
- 1 piece cinnamon bark (slightly longer than an index finger)
- 2 star anise
- 2 bulbs garlic, washed
- 2 tbsp salt
- The dark soya sauce reserved from the chicken marinade
- 100ml Chinese rice wine (optional)

### Finishing sauce & coating

- Sesame oil, for brushing the skin (quantity not specified)
- 300ml poaching liquid, reserved from the pot
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tsp sugar
- Cornstarch solution (quantity not specified)

## Method

1. **Marinate the chicken:** Wash the chicken inside and out. Season the cavity with the 2 tsp salt. Pour the 10 tbsp dark soya sauce over the chicken and rub it thoroughly over the skin, thighs and cavity. Put the sliced ginger and the spring onion knot into the cavity. Marinate in the fridge for about 2 hours.
2. **Rest the chicken:** Take it out of the fridge at least 30–45 minutes before cooking so it comes to room temperature.
3. **Caramelise the sugar:** Heat a pot over medium heat with a little oil. Add the rock sugar and fry slowly until melted, dark brown and bubbling.
4. **Poaching liquid:** Carefully add the 1.5L hot water, then the 1L normal water. Add the cinnamon bark, star anise, garlic bulbs, 2 tbsp salt, the reserved dark soya sauce marinade and the Chinese rice wine. Stir gently.
5. **Initial boil:** Cover and bring to a boil. Boil for 10 minutes to extract the flavour of the spices.
6. **Poach — phase 1:** Carefully lower the chicken into the boiling liquid breast-side down, moving it around so hot liquid flows into the cavity. Cover, reduce the heat to low and cook for 20 minutes.
7. **Poach — phase 2:** Turn the heat off completely without lifting the lid and leave the chicken to poach undisturbed in the hot liquid for another 20 minutes.
8. **Coat and cool:** Carefully lift the chicken out and brush the skin with a layer of sesame oil to keep it moist while it cools.
9. **Thicken the sauce and serve:** Ladle 300ml of the poaching liquid into a pan. Taste and add the 1 tsp salt and 2 tsp sugar. Heat and slowly stir in the cornstarch solution until thickened. Drizzle the sauce over the chicken before serving.
