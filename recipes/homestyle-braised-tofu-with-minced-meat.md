# Homestyle Braised Tofu with Minced Meat 家常红烧豆腐

> Firm tofu simmered with a little minced pork in a black bean and soy bean paste sauce, thickened and finished with Shaoxing wine and sesame oil.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (200g firm tofu)

## Ingredients

### Main & aromatics

- Oil, a good amount
- 15g minced pork — the creator notes chicken or mushrooms can be used instead
- 5g ginger, minced
- 3 cloves garlic, chopped
- 200g firm tofu, cut into small cubes
- 2 stalks spring onion, cut into small pieces

### Sauce & seasoning

- 1 tsp black bean paste
- 1 tsp soy bean paste
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- ½ tsp sugar
- 200ml water
- A few dashes pepper
- 1–2 tbsp dark soy sauce (the creator used 2 tbsp)
- Cornstarch solution (quantity not specified)
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (Chinese cooking wine)
- ½ tsp sesame oil

## Method

1. **Sauté the minced pork:** Heat a pan or wok over medium heat with a good amount of oil. Add the unmarinated minced pork and fry until dry and browned, to remove all the moisture.
2. **Aromatics:** Add the minced ginger and chopped garlic and fry until fragrant.
3. **Pastes and seasonings:** Add the black bean paste, soy bean paste, light soy sauce and sugar. Stir-fry well.
4. **Water and tofu:** Pour in the water and stir to deglaze the pan slightly. Add the tofu cubes and spread them out evenly.
5. **Simmer:** Cover, turn the heat down to the lowest setting and simmer for about 15–20 minutes (the creator simmered for 20).
6. **Season and thicken:** Remove the lid, add a few dashes of pepper and the dark soy sauce. Gently mix and increase the heat to medium-high. Slowly stir in the cornstarch solution until thickened to your liking.
7. **Finish:** Splash the Shaoxing wine around the sides of the pan and drizzle in the sesame oil. Add the cut spring onion, turn off the heat, give a gentle final toss and serve.
