# Ultimate Chicken Parm Sandwich

> Panko-crusted fried chicken cutlets topped with a rich vodka sauce and fresh mozzarella, broiled until bubbling, then built into a toasted sandwich with pesto and basil. Chef Jack Ovens.

**Servings:** 4 large sandwiches

## Ingredients

### Chicken & breading

- 2 large chicken breasts, sliced horizontally into 4 fillets
- 2 whole eggs, beaten
- ¾ cup plain all-purpose flour
- 1½ cups Panko breadcrumbs
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dried basil
- Cooking oil (enough to shallow-fry)
- Sea salt flakes & cracked black pepper, to taste

### Vodka sauce

- 2 tbsp (28g) unsalted butter
- 1 brown or yellow onion, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed and roughly chopped
- 100g (3.5 oz) tomato paste
- 1 tsp chilli flakes (optional)
- 2½ tbsp to ⅓ cup vodka
- ¾ cup (180ml) thickened/heavy cream
- Salt & cracked black pepper, to taste

### Sandwich build

- 1 loaf white bread or baguette, cut into 4 sandwich pieces
- Fresh mozzarella, thinly sliced (~3 slices per sandwich)
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter (for toasting)
- Sundried tomato or basil pesto (optional spread)
- Fresh basil leaves (optional garnish)

## Method

### Prep

- Finely dice the onion and roughly chop the garlic.
- Thinly slice the mozzarella and set aside.
- Butterfly the chicken: slice each breast horizontally into 2 even fillets (4 total).
- Slice the bread into 4 portion pieces.

### Vodka sauce

1. **Sweat the onions (3 min):** Melt the butter in a large pan over medium-high. Add the onion, a pinch of salt and ~10 cracks of pepper. Cook, stirring, until soft and translucent without browning.
2. **Garlic & paste (4 min):** Stir in the garlic and sweat 1 min. Add the tomato paste and chilli flakes; cook 3 min, stirring constantly, until the paste deepens in colour and smells fragrant.
3. **Deglaze & simmer (7 min):** Pour in the vodka and stir 1 min, scraping up stuck flavour. Add the cream, season, and bring to a simmer. Reduce to low and cook 6 min until thickened; remove from heat.

### Bread & fry the chicken

4. **Dredging stations:** Bowl 1 — beaten eggs. Bowl 2 — flour. Bowl 3 — Panko mixed with oregano, basil, salt and pepper.
5. **Bread:** Coat each fillet in flour (shake off excess), dip in egg (no dry spots), then press firmly into the seasoned Panko until fully coated.
6. **Fry (4–5 min):** Heat oil to 170°C (see note) in a high-sided pan. Fry 2 fillets at a time, 4–5 min, flipping halfway, until deeply golden and cooked through. Rest on a wire rack and sprinkle immediately with sea salt flakes.

### Assemble & melt

7. **Toast:** Melt 1 tbsp butter in a pan over medium-high and toast the bread faces golden.
8. **Sauce & cheese:** Place the fried fillets on a baking tray. Spoon 2–2½ tbsp warm vodka sauce over each, then top with 3 slices mozzarella.
9. **Broil:** Under a preheated grill/broiler at 180°C (350°F) for ~5 min, until the cheese is melted, bubbling and golden.
10. **Build:** Spread pesto on the bottom bread if using, and a little extra vodka sauce on the faces. Lay down the cheesy chicken parm, garnish with basil and cracked pepper, and top with the other bread. Cut in half and serve.
