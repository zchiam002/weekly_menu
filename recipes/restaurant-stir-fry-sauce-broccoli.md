# Restaurant-Style Stir Fry Sauce for Any Vegetables (Chinese Garlic Oyster Sauce Broccoli) 餐馆蔬菜酱

> A reusable garlic and oyster sauce, thickened to a glossy glaze with fish sauce and a little dark soy and tossed with blanched broccoli. The whole batch is for about 260g of vegetables and works with any greens.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (one batch of sauce is for 260g of vegetables)

## Ingredients

### Blanching vegetables

- Water, for boiling (quantity not specified)
- ½ tsp salt
- A little oil
- 260g broccoli

### Reusable garlic oyster sauce

- Oil, a good amount
- 3–4 pieces ginger, thinly sliced
- 1 whole bulb garlic, chopped
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- ½ tsp sugar
- Cornstarch solution: 150ml water mixed with 1 tbsp cornstarch — the creator notes tapioca starch or potato starch can be used instead
- A little dark soy sauce (optional)

## Method

1. **Blanch the broccoli:** Bring a pot or wok of water to a boil over medium heat. Add the salt, a little oil and the broccoli. Blanch for 10–20 seconds to keep it crunchy, then lift out and set aside.
2. **Infuse the oil with ginger:** Heat a wok or pan over medium heat with a good amount of oil. Add the ginger slices and fry until browned on the sides. Remove and discard the ginger.
3. **Sauté the garlic:** Keeping the heat at medium, add the chopped garlic to the infused oil and stir-fry until fragrant without burning.
4. **Make the sauce:** Add the fish sauce, oyster sauce and sugar and stir briefly. Pour in the cornstarch solution (150ml water with 1 tbsp cornstarch) and a little dark soy sauce. Stir continuously until the sauce thickens into a glossy glaze.
5. **Toss and serve:** Turn off the heat, add the blanched broccoli to the sauce, toss until every piece is coated and serve.
