# Spicy Potatoes with Minced Meat 香辣土豆炒肉碎

> Minced pork and thinly sliced potatoes in a spicy xiang la jiang sauce, steamed briefly in the pan.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (330g minced pork, 330g potatoes)

## Ingredients

### Main

- 330g minced pork
- 330g potatoes, thinly sliced and soaked in water
- 4 cloves garlic, chopped
- 8g ginger, chopped
- A few stalks green onions / spring onions

### Sauce & seasoning

- Oil (quantity not specified)
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- Crushed white pepper (quantity not specified)
- 1 big tbsp xiang la jiang 香辣酱 (spicy fragrance sauce)
- ½ cup water
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce
- ½ tsp sugar

## Method

1. **Fry the aromatics and meat:** Heat some oil in a pan over medium to low heat. Fry the garlic and ginger until fragrant. Add the minced pork and fry until dry and slightly browned, with almost no liquid left.
2. **Season the meat:** Add the oyster sauce and crushed white pepper and mix well.
3. **Fry the spicy sauce:** Push the meat to one side. Add the xiang la jiang to the pan and fry it on its own to release the red chilli oil, then mix thoroughly into the meat.
4. **Add the potatoes and steam:** Add the thinly sliced potatoes and ½ cup water. Cover and steam for 5–10 minutes (the creator steamed for 5).
5. **Final seasoning:** Add the dark soy sauce, sugar and spring onions. Mix well and serve.
