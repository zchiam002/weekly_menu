# Macau-Style Baked Portuguese Chicken Rice 焗葡國雞飯

> Christie at Home's version — tender chicken and soft potatoes in a rich coconut-curry sauce, baked over egg fried rice under a bubbly layer of melted cheese.

**Servings:** 4–6 (9x13-inch deep baking dish)

## Ingredients

### Fried rice base
- 6 cups cooked jasmine rice (cold, day-old)
- 4 large eggs
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil

### Chicken & marinade
- 6 boneless, skinless chicken thighs, sliced in half
- 1 tsp Shaoxing cooking wine (or dry sherry / chicken broth)
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp curry powder
- ¼ tsp salt

### Coconut curry sauce & topping
- 14 fl oz (1 can) full-fat coconut milk
- 2 cups Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled, cubed, boiled fork-tender
- ¾ cup yellow onion, diced
- 1 tsp curry powder
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 cup mozzarella (or cheddar-mozzarella blend), shredded

## Method

1. **Prep:** Marinate the halved chicken thighs with Shaoxing, garlic powder, ½ tsp curry powder and ¼ tsp salt, 15 min. Par-boil the potato cubes ~5 min until fork-tender. Preheat oven to broil / high bake (500°F / 260°C).
2. **Fried rice:** Heat 2 tbsp oil, stir-fry the cold rice to break up clumps. Add 3 tbsp soy sauce and toss to a light golden colour. Push aside, scramble the eggs, then fold through. Spread into the baking dish.
3. **Chicken & sauce:** Wipe the pan, add 1 tbsp oil and the onion; add the chicken and sear both sides until cooked through. Add the potatoes, 1 tsp curry powder, 1 tsp turmeric and 1 tsp salt; stir to coat. Pour in the coconut milk, bring to a soft boil and simmer a few minutes until thickened.
4. **Assemble & bake:** Pour the chicken/potato/sauce over the rice. Top with the cheese. Broil 5–10 min until melted, bubbly and golden-spotted.
