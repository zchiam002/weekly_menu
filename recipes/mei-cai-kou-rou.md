# Chinese Pork Belly with Preserved Vegetables 梅菜扣肉

> Mei cai kou rou — pork belly blanched with warm spices, marinated, then steamed upside down over salty-sweet preserved mustard greens until meltingly soft. Inverted onto the plate at the table.

**Servings:** 6–8

## Ingredients

### Blanching the pork belly

- 420g pork belly
- 1 thumb-size ginger, sliced
- 1 tsp white peppercorns
- 1½ tsp salt
- 1 piece cinnamon bark
- 1 bay leaf
- 2 star anise
- ½ tsp cloves

### Marinade

- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 2 tbsp low-sodium light soy sauce
- 1½ tbsp Chinese cooking wine (Shaoxing Huatiao)
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- ½ cup water

### Other

- 3–4 cloves garlic
- 300g mei cai (preserved mustard greens)
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce, to marinate the cooked pork
- Cornstarch solution, to thicken the sauce

## Method

1. **Blanch:** Put the pork belly in a pot with the sliced ginger, white peppercorns, salt, cinnamon bark, bay leaf, star anise and cloves. Cover with water, bring to a boil, then simmer until the pork is cooked through and a chopstick passes easily. Lift out and pat dry.
2. **Colour the pork:** Rub the cooked pork belly all over with the 1 tbsp dark soy sauce and let it take on colour.
3. **Sear:** Fry the pork belly, skin-side down first, until the skin blisters and browns. Cool slightly, then slice into even pieces about ½cm thick.
4. **Prepare the mei cai:** Soak the preserved vegetables well and rinse repeatedly to wash out the excess salt and grit — this step decides whether the dish is balanced or overwhelming. Squeeze dry and chop.
5. **Fry the mei cai:** Fry the chopped garlic until fragrant, then add the mei cai and stir-fry until aromatic.
6. **Assemble:** Mix the marinade — oyster sauce, light soy sauce, Shaoxing, sugar, sesame oil and water. Lay the pork slices skin-side down in a heatproof bowl, packed neatly. Pack the fried mei cai on top and pour the marinade over.
7. **Steam:** Steam for 1½–2 hours until the pork is completely tender and the fat has softened.
8. **Serve:** Pour off the liquid into a pan and thicken it with the cornstarch solution. Invert the bowl onto a serving plate so the pork sits on top, and spoon the thickened sauce over.
