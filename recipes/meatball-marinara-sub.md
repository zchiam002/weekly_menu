# Meatball Marinara Sub

> Rustic, comforting meatball sub — beef meatballs seared then braised in a rich homemade marinara, piled into oiled sub rolls with mozzarella and Parmesan and broiled until bubbling. Lirim Gula. Makes a big batch (also great over pasta).

**Servings:** ~6–8

## Ingredients

### Meatballs

- 1.5 kg (3.3 lb) good-quality ground beef (12% fat recommended)
- 1 medium onion, blended
- 3 cloves garlic, blended
- 2 whole eggs
- Breadcrumbs (added gradually by eye until the mix is easy to mould)
- Olive oil (a splash for blending, plus more for frying)
- Salt & black pepper, to taste

### Marinara sauce

- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, smashed and roughly chopped
- A handful cherry tomatoes, halved (optional)
- Tomato puree / tomato paste
- 2 cans chopped tomatoes
- Water (to rinse out the tomato cans)
- Salt & black pepper, to taste

### Finishes & subs

- Fresh basil leaves, torn (added at the end)
- Sub rolls / baguettes
- Fresh mozzarella, shredded or sliced
- Parmesan, grated
- Olive oil (for brushing the bread)

## Method

### Mix & shape the meatballs

1. **Blend aromatics:** Pulse 1 onion, 3 garlic cloves and a splash of olive oil in a blender until smooth.
2. **Combine:** In a large bowl, mix the beef with the onion-garlic paste, 2 eggs, and a generous seasoning of salt and pepper.
3. **Bind:** Gradually add breadcrumbs, mixing by hand until the meat loses its stickiness and holds a shape.
4. **Roll & chill:** Roll into even, tightly packed meatballs on a parchment-lined tray. Chill 30 min in the fridge (or 10–15 min in the freezer) to firm up so they don't fall apart.

### Sear & build the marinara

5. **Brown the meatballs (5 min):** Heat a splash of olive oil in a large pot over medium-high. Sear the meatballs in batches to build a deep crispy crust — don't cook through yet. Remove and set aside, keeping the rendered fat and juices in the pot.
6. **Sauté the base (10–15 min):** In the same pot, add the chopped onion, smashed garlic and cherry tomatoes. Pour in a little "tomato water" (from rinsing the cans) and scrape up the browned fond. Cook down 10–15 min.
7. **Cook out the paste (5–10 min):** Add the tomato puree, stir through, and fry 5–10 min to remove the raw metallic edge and sweeten the base.
8. **Simmer (30 min):** Add the 2 cans chopped tomatoes and the remaining tomato water. Season generously. Bring to a boil, then simmer uncovered ~30 min until thick, rich and deep red.

### Bring together & assemble

9. **Braise the meatballs:** Return the seared meatballs and their resting juices to the sauce. Simmer gently 15–20 min so they finish cooking through and soak up the sauce.
10. **Fresh finish:** Off the heat, stir in torn basil. (Don't add basil earlier or you lose its bright aroma.)
11. **Build the sub:** Slice open the rolls, brush the insides with olive oil and season. Spoon in the meatballs with plenty of marinara.
12. **Melt the cheese:** Top with mozzarella, grated Parmesan and a couple of basil leaves. Broil or bake in a hot oven until the cheese is melted, bubbly and toasted.
