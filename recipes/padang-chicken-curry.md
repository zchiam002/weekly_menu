# Padang / Indonesian Chicken Curry 印尼咖喱鸡

> Quick Indonesian-style chicken curry using Dancing Chef Indonesian Curry Paste, simmered with chicken and potato in coconut milk. Pairs with prata or rice.

**Servings:** ~4 (450g chicken)

## Ingredients

- 1 packet Dancing Chef Indonesian Curry Paste (100g)
- 450g chicken, cut into pieces
- 150g potato, cut into pieces and half-cooked
- 200ml coconut milk
- 300ml water

### Optional (to enhance flavour)
- Lemongrass, bruised
- Kaffir lime leaves

## Method

1. Add the curry paste, water and coconut milk to a pot; bring to a boil.
2. Add the chicken and potato; simmer until the chicken is cooked through.
3. Remove from heat and serve.

### Notes
- Paste already contains shallot, chilli, garlic, galangal, lemongrass, candlenut, turmeric, coriander & pepper — no separate spice blend needed.
- This curry **already includes potato**, so Wednesday's potato is covered by the curry itself.
