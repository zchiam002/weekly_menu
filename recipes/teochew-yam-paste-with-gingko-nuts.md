# Teochew Yam Paste with Gingko Nuts 潮州白果芋泥 (Orh Nee / Orh Ni)

> Teochew orh nee — steamed, sweetened and pan-folded yam paste served with pureed pumpkin, sweet gingko nuts and salted coconut milk.

**Servings:** Not specified in source

## Ingredients

### Sweetened gingko nuts

- 200ml water
- 3 tbsp brown sugar
- ~200g canned gingko nuts, drained

### Salted coconut milk

- 250ml coconut milk
- ⅓ tsp salt

### Yam & pumpkin paste

- Taro / yam (quantity not specified)
- 150g pumpkin
- 50g brown sugar
- ~1–2 tbsp cooking oil (normal cooking oil or vegetable oil)

## Method

1. **Steam the yam:** Steam the taro / yam for about 20–25 minutes, or until soft.
2. **Cook the gingko nuts:** In a pot combine the water, 3 tbsp brown sugar and the drained gingko nuts. Cook for about 20 minutes, then turn off the heat and set aside.
3. **Heat the coconut milk:** In a pot over the lowest flame possible, heat the coconut milk with the salt. Bring to a gentle boil until bubbles form around the side, then turn off the heat and set aside.
4. **Steam and puree the pumpkin:** Steam the pumpkin until soft. Puree it separately and set aside.
5. **Puree the yam:** Put the hot steamed yam into a food processor (or mash with a fork or potato masher), add the 50g brown sugar and puree until smooth.
6. **Fry the yam paste:** Heat the oil in a pan, add the pureed yam and fold over low heat until the sugar is evenly mixed in and it becomes a smooth, soft dough. Remove from the heat.
7. **Assemble and serve:** Dish the yam paste into bowls, top with the pumpkin puree and gingko nuts, and pour the coconut milk over.
