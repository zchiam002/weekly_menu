# Hakka Mei Cai Kou Rou 梅菜扣肉

> Melt-in-your-mouth Hakka pork belly with sweet preserved mustard greens. The skin is poached, poked, dark-soy coated and fried before steaming — that's what gives it the pillowy texture.

**Servings:** 4–6

## Ingredients

### Poached pork belly

- 600g (21.16 oz) pork belly
- 1 thumb-sized young ginger
- 2 tbsp Chinese rice wine

> After poaching, poke holes into the pork belly skin, then coat it with premium dark soy sauce before frying.

### Marinated pork belly

- 1½ tbsp oyster sauce
- 1½ tbsp light soy sauce
- 2 tbsp premium dark soy sauce
- 1 tsp five spice powder
- 1 tsp sugar
- 125ml (4.23 fl oz) water
- A few dashes white pepper

### Stir-fried preserved vegetables

- 1 pinkie-sized cinnamon bark
- 1 star anise
- 300g sweet preserved mustard (mei cai)
- 4 cloves garlic
- 4 slices young ginger
- ½ tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce

## Method

1. **Poach:** Put the pork belly in a pot with the young ginger and the Chinese rice wine, cover with water and poach until cooked through. Lift out and pat dry.
2. **Poke & colour:** Poke holes all over the pork belly skin, then coat the skin with premium dark soy sauce.
3. **Fry:** Fry the pork belly skin-side down until the skin blisters and browns. Cool slightly, then slice.
4. **Marinate:** Mix the oyster sauce, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, five spice powder, sugar, water and white pepper. Toss the sliced pork in the marinade.
5. **Mei cai:** Soak the sweet preserved mustard well and rinse repeatedly to wash out excess salt and grit, then squeeze dry and chop. Fry the cinnamon bark and star anise until fragrant, add the garlic and young ginger slices, then the mei cai. Season with the sugar and light soy sauce and stir-fry until aromatic.
6. **Assemble:** Pack the pork slices skin-side down into a heatproof bowl, top with the fried mei cai and pour over the marinade.
7. **Steam:** Steam until the pork belly is completely tender and the fat has softened.
8. **Serve:** Invert the bowl onto a serving plate so the pork sits on top.
