# Thai Olive Fried Rice 泰式橄榄炒饭 (Khao Phad Nahm Liap)

> Thai fried rice with minced pork and salted black olives, seasoned with fish sauce and topped with sliced omelette, crispy dried shrimp, cashews, chilli and lime.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (~600g cooked rice, 200g minced pork)

## Ingredients

### Omelette topping

- 3 eggs, beaten together, no seasoning
- Oil (quantity not specified)

### Crispy dried shrimp

- 35g dried shrimp, rehydrated
- A little oil

### Main & aromatics (fried rice)

- Oil (quantity not specified)
- 5 cloves garlic, chopped
- 10 salted black olives, pitted and chopped
- 200g minced pork — the creator notes minced chicken can be used instead
- White pepper (quantity not specified), plus a little more
- ~600g cooked / overnight rice (from 2 cups of uncooked rice)
- 2 tbsp fish sauce

### Condiments & garnishes

- Roasted cashew nuts (quantity not specified)
- A few padi chillies, sliced
- A few slices of fresh shallot
- 1 wedge green lime, plus a little chopped lime
- Sliced omelette (from the 3 eggs)
- Crispy dried shrimp (from the 35g dried shrimp)

## Method

1. **Fry the omelette:** Heat a suitable amount of oil in a pan over medium or medium-low heat. Pour in the beaten eggs and fry. Add a little more oil before flipping and fry the second side for about 30 seconds until set. Remove and slice thinly.
2. **Crispy dried shrimp:** Add a little oil to the same pan and spread the rehydrated dried shrimp across it. Turn the heat to low and fry slowly for about 3 minutes, stirring constantly so they don't burn, until hard and crispy. Remove and set aside.
3. **Aromatics and pork:** In the same pan with the remaining oil (add a little more if it's dry), add the garlic and chopped olives. Stir well, then add the minced pork. Cook over medium to medium-high heat until thoroughly cooked, then add white pepper and fry until dry.
4. **Rice:** Add the overnight rice over medium-high heat (don't lower the heat). Use a spatula to press and break up any clumps, stir-frying until dry.
5. **Season:** Add the fish sauce and a little more white pepper. Stir-fry thoroughly to combine.
6. **Plate and garnish:** Transfer to a serving dish and top with the cashews, padi chilli, fresh shallot, lime wedge (and chopped lime), sliced omelette and crispy dried shrimp.
