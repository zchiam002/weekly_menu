# Szechuan Spicy Stir Fry Vegetables with Beef 麻辣香锅 (Mala Xiang Guo)

> Blanched lotus root, potato, cabbage, bean curd skin, enoki and glass noodles stir-fried with beef in a spiced chilli oil, finished with pounded Sichuan peppercorns and roasted peanuts.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (300g beef plus the blanched vegetables)

## Ingredients

### Beef & marinade

- 300g beef
- 1 tsp cornflour
- 1 tbsp water

### Vegetables & noodles (for blanching)

- 200g lotus root
- 1 potato
- 150g Chinese cabbage
- 60g thin bean curd skin
- 200g enoki mushroom
- Sweet potato thick vermicelli (quantity not specified), pre-soaked in hot water for a few hours

### Whole spices & aromatics (oil infusion)

- Oil (quantity not specified)
- 1 piece cinnamon bark
- 1 piece star anise
- ½ tsp cloves
- 1–2 bay leaves
- ~20 dried chillies, hydrated, deseeded and cut
- 1 thumb-size piece ginger, sliced

### Sauce & seasoning

- 2 tbsp spicy bean paste
- 1 tbsp xiang la jiang 香辣酱
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce

### Finishing & garnishes

- 1½ tbsp Sichuan peppercorns, pounded
- Chinese parsley (quantity not specified)
- 40g roasted peanuts

## Method

1. **Blanch the root vegetables:** Boil the lotus root and potato in hot water until just cooked through, so they keep some crunch, then drain.
2. **Blanch the cabbage:** In the same boiling water, boil the Chinese cabbage until done, then scoop out.
3. **Blanch the bean curd skin and mushrooms:** Briefly blanch the thin bean curd skin (to wash off the oil) and the enoki in boiling water, then remove.
4. **Blanch the vermicelli:** Add the pre-soaked sweet potato vermicelli to the boiling water and heat until the water comes back to a boil (about 4 minutes), then remove.
5. **Infuse the oil:** Heat oil in a pan, add the cinnamon bark, star anise, cloves and bay leaf, and fry over low heat until fragrant. Remove and discard the whole spices.
6. **Fry the dried chillies:** In the infused oil, fry the cut, hydrated dried chillies until fragrant and the colour changes. Remove the chillies and set aside.
7. **Aromatics and sauce:** Add the sliced ginger, spicy bean paste and xiang la jiang to the pan and stir-fry.
8. **Cook the beef:** Add the beef (marinated with the cornflour and water) and stir-fry until about 50–60% cooked.
9. **Stir-fry everything together:** Add all the blanched vegetables, bean curd skin, mushrooms, vermicelli and the fried dried chillies back into the pan. Stir well to coat in the sauce.
10. **Peppercorns and garnishes:** Add the pounded Sichuan peppercorns, Chinese parsley and roasted peanuts. Toss and stir-fry evenly.
11. **Season and finish:** Add the light soy sauce, mix thoroughly and serve.
