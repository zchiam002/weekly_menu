# Sticky Coffee Ribs 咖啡排骨 (Coffee Pork Ribs)

> Spare ribs marinated with bicarbonate of soda, egg white and cornflour, double-fried until crisp, then tossed in a sticky glaze of 3-in-1 instant coffee, dark soy, oyster sauce and honey.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (580g spare ribs)

## Ingredients

### Ribs & marinade

- 580g prime ribs / spare ribs, cut into manageable pieces
- ½ tsp bicarbonate of soda
- ½ tsp five-spice powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 2 tbsp Chinese rice wine
- 2 egg whites
- 3 tbsp corn flour
- Pepper (quantity not specified)

### Sauce

- ¾ cup water
- 2 packets instant coffee (3-in-1, with creamer and sugar)
- 2 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp corn flour
- 2 tbsp dark soy sauce — the transcript has this as "tartar sauce"; the extracted recipe reads it as dark soy sauce, so check the video
- 2 tbsp honey — the creator notes sugar, cane sugar or brown sugar can be used instead

### Frying & garnish

- Oil, for deep frying (quantity not specified)
- Spring onions (quantity not specified)
- Sesame seeds (quantity not specified)

## Method

1. **Marinate the ribs:** Put the ribs in a bowl with the bicarbonate of soda, five-spice powder, salt, light soy sauce, oyster sauce, rice wine, egg whites, corn flour and pepper. Mix well and marinate in the fridge for at least 1 hour.
2. **Rest the ribs:** Take the ribs out of the fridge and let them come back to room temperature before cooking.
3. **Mix the sauce:** In a bowl combine the water, instant coffee, garlic, oyster sauce, corn flour, dark soy sauce and honey. Mix thoroughly.
4. **First deep-fry:** Heat oil in a pan over medium heat. Deep-fry the ribs in two batches until golden brown and about 80% cooked through (turning them after about 2 minutes). Remove and drain.
5. **Double fry:** Turn the heat up to high until the oil is very hot. Return all the ribs and fry for 1–1½ minutes until the skin is tight and crispy. Remove and drain.
6. **Reduce the sauce:** Pour the oil out of the pan and put it back over heat. Stir the sauce mixture well, pour it in and bring to a boil until it reduces to a sticky glaze with small bubbles that coats a metal spoon easily.
7. **Coat and serve:** Add the fried ribs to the sticky coffee sauce and toss until every piece is evenly coated. Dish up, garnish with spring onions and sesame seeds and serve.
