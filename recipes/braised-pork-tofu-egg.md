# Braised Pork with Tofu and Egg (Tau Yu Bak / Lou Bak)

> Comfort-food classic — pork belly and shoulder braised in caramelised soy with garlic, shallots and warm spices, then infused with firm tofu and hard-boiled eggs. Perfect with hot white rice.

**Servings:** 3–4 | **Total time:** ~1 hr 15 min

## Ingredients

- Pork: a mix of pork belly and pork shoulder (keeps it from being overly greasy while staying succulent)
- 4 hard-boiled eggs, peeled
- 1 block firm tofu (tau kwa), cut into thick slices
- 6 cloves garlic, peeled
- 6 shallots, peeled
- 80ml light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp premium caramelised dark soy sauce (thick and sweet variety)
- 1 tbsp white sugar
- 2 tbsp cooking oil
- Spices: 1 star anise, 4 whole cloves, 1 cinnamon stick (~3 inches), ½ tsp five-spice powder
- Water (for braising)

## Method

1. **Prep (10 min):** Cut the pork belly and shoulder into uniform pieces about "two fingers" wide. Peel the hard-boiled eggs, slice the tofu into thick slabs, and peel the garlic and shallots. Measure the 80ml light soy sauce and set it right next to the stove before cooking.
2. **Caramelise the base (5 min):** In a cold pot over low heat, add the oil, then the sugar. Stir continuously, slowly frying the sugar until it melts and turns light golden brown. Watch closely — sugar burns in seconds.
3. **Stop the cook with soy (2 min):** The instant the sugar turns brown, pour in the 80ml light soy sauce (it will splatter). This cools the oil and arrests the caramelisation so the sugar doesn't turn black and bitter. Stir in the 1 tbsp dark soy sauce.
4. **Sear meat & aromatics (5 min):** Add the pork to the sauce. Turn to medium and stir-fry ~2 min to coat and colour. Add the garlic, shallots, star anise, cloves and cinnamon stick. Sauté another 1–2 min until deeply aromatic.
5. **Long simmer (40 min):** Pour in enough water to almost cover the meat. Bring to a rolling boil, cover, reduce to low, and simmer 40 min to break down the collagen. Check halfway that the liquid hasn't dried out.
6. **Infuse tofu & eggs (10 min):** After 40 min the liquid should be reduced by about half. Nestle in the tofu slices and peeled eggs. Sprinkle ½ tsp five-spice powder over the top. Stir gently, cover, and simmer a final 10 min.
7. **Serve (2 min):** Uncover and give a final mix — the eggs and tofu should be an even amber colour. Taste and adjust seasoning. Ladle into a deep bowl and serve steaming hot with white rice.
