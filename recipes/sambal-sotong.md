# Sambal Sotong 叁巴苏东 (Asian Spicy Calamari)

> Quick stir-fry of cuttlefish or squid with okra and green onion in chilli paste.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (230g squid)

## Ingredients

### Main & vegetables

- Oil (quantity not specified)
- 1 medium green onion, sliced
- 5 lady's fingers (okra), sliced
- 230g cuttlefish / calamari / squid

### Sauce

- 3 tbsp chilli paste
- A little water (quantity not specified)

## Method

1. **Sauté the onion:** Heat some oil in a pan, add the sliced green onion and stir-fry until softened and fragrant.
2. **Okra and chilli paste:** Add the sliced okra and the chilli paste and stir well to combine.
3. **Cook the squid:** Add the squid and stir-fry for about 2–3 minutes until cooked through and curled up.
4. **Finish:** Add a little water if you want a slightly saucy consistency, stir-fry until completely cooked, turn off the heat and serve.
