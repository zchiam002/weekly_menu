# Madam Kwan's Beef Rendang (Paste)

> Shortcut beef rendang using Madam Kwan's ready-made rendang paste — beef simmered in coconut milk with lime leaf and lemongrass until tender. 50 minutes rather than an afternoon.

**Servings:** 3–4 (per packet)

## Ingredients

- 1 packet Madam Kwan's rendang paste
- 600g sliced beef
- 300g fresh coconut milk
- 300g water
- 1 lime leaf
- ½ stalk lemongrass

## Method

1. Add the packet of rendang paste, the water and the coconut milk to a pot.
2. Add the sliced beef, lime leaf and lemongrass, and stir.
3. Simmer on low heat for 50 minutes, or until the meat is tender.
4. Best served with rice, or nasi kunyit (turmeric sticky rice).

## Paste ingredients (as printed)

Shallots (26%), cooking oil (palm), garlic (13%), curry powder, chilli, candlenut, lemongrass, turmeric, ginger, galangal, salt, sugar, flavour enhancer (INS 621), preservatives (INS 211, INS 202).

**Allergens:** Seafood (crustaceans), tree nuts.

**Nutrition:** per 100g — 550 kcal, sodium 1762mg (77% DV). Per 200g serving — 1100 kcal, sodium 3524mg (153% DV).

## Notes

- **Scaling for ~5.5 adults:** roughly 1.5–2 packets. Two packets with ~900g–1kg sliced beef, 500ml coconut milk, 500ml water, 2 lime leaves and 1 stalk lemongrass gives a generous, saucier pot.
- **Very high sodium** — do not add extra salt, and go easy on salty sides at the same meal.
- Contains crustacean (shrimp) as a background flavouring in the paste, and candlenut (tree nut).
