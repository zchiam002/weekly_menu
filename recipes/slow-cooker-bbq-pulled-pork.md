# Slow Cooker BBQ Pulled Pork

> Set-and-forget pulled pork — a spice-rubbed pork shoulder slow-cooked over onions in a BBQ, Worcestershire and liquid-smoke sauce for 8 hours, then shredded and tossed juicy. Natasha's Kitchen.

**Yield:** ~8–10 servings

## Ingredients

### Meat & dry rub

- 1 pork shoulder roast (pork butt), ~3–4 lb
- 1 tbsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp paprika

### Slow cooker base & sauce

- 1 large onion, chopped
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1 cup BBQ sauce (plus extra for serving)
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tbsp liquid smoke
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 3 garlic cloves, pressed

### For serving (optional)

- Burger buns or sandwich rolls
- Coleslaw (Natasha recommends a creamy coconut coleslaw)

## Method

1. **Base (10 min):** Chop the onion and spread it evenly across the bottom of the slow cooker as a flavour base.
2. **Season & rub (5 min):** Stir together the salt, black pepper and paprika. Sprinkle over the pork and massage into all sides. Place the roast on top of the onions.
3. **Liquids (5 min):** Mix the chicken broth, BBQ sauce, Worcestershire, liquid smoke, brown sugar and pressed garlic. Pour over the pork.
4. **Slow cook (8 hr):** Cover and cook on Low for 8 hours, until the meat is completely tender and pulls apart effortlessly with a fork.
5. **Shred & sauce (10 min):** Transfer the pork to a large dish and shred with two forks, discarding large pieces of fat. Ladle a little of the slow cooker liquid over to keep it juicy, drizzle with extra BBQ sauce, and toss to coat.
6. **Serve:** Pile onto buns with coleslaw, or serve alongside mashed potatoes.
