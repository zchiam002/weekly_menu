# Braised Ribs with Radish & Carrots 红烧排骨

> Pork ribs caramelised in rock sugar and braised with rice wine, oyster sauce, dark soy and whole spices, with radish and carrot added for the last 20 minutes and the sauce thickened to coat.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (650g pork ribs, 320g radish, 140g carrots)

## Ingredients

### Main & aromatics

- Oil (quantity not specified)
- 25g rock sugar
- 650g pork ribs
- 200ml Chinese rice wine
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 3 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 piece cinnamon bark (~4–6 inches)
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 star anise
- 20g young ginger, slightly crushed
- Water, enough to just cover the ribs

### Root vegetables

- 320g radish, cut into cubes
- 140g carrots

### Seasoning & thickening

- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp sugar
- Cornstarch solution (quantity not specified)

## Method

1. **Caramelise the sugar:** Heat a wok over medium heat with some oil. Add the rock sugar and melt it until caramelised, with bubbles forming.
2. **Brown the ribs and add seasonings:** Add the ribs and brown them. Pour in the rice wine, oyster sauce and dark soy sauce, then add the cinnamon bark, bay leaf, star anise and ginger. Mix thoroughly.
3. **Water and initial boil:** Add enough water to just cover the ribs. Turn the heat up to high, cover and bring to a boil.
4. **First simmer:** Once boiling, put the lid back on, reduce the heat to low and simmer for 40 minutes.
5. **Radish and carrots:** Add the radish and carrots. Turn the heat up to medium or medium-low, put the lid back on and cook for another 20 minutes.
6. **Season and thicken:** Add the salt and sugar and mix gently. Turn the heat up to medium and stir in the cornstarch solution until the sauce coats the ribs nicely, then serve.
