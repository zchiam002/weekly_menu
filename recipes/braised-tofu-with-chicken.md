# Braised Tofu with Chicken 豆腐鸡煲 (Chicken Beancurd Pot)

> Browned firm tofu stewed in a claypot with marinated chicken breast, mushrooms, dried shrimp and carrot in an oyster and dark soy sauce made with the mushroom and shrimp soaking water, thickened and finished with Hua Tiao wine.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (330g chicken breast, 400g tofu)

## Ingredients

### Chicken & marinade

- 330g chicken breast, cut into pieces
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 3 tsp corn flour
- Dashes of pepper

### Mushrooms & marinade

- Dried mushrooms (quantity not specified), rehydrated in 1 cup water — reserve the water
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil

### Dried shrimp

- 30g dried shrimp, rehydrated in 1 cup water — reserve the water

### Main & aromatics

- 400g firm beancurd / tofu, cut into cubes
- Oil (quantity not specified)
- 10g ginger, chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1 red onion, sliced
- 2 red chillies, deseeded and cut into pieces
- 180g carrot, thinly sliced
- 1 stalk spring onion — white stem separated, green part kept for garnish

### Sauce & seasoning

- 1 cup reserved mushroom soaking water
- 1 cup reserved dried shrimp soaking water
- 3 tbsp oyster sauce
- 2 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- About ½ cup water (if the sauce gets too thick)
- Cornstarch solution (quantity not specified)
- Chinese cooking wine (Hua Tiao Jiu) (quantity not specified)

## Method

1. **Marinate the chicken:** Combine the chicken breast with the light soy sauce, sesame oil, corn flour and pepper. Mix well and marinate for at least 20–30 minutes.
2. **Marinate the mushrooms:** Rehydrate the dried mushrooms in 1 cup of water (reserve the soaking water). Marinate them with the sugar, light soy sauce and sesame oil. Mix well and set aside.
3. **Brown the tofu:** Heat a claypot (or pan) with some oil. Add the tofu cubes and brown on all sides. Remove and set aside.
4. **Aromatics and vegetables:** In the remaining oil add the ginger, garlic, red onion, red chillies and rehydrated dried shrimp (keeping the 1 cup of soaking water). Stir-fry until fragrant. Add the carrot, the marinated mushrooms and the white stem of the spring onion.
5. **Liquids and sauce base:** Pour in the reserved dried shrimp water and mushroom water. Add the oyster sauce, dark soy sauce and sugar. Mix well, cover and bring to a boil.
6. **Chicken and tofu:** Add the marinated chicken to the boiling sauce and spread it out. Add the browned tofu and any crispy bits. Add about ½ cup extra water if the sauce is too thick.
7. **Simmer:** Cover and stew for 20 minutes.
8. **Thicken:** Reduce the heat to medium or medium-low. Slowly pour in the cornstarch solution, stirring gently, to thicken the sauce without breaking the tofu.
9. **Finish:** Splash the Hua Tiao wine around the edge of the pot and sprinkle with the green spring onion before serving.
