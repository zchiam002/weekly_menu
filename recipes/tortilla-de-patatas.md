# Tortilla de Patatas (Spanish Omelette)

> Classic Spanish potato omelette for one or two — potatoes and onion fried soft, rested in beaten egg for a better texture, then cooked and plate-flipped to golden. Soft and slightly moist in the middle.

**Servings:** 1–2 | **Total time:** ~35–45 min

## Ingredients

- 1 medium potato (waxy varieties like Yukon Gold or red potatoes work best)
- 1 medium onion
- 3 eggs
- Cooking oil (enough to coat and fry the vegetables in a 10-inch pan)
- ½ tsp salt (or slightly less)
- Freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. **Prep the vegetables (5 min):** Peel the potato, then use a mandolin to slice the potato and onion thin (~1/5 inch). Cut the slices into quarters or halves so they cook faster and fit evenly. You should have about 2 cups potatoes and 1 cup onions.
2. **Fry (10–15 min):** Heat a 10-inch pan over medium heat with the oil until it shimmers. Add the potatoes and onions, tossing to coat. Cook 10–15 min, flipping and stirring every 3–4 min, until the potatoes are fork-tender and the onions golden and caramelised.
3. **Strain & cool (2 min):** Scoop the mixture out with a strainer, leaving the oil behind in the pan for later.
4. **Egg base (2 min):** Beat the 3 eggs until smooth. Stir in the warm potato-onion mixture. Season with up to ½ tsp salt and a pinch of black pepper.
5. **The secret rest (15–20 min):** Stir gently to combine, cover the bowl, and let it sit at room temperature 15–20 min so the potatoes absorb the egg — this gives a much better texture.
6. **Cook the first side (3–4 min):** Leave about 1 tbsp of the reserved oil in the pan over medium heat. Pour in the mixture and smooth into an even layer. Cook on medium-high ~1 min until the bottom edges begin to set, then medium-low for 2–3 min. The top should still be runny.
7. **The plate flip (1 min):** Loosen the edges with a spatula so it slides freely. Place a large flat plate upside down over the pan, hold both firmly, and quickly invert to flip the omelette onto the plate.
8. **Finish (2 min):** Slide it back into the pan, uncooked side down. Cook over medium-high briefly to brown the bottom, then medium-low 1–2 min until the middle sets. Slide onto a clean plate and serve warm or at room temperature.

> Chef's tip: golden brown outside but soft and slightly moist inside. Customise by mixing in cubed ham, cooked bacon, or cheese at step 4.
