# Enoki Mushroom Meat Roll 日式金菇肥牛(猪肉)卷

> Very thin shabu shabu beef (or pork) rolled around bundles of enoki, seared, then simmered in a mirin, soy and honey glaze.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (makes 8 rolls from 250g beef and 150g enoki)

## Ingredients

### Main & meat rolls

- 250g shabu shabu beef (very thinly sliced)
- 150g enoki mushroom, roots cut off, washed, patted dry and divided into 8 portions
- Oil (quantity not specified)

### Sauce

- 3 tbsp water
- 5g grated ginger
- 1 red shallot, grated
- 1 clove garlic, grated
- 2 tbsp mirin (sweet cooking wine)
- 2½ tbsp Japanese soy sauce — the creator notes regular soy sauce can be used instead
- 1 tbsp honey — the creator notes ½ tbsp sugar can be used instead

## Method

1. **Prepare the mushrooms:** Cut the roots off the enoki, wash thoroughly, pat dry and divide into 8 equal portions.
2. **Assemble the rolls:** Lay 2 slices of shabu shabu beef flat on a plate, overlapping by about half. Place 1 portion of enoki near the end and roll up tightly. Press down on the seam. Repeat to make 8 rolls.
3. **Mix the sauce:** In a bowl combine the water, grated ginger, grated red shallot, grated garlic, mirin, Japanese soy sauce and honey. Mix thoroughly.
4. **Sear the rolls:** Heat a pan with oil until hot and well coated. Place the rolls in seam-side down first, to seal them and stop them unrolling. Sear all sides until browned.
5. **Sauce and simmer:** Pour the sauce over the seared rolls. Simmer and reduce the sauce slightly until the mushrooms are soft and the meat has taken up the glaze. Remove from the heat and serve.
