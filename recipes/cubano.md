# Cubano (Cuban Sandwich)

> The full works — overnight mojo-marinated pork shoulder braised and shredded, homemade Cuban bread, seared ham, Swiss, mustard and pickles, buttered and pressed until the crust shatters.

**Yield:** Bread makes 2 loaves; pork yields 3–4 lb — enough for roughly 6–8 large sandwiches with leftover pork.

## Ingredients

### 1. Mojo pork & marinade

- 3–4 lb boneless pork shoulder
- 2 peeled shallots
- 2 heads' worth of garlic cloves (~15 cloves)
- Zest of 2 oranges
- Zest of 3 limes
- 1 cup (240ml) fresh orange juice
- 1 cup (240ml) fresh lime juice
- 2 tbsp fresh oregano leaves
- ½ bunch fresh mint leaves
- 1 tbsp (5g) ground cumin
- 2 whole serrano chiles
- 1 cup (240ml) olive oil
- 2 tbsp (26g) kosher salt
- Optional: a splash of chicken stock (for braising)

### 2. Homemade Cuban bread (makes 2 loaves)

- 1¼ cups (295ml) warm water (~100°F)
- 1 tbsp (10g) active dry yeast
- 3½ cups (500g) bread flour
- 1 tbsp (13g) granulated sugar
- 2 tsp (5g) fine sea salt
- 2½ tbsp (30g) solidified lard

### 3. Sandwich assembly (per sandwich)

- Freshly baked Cuban bread, cut to length
- Softened unsalted butter (for toasting and pressing)
- 5–6 slices high-quality boiled ham (cooked, not smoked)
- Shredded mojo pork
- Swiss cheese slices
- Yellow mustard
- Whole dill pickles, sliced lengthwise into "long boys"

## Method

1. **Marinate the pork (overnight):** Blend the shallots, garlic, orange and lime zest, orange and lime juice, oregano, mint, cumin, serrano chiles, olive oil and kosher salt until completely smooth. **Reserve 1 cup of marinade** in the fridge for later. Pour the rest over the pork shoulder in a large bag, seal, and marinate overnight in the fridge.
2. **Mix & knead the bread dough (45–60 min rise):** Whisk the warm water and yeast; let bloom 8 min. In a stand mixer, combine bread flour, sugar and sea salt. Mix on medium-low while pouring in the yeast mixture. Knead 3–5 min until it starts to smooth out. Add the lard and mix 1 min more until incorporated. Shape into a ball, place in an oiled bowl, cover, and rise 45–60 min until doubled.
3. **Shape & bake the bread (~1 hr):** Punch down and divide into two. Rest under a damp towel 10 min. Flatten each into a ½-inch thick rectangle, roll up tightly bottom to top, pinch the seams, and taper the ends. Place on a parchment-lined sheet, cover with an inverted baking sheet, and proof 30 min. Spray with water, score a long centre line with a razor, and bake at 400°F with a small pan of boiling water underneath for steam. Steam 8–10 min, then remove the water pan, drop to 375°F, and bake 20–22 min more until lightly browned. Cool completely.
4. **Braise & shred the pork (3–3.5 hr):** Place the pork in a large Dutch oven. Pour in all the marinade from the bag (it should come halfway up the meat; top off with chicken stock if not). Bake uncovered at 400°F for 10 min, then lower to 350°F. Cook 3–3.5 hr, turning occasionally, until tender and browned. Rest the meat, scrape up the caramelised solids/sauce from the pot, shred the pork, and toss it with those pan solids.
5. **Sear the fillings & build (10 min):** Slice a length of bread in half. Butter the inside faces and toast face-down in a pan until golden. In the same pan, add a little butter and sear the ham until coloured on both sides. Coat both toasted faces with yellow mustard. On the bottom: layer pickles, seared ham, a mountain of shredded mojo pork, and Swiss cheese (optionally torch to start it melting). Top with the upper bread.
6. **Press (5 min):** Spread softened butter over the outside top and bottom of the sandwich. Place in a cold pan over medium heat and set a heavy preheated cast-iron skillet on top as a press. Once the bottom is crisp and golden, flip, replace the skillet, and press until the cheese is fully melted and the crust shatters. Slice diagonally and serve.
