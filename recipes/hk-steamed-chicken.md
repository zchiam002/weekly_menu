# HK-Style Steamed Chicken (蒸滑鸡)

> Simple, silky Cantonese steamed chicken — boneless chicken leg marinated with ginger, garlic, soy and oyster sauce, then steamed until tender. A quick one-plate classic.

**Servings:** 3–4

## Ingredients

- 2 boneless chicken legs (~480g), cut into smaller pieces
- 10g young ginger, cut into thin strips
- 3 cloves garlic, chopped
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1.5 tbsp cornflour
- 1 tbsp sesame oil

## Method

1. **Marinate:** Mix the chicken pieces with the ginger strips, chopped garlic, light soy sauce, oyster sauce, cornflour and sesame oil until evenly coated. Marinate ~20–30 min for better flavour.
2. **Plate:** Spread the marinated chicken in a single layer on a heatproof plate, arranging the ginger over the top.
3. **Steam:** Steam over rapidly boiling water for ~12–15 min, until the chicken is cooked through and tender (juices run clear).
4. **Serve:** Spoon the steaming juices over the chicken and serve hot with rice. Garnish with spring onion if you like.
