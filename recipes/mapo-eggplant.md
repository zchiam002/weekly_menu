# Mapo Eggplant 麻婆茄子

> Eggplant pan-fried until softened, then simmered with minced pork in a doubanjiang and Sichuan pepper sauce and thickened.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (300g eggplant, 200g minced pork)

## Ingredients

### Eggplant base

- Oil, a suitable amount
- 300g eggplant, cut into pieces, soaked in salted water and dabbed dry

### Aromatics & meat

- A little more oil
- 20g young ginger, chopped
- 5 cloves garlic, chopped
- 200g minced pork — the creator notes minced chicken, beef or mutton / lamb can be used instead

### Sauce & seasoning

- 1½ tbsp spicy broad bean paste (doubanjiang)
- 1 tsp powdered Sichuan pepper (Chinese prickly ash powder)
- A few dashes pepper
- 250ml water
- Cornstarch solution (quantity not specified)
- 1 bunch spring onions, chopped

## Method

1. **Pan-fry the eggplant:** Heat a suitable amount of oil in a wok over medium heat. Add the prepared eggplant, spread it out and cook, moving it around occasionally, for a few minutes until softened, lightly browned and slightly translucent. Remove and set aside.
2. **Sauté the aromatics:** In the same pan add a little more oil over medium heat. Fry the young ginger until fragrant, then the garlic until fragrant.
3. **Cook the minced pork:** Add the minced pork, breaking it apart, and cook over medium heat until no redness remains.
4. **Fry the spicy pastes and spices:** Add the spicy broad bean paste, powdered Sichuan pepper and a few dashes of pepper. Stir-fry until the oil turns red and fragrant.
5. **Add liquid and simmer:** Add the water and mix well. Add the cooked eggplant and spread it out. Cover and simmer for 5 minutes.
6. **Thicken and finish:** Remove the lid, turn the heat to medium-low and slowly stir in the cornstarch solution until the sauce is as thick as you like. Add the chopped spring onions, stir well and serve.
