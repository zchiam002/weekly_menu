# Mapo Eggplant (麻婆茄子)

> Chinese spicy aubergine / brinjal with minced meat — cornstarch-dusted eggplant fried until golden, folded into a glossy doubanjiang and Sichuan peppercorn sauce with minced pork or chicken.

**Servings:** Not specified in source

## Ingredients

### Eggplant preparation

- 2–3 Chinese eggplants / brinjals (approx. 300–400g), cut into long strips or batons
- 1 tbsp salt, for soaking/salting the eggplant (removes excess moisture and reduces oil absorption)
- 2–3 tbsp cornstarch, for coating
- Cooking oil, for shallow or deep frying

### Meat marinade

- 150g minced pork or minced chicken
- 1 tsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine
- ½ tsp cornstarch
- A dash of white pepper

### Mapo sauce & aromatics

- 1 tbsp spicy broad bean paste (doubanjiang / 豆瓣酱)
- 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns, ground or toasted
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 thumb-sized piece ginger, minced or fine julienne
- 1–2 fresh red chillies or chilli padi, chopped, to taste
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce (for colour)
- 1 tsp sugar
- ½ cup water or chicken stock
- Cornstarch slurry (1 tsp cornstarch mixed with 1 tbsp water)
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 2 stalks spring onions / scallions, chopped, for garnish

## Method

1. **Pre-cook the eggplant:** Cut the eggplants into bite-sized batons. Soak in salted water 10–15 minutes, then drain and pat thoroughly dry with paper towels. Lightly dust with cornstarch until evenly coated. Heat oil in a wok or skillet over medium-high heat and fry or shallow-fry the eggplant 3–4 minutes until tender and golden brown. Drain on paper towels and set aside.
2. **Marinate the meat:** Mix the minced pork (or chicken) with the light soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, cornstarch and white pepper. Set aside 10 minutes.
3. **Stir-fry meat and aromatics:** Heat 1 tbsp oil in the wok over medium heat. Stir-fry the marinated mince until cooked through and slightly browned. Push the meat to the side, then add the garlic, ginger and doubanjiang. Stir-fry 1 minute until fragrant and the red oil comes out.
4. **Mapo sauce:** Stir in the light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, sugar, ground Sichuan peppercorns and water (or stock). Bring to a gentle boil. Pour in the cornstarch slurry, stirring continuously, until the sauce thickens to a glossy consistency.
5. **Combine and serve:** Gently fold in the fried eggplant, tossing briefly so it absorbs the sauce without breaking apart. Drizzle with sesame oil and toss in the fresh red chillies and spring onions. Serve hot over steamed white rice.
