# Chinese Chicken Broccoli 西兰花炒滑鸡

> Silky cornflour-marinated chicken thigh stir-fried with carrot and broccoli in an oyster and soy sauce, finished with a splash of Shaoxing wine.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (1 chicken thigh ~280g, 370g broccoli)

## Ingredients

### Chicken & marinade

- 1 piece boneless chicken thigh (~280g), cut into small pieces
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- ½ tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp Chinese cooking wine (Shaoxing jiu) — the creator notes this is optional and can be left out if non-alcoholic
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- Dashes of pepper
- 1½ tbsp cornflour

### Main & aromatics

- A little oil
- ~5g ginger, thinly sliced
- 4 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1 cup water (+ 2 tbsp extra if needed)
- 1 carrot (~180g), cut into chunks
- 370g broccoli

### Sauce & finish

- 1½ tbsp oyster sauce
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp Chinese cooking wine (Shaoxing jiu)

## Method

1. **Marinate the chicken:** Combine the chicken with the light soy sauce, oyster sauce, Shaoxing wine, sesame oil and pepper and mix well, then add the cornflour and mix again. Cover and marinate in the fridge for at least 1 hour.
2. **Rest the chicken:** Make sure the marinated chicken is at room temperature before cooking.
3. **Aromatics:** Heat a pan over medium heat with a little oil. Fry the ginger and garlic until fragrant.
4. **Boil the carrots:** Add the 1 cup of water and the carrot chunks. Cover and boil for 5–8 minutes until softened.
5. **Chicken and seasonings:** Add the room-temperature chicken and stir immediately to prevent clumping (add ~2 tbsp extra water if it's too starchy). Add the oyster sauce and light soy sauce and stir to combine, then stir in the dark soy sauce to darken the colour.
6. **Broccoli:** When the chicken is about 80% done add the broccoli. Cover and steam over slightly reduced heat for about 15 seconds, stir, then cover and steam for another 30–50 seconds until cooked and crunchy.
7. **Finish and reduce:** Splash the Shaoxing wine around the side of the pan, turn the heat up slightly and stir for about 10 seconds to reduce the sauce and make sure the chicken is fully cooked, then serve.
