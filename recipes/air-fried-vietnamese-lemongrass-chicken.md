# Air-fried Vietnamese Lemongrass Chicken 气炸越南香茅鸡

> Boneless chicken legs marinated in a blended lemongrass, fish sauce, honey and oyster sauce paste, then air-fried in two stages.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (550g boneless chicken leg)

## Ingredients

### Marinade

- 4 lemongrass, white stem only
- 2 cloves garlic, coarsely cut
- 1 thumb-size piece young ginger, coarsely cut
- 4 shallots, coarsely cut
- 3 tbsp fish sauce — the creator notes soy sauce can be used instead
- 1½ tbsp oyster sauce
- 3 tbsp honey — the creator notes brown sugar or cane sugar can be used instead
- 40ml water
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- A few dashes white pepper

### Main

- 550g boneless chicken leg, with cuts made in the thicker parts

## Method

1. **Blend the marinade:** Put the lemongrass, garlic, young ginger, shallots, fish sauce, oyster sauce, honey, water, cooking oil and white pepper in a food processor or blender and blend into a smooth paste.
2. **Marinate the chicken:** Pour the marinade over the chicken legs and massage it well into the meat. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour (preferably overnight).
3. **Rest and prep:** Take the chicken out of the fridge at least 30 minutes before air-frying. Wipe off most of the excess marinade from the skin to prevent burning.
4. **First air-fry:** Arrange the chicken in a single layer in the air fryer basket (in batches if needed). Air-fry at 150°C for 10 minutes. (If using an oven instead, preheat to 180°C for at least 30 minutes and bake for 20 minutes.)
5. **Second air-fry and serve:** Air-fry again at 180°C for 5 minutes until browned and cooked through, then serve.
