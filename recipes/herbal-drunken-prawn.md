# Herbal Drunken Prawn 药材醉虾

> Tiger prawns marinated in Shaoxing wine, then cooked in a herbal chicken stock of yu zhu, dang gui, dang shen, red dates and ginger and finished with wolfberries. Restaurant-style.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (based on 600g tiger prawns)

## Ingredients

### Prawn marinade

- 600g tiger prawns
- 125ml Shaoxing wine (Chinese cooking wine) — the creator notes 2 tbsp brandy can be used as a substitute

### Herbal stock base

- 1L chicken stock
- 20g yu zhu 玉竹
- 10g angelica root (dang gui 当归)
- 2–3 pieces young ginger, thinly sliced
- 8g dang shen 党参
- 2 red dates, pitted and halved
- 2 stems spring onion, white parts only
- 125ml Shaoxing wine

### Seasoning & finish

- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp wolfberries (goji berries 枸杞)

## Method

1. **Marinate the prawns:** Combine the tiger prawns and 125ml Shaoxing wine in a bowl, mix well and marinate in the fridge for 30 minutes. Take them out 10–15 minutes before cooking so they come up to room temperature.
2. **Combine the stock base:** In an unheated pot or wok, combine the chicken stock, yu zhu, angelica root, ginger, dang shen, red dates, spring onion whites and the second 125ml of Shaoxing wine.
3. **Initial boil:** Cover and bring to a boil over medium heat.
4. **Steep the herbs:** Turn off the heat and, keeping it covered, steep the herbs in the stock for 20 minutes.
5. **Re-boil:** Put the lid back on and bring the stock back to a boil over medium heat.
6. **Cook the prawns:** Add the marinated prawns together with their marinade, cover and cook over medium heat for 3–5 minutes until fully cooked.
7. **Season and finish:** Turn off the heat, stir in the salt, sugar and wolfberries, and serve.
