# Herbal Drunken Prawns

> Restaurant-style Chinese Shaoxing wine shrimp in a herbal chicken stock — 药材醉虾

**Servings:** 3-4 (not stated in the video; estimated from 600g prawns)

## Ingredients

### Prawn marinade
- 600g tiger prawns
- 125ml Shaoxing Huatiao wine (or 2 tbsp brandy)

### Herbal stock
- 1L chicken stock
- 20g yuzhu 玉竹
- 10g angelica root 当归
- 8g dangshen 党参
- 3 thin slices young ginger
- 2 red dates 红枣, pitted and halved
- 2 stalks spring onion (white part only)
- 125ml Shaoxing Huatiao wine

### Seasoning & finish
- 0.5 tsp salt (or to taste)
- 0.5 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp wolfberries / goji berries 枸杞

## Preparation

1. Marinate the prawns: mix the prawns with 125ml Shaoxing Huatiao wine in a large bowl. Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
2. Take the prawns out of the fridge 10–15 minutes before cooking so they cook evenly.
3. In an unheated pot or wok, add the chicken stock, yuzhu, angelica root, dangshen, ginger, red dates, spring onion stems and 125ml Shaoxing Huatiao wine.
4. Cover, set to medium heat and bring to a boil.
5. Once boiling, turn off the heat, keep the lid on and steep the herbs for 20 minutes.
6. Cover and bring the stock back to a boil over medium heat.
7. Add all the prawns with their marinade. Cover and cook on medium for 3–5 minutes, until completely orange-red and cooked through.
8. Uncover, taste the broth, then add the salt, sugar and wolfberries. Stir well and serve hot.
