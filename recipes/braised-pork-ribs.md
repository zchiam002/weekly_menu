# Braised Pork Ribs

> Prime ribs slow-braised with star anise and cinnamon in a sweet-savoury soy and rice wine gravy, finished sticky. Falls off the bone.

**Servings:** 3–4

## Ingredients

- 500g prime ribs (spare ribs or beef ribs also work)
- 1 star anise
- 1 piece cinnamon bark (5–7cm)
- 1 thumb-size young ginger, minced
- 2 shallots, minced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup Chinese rice wine
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp hoisin sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 2 tbsp low-sodium light soy sauce
- 2 tbsp premium dark soy sauce
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar (or apple cider vinegar)
- Water, enough to cover the ribs

## Method

1. **Aromatics:** Heat a little oil in a pot and fry the minced ginger, shallots and garlic with the star anise and cinnamon bark until fragrant.
2. **Sear:** Add the ribs and brown them on all sides.
3. **Deglaze:** Pour in the Chinese rice wine and let it bubble.
4. **Season:** Add the brown sugar, hoisin sauce, oyster sauce, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce and rice vinegar. Stir to coat the ribs.
5. **Braise:** Add enough water to cover the ribs. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer covered until the meat is tender and pulling from the bone.
6. **Reduce:** Uncover and reduce the gravy until it thickens and glazes the ribs. Serve with the sauce spooned over.
