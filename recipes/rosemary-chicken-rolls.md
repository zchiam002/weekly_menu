# Rosemary Chicken Rolls 迷迭香鸡肉卷

> Skin-on chicken thighs scored, rubbed with rosemary and brushed with a ketchup, soy, oyster and honey barbecue sauce, then rolled in foil and air-fried, unwrapped and glazed.

**Servings:** Not specified in source — makes 4 rolls (one per thigh)

## Ingredients

### Barbecue sauce

- 4 tbsp tomato ketchup
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1½ tbsp honey

### Chicken

- 4 pieces boneless chicken thigh, skin-on (~200–230g each)
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 2 tsp rosemary (dried or fresh)

### Other

- A little oil, for rubbing on the aluminium foil
- Aluminium foil, a ~30 × 30cm sheet per roll
- About 1 cup water, for the air fryer drawer

## Method

1. **Barbecue sauce:** In a bowl combine the ketchup, light soy sauce, oyster sauce and honey. Mix well and set aside.
2. **Score and season:** Lightly score the flesh side of the thighs with a knife. Mix the salt, black pepper and rosemary and rub it into the chicken.
3. **Sauce and marinate:** Brush the barbecue sauce liberally over the flesh side, leaving the skin side plain. Marinate in the fridge for at least 2 hours, or overnight.
4. **Roll and wrap:** Pour a little oil onto a ~30 × 30cm sheet of foil and rub to spread. Place a thigh skin-side down, roll tightly into a log, wrap in the foil and twist both ends tight to compact it. Repeat with the remaining thighs.
5. **First air-fry:** Pour about 1 cup of water into the air fryer drawer to help it cook evenly. Put the foil-wrapped rolls in and cook at 200°C for 20 minutes.
6. **Unwrap and baste:** Take the rolls out and carefully remove the foil. Return them to the air fryer and baste generously with the remaining barbecue sauce.
7. **Second air-fry:** Air-fry at 200°C for another 5 minutes until browned and glossy.
