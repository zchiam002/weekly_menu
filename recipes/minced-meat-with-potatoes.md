# Minced Meat with Potatoes 家常肉末土豆

> Shallow-fried potato slices simmered with minced pork in an oyster, light and dark soy gravy, thickened and finished with spring onion.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (400g potatoes, 200g minced pork)

## Ingredients

### Main & aromatics

- 400g potatoes, thinly sliced
- Oil, for shallow frying (quantity not specified), plus 1 tbsp kept after frying
- 4 cloves garlic, chopped
- 200g minced pork

### Seasoning & sauce

- ~⅓ tsp salt
- A few dashes pepper
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 250ml water
- Cornstarch solution (quantity not specified)
- Spring onions (quantity not specified)

## Method

1. **Shallow-fry the potatoes:** Heat a wok or pan of oil over medium heat until hot (test with a bamboo skewer until bubbles rise). Add the potato slices and fry for about 3 minutes until the sides turn slightly brown. Remove and set aside. (The creator notes the potatoes can be air-fried instead, at 180°C for about 15 minutes.)
2. **Garlic:** Pour off most of the oil, leaving 1 tbsp, over medium heat. Add the garlic and fry until fragrant without browning.
3. **Brown the pork and season:** Add the minced pork and stir-fry, breaking it up so it cooks evenly and releases its fat. Add the salt and pepper. Fry until no redness remains.
4. **Sauces and water:** Stir in the oyster sauce, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce and sugar. Mix until glossy, then pour in the water.
5. **Simmer with the potatoes:** Add the fried potatoes and spread them out gently. Cover, turn the heat to medium-low and simmer for about 5 minutes.
6. **Thicken and finish:** Uncover, turn the heat to medium and stir in the cornstarch solution to thicken the sauce. Add the spring onions, stir to combine and serve.
