# Hainanese Oxtail Stew

> Hainanese-Western oxtail stew — flour-dredged oxtail fried until lightly browned, then braised two hours in tomato, beef stock and soy with cinnamon, star anise and cloves. Served with toasted baguette. The Meatmen Channel.

**Servings:** 4–6 (based on 2 kg of oxtail)

## Ingredients

### Oxtail & coating

- 2 kg oxtail
- Plain flour, for coating the oxtail *(quantity not stated)*
- Oil, for deep frying *(quantity not stated)*

### Aromatics & vegetables

- 2 carrots, peeled and cut into chunks
- 4 stalks celery, chopped
- 2 onions, cut into wedges
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 50g butter
- Salt & pepper, to taste

### Sauce & spices

- 1 can tomato paste *(can size not stated)*
- 2 cans whole tomatoes *(can size not stated)*
- 2 tbsp plain flour, for thickening
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 3 star anise
- 5 cloves
- 2 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 litre beef stock

### To serve

- Toasted baguette slices
- Fresh coriander, to garnish

## Method

1. **Prep the vegetables:** Peel and cut the carrots into chunks. Chop the celery. Cut the onions into large wedges.
2. **Dredge and fry the oxtail:** Coat the oxtail thoroughly in plain flour. Heat oil in a deep pan and fry the oxtail until lightly browned on all sides. Set aside on a wire rack to drain.
3. **Sauté the aromatics:** In a large heavy-bottomed pot, melt the olive oil and butter over medium heat. Add the carrots, celery and onions. Season with salt and pepper and sauté until the vegetables begin to caramelize.
4. **Build the stew base:** Stir in the tomato paste and cook through. Sprinkle in 2 tbsp plain flour and stir well to coat the vegetables. Add the canned tomatoes, then the bay leaves, cinnamon stick, star anise and cloves.
5. **Add liquids and simmer:** Pour in the dark soy sauce, light soy sauce and beef stock. Return the browned oxtail to the pot and mix evenly. Cover and simmer on low heat for 2 hours, until the meat is tender and falls easily off the bone.
6. **Serve:** Ladle into bowls, garnish with coriander, and serve with toasted baguette slices for dipping.

> **Substitution (household decision, 26 Sep):** if oxtail can't be found, beef shank. Estimates,
> not from the video: ~2–2.3kg shank for 6.5 pax, cut into ~5cm chunks, check tenderness from 2
> hours (may need up to 2½). Leaner and less bone than oxtail, so the sauce comes out a little
> less rich.
