# Chicken Tsukune (Japanese Chicken Meatballs)

> Japanese chicken meatballs — minced chicken kneaded with miso, grated onion and leek, pan-steamed until juicy, then finished either with a sweet soy tare glaze or simply salted (shio).

**Servings:** ~2–3

## Ingredients

### Meatball base

- Ground/minced chicken
- 100g onion (about half an onion)
- 50g Japanese leek (naganegi), green and white parts
- A pinch of salt
- 5g miso
- 10g sake
- 10g soy sauce
- Vegetable oil, for frying
- 30g hot water (for steaming)

### Tare (sweet soy glaze)

- 30g sake
- 30g mirin
- 30g soy sauce
- 10g brown sugar

### Shio (salt version)

- Extra pinch of salt, for seasoning

## Method

### 1. Prepare the vegetables

1. Grate the 100g onion, transfer to a clean cloth, and squeeze out the excess water (stops the patties becoming too soft).
2. Slice the leek thinly lengthwise, then finely mince the 50g.

### 2. Make the meat mixture

3. In a bowl, combine the ground chicken with a pinch of salt and the miso. Knead thoroughly until sticky.
4. Add the sake and soy sauce; mix well into the meat.
5. Mix in the squeezed grated onion and minced leek until combined.
6. Cover and chill in the fridge 30 min to set the flavours.

### 3. Shape & steam

7. Add vegetable oil to a large flat pan. Using a spoon and your hand, shape the mixture into smooth round meatballs and place in the pan.
8. Turn on the heat and cook a few minutes until the bottom turns white.
9. Pour in the 30g hot water, cover, and steam on medium heat 4 min.
10. Remove the lid and continue heating until the remaining liquid evaporates. Set the meatballs aside.

### 4. Make the tare

11. In the same pan (keeping the meat flavour), combine the sake, mirin, soy sauce and brown sugar. Cook over medium-low a few minutes until the sugar dissolves and it simmers. Strain into a small bowl.

### 5. Glaze & finish

12. **Tare (sweet soy):** Return the meatballs to the pan and sear a few minutes to brown. Pour over the tare and cook over medium heat, spooning sauce over them, until reduced and nicely glazed.
13. **Shio (salt):** Alternatively, pan-fry the cooked meatballs until browned, then sprinkle lightly with salt.

### 6. Serve

14. Optional: thread onto bamboo skewers and serve.
