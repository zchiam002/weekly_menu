# Taiwanese Braised Chicken Rice 台湾卤鸡肉饭 (Taiwanese Chicken Lu Rou Fan)

> Chicken thigh braised with shallots, dried mushrooms, five-spice and soy together with hard-boiled eggs, reduced and thickened into a gravy to spoon over steamed rice.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (~460g chicken thigh, 600ml water)

## Ingredients

### Main & aromatics

- 10 shallots, chopped
- 8 dried Chinese mushrooms, rehydrated and cut into small pieces
- 2 cloves garlic, chopped
- 2 pieces boneless, skinless chicken thigh, cut into small pieces (~460g)
- 4 hard-boiled eggs

### Sauce & seasoning

- Oil, a good amount
- 1 tsp five spice powder
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 40ml light soy sauce
- 40ml dark soy sauce
- 15g brown sugar
- 100ml rice wine (optional)
- 600ml water
- A few dashes white pepper
- Cornstarch solution (quantity not specified)

## Method

1. **Sauté the aromatics:** Heat a good amount of oil in a wok over medium heat. Add the chopped shallots and fry until fragrant. Add the rehydrated, chopped mushrooms and the garlic and stir-fry over medium heat until fragrant.
2. **Cook the chicken:** Add the chicken thigh pieces and fry over medium heat until the chicken turns white.
3. **Seasonings:** Add the five spice powder, oyster sauce, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, brown sugar and rice wine (optional). Mix thoroughly.
4. **Liquid and eggs:** Pour in the water, add a few dashes of white pepper and the hard-boiled eggs. Turn the heat to medium-high and bring to a boil.
5. **Simmer:** Cover, turn the heat down to low and simmer for 20–30 minutes.
6. **Reduce the gravy:** Remove the lid, turn the heat up to medium-high and cook down the sauce for 5–8 minutes.
7. **Thicken and finish:** Slowly stir in the cornstarch solution until thickened to your liking. Turn off the heat and serve over steamed rice.
