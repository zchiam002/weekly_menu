# Lotus Leaf Rice 荷叶饭

> Savoury glutinous rice with dried shrimp, Chinese mushrooms and lap cheong, wrapped in a lotus leaf and steamed so the leaf perfumes the whole parcel. Dim sum classic.

**Servings:** 6–8

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 30g dried shrimp, soaked 30 min *(reserve the soaking liquid)*
- 6 dried Chinese mushrooms, soaked & diced *(reserve the soaking liquid)*
- 2½ Chinese sausages (lap cheong), diced
- 300g glutinous rice, soaked 3 hours
- 150ml chicken stock
- 50ml dried shrimp soaking liquid
- 50ml mushroom soaking liquid
- 1½ tbsp oyster sauce
- 1½ tbsp premium dark soy sauce
- 1 lotus leaf, soaked 2 hours

## Method

1. **Soak ahead:** Glutinous rice 3 hours, lotus leaf 2 hours, dried shrimp 30 min, dried mushrooms until soft. Reserve the shrimp and mushroom soaking liquids.
2. **Aromatics:** Heat the oil and fry the minced garlic until fragrant. Add the soaked dried shrimp, diced mushrooms and diced Chinese sausage; stir-fry until aromatic and the sausage renders.
3. **Rice:** Add the drained glutinous rice and toss to coat in the oil and aromatics.
4. **Liquid & seasoning:** Pour in the chicken stock, the shrimp soaking liquid and the mushroom soaking liquid. Stir in the oyster sauce and dark soy sauce. Cook, stirring, until the liquid is absorbed and the rice is partly cooked.
5. **Wrap:** Lay out the softened lotus leaf, spoon the rice into the middle and fold into a parcel.
6. **Steam:** Steam the parcel until the glutinous rice is fully cooked and tender. Open at the table so the lotus fragrance is released.
