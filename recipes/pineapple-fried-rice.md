# Thai Pineapple Fried Rice 泰式凤梨炒饭

> Thai pineapple fried rice with chicken and prawns — egg-coated overnight rice stir-fried with turmeric, raisins, cashews and fresh pineapple, served in a pineapple boat.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (rice cooked from 2 cups of raw rice)

## Ingredients

### Chicken marinade

- 100g chicken thigh meat
- ½ tsp cornflour
- 1 tsp light soy sauce
- A little white pepper

### Prawn marinade

- 100g prawn meat
- ½ tsp cornflour
- 1 tsp light soy sauce
- A little white pepper

### Fried rice sauce & seasoning

- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- ½ tsp turmeric powder — the creator notes curry powder can be used instead
- 1 tsp chicken stock powder — the creator notes pork stock powder or any other stock powder can be used instead
- ½ tsp salt
- A little white pepper

### Rice & egg base

- Overnight rice, cooked from 2 cups of raw rice
- 2 eggs, beaten

### Main & aromatics

- 100g pineapple flesh, cut into small pieces
- ~2 tbsp oil
- 3 shallots, thinly sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, chopped
- 25g raisins
- 30g toasted, salted cashew nuts

## Method

1. **Pineapple boat (optional):** Cut the top off a fresh pineapple to make a boat. Score along the inner edge, leaving about an inch from the skin, cut the flesh into cubes and scoop it out with a tablespoon. Measure out 100g of flesh, cut it into smaller pieces and set aside.
2. **Marinate the chicken and prawns:** In separate bowls, marinate the chicken with its cornflour, light soy sauce and white pepper, and the prawns with theirs. Mix well and set aside.
3. **Rice and egg:** Add the beaten eggs to the overnight rice and mix thoroughly to coat the grains and break up any lumps.
4. **Mix the sauce:** In a small bowl combine the 2 tbsp light soy sauce, turmeric and chicken stock powder.
5. **Aromatics:** Heat ~2 tbsp oil in a pan. Fry the sliced shallots until light brown, then add the garlic and fry over medium heat.
6. **Chicken and prawns:** Lower the heat to medium / medium-low, add the marinated chicken and stir-fry until the outside is partly cooked. Add the prawns and cook until about 80% done.
7. **Rice and sauce:** Turn the heat to medium / medium-high, add the egg-coated rice and stir-fry to dry out the rice and cook the egg. Pour in the sauce and stir-fry until evenly coated and golden yellow. Add a little white pepper and stir.
8. **Fruit, nuts and finish:** Add the raisins, pineapple and cashews and mix well. Add the ½ tsp salt, give a final stir-fry and serve in the pineapple boat.
