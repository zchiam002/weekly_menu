# Din Tai Fung Inspired Refreshing Oriental Salad 鼎泰丰小菜

> Cold salad of blanched spiced bean curd, black fungus, bean vermicelli, soybean sprouts and seaweed in a soy, rice vinegar and sesame oil dressing, chilled for an hour.

**Servings:** Not specified in source

## Ingredients

### Salad base

- 60g spiced bean curd, blanched and drained dry
- 50g black fungus, cut into thin strips, blanched and drained dry — the creator notes dried black fungus soaked soft can be used instead
- 20g bean vermicelli (tung hoon), rehydrated and blanched
- Soybean sprouts (quantity not specified), blanched — the creator notes bean sprouts can be used instead
- 5g Japanese seaweed (dried seaweed), rehydrated

### Dressing

- 3 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp white rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp chilli oil (optional)

## Method

1. **Blanch the spiced bean curd:** Heat a pan of water, add the spiced bean curd for a quick blanch, remove immediately and drain dry.
2. **Blanch the black fungus:** In the same water give the thin strips of black fungus a short blanch, then remove and drain dry.
3. **Blanch the vermicelli:** Blanch the rehydrated bean vermicelli for about a minute until soft, then remove and drain dry.
4. **Blanch the soybean sprouts:** Blanch the sprouts in the boiling water (short for crunchy / al dente, longer for soft) and drain dry.
5. **Assemble:** In a mixing bowl combine the black fungus, spiced bean curd, soybean sprouts, bean vermicelli and rehydrated seaweed.
6. **Dress and mix:** Add the light soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil and chilli oil (optional). Mix thoroughly.
7. **Chill and serve:** Refrigerate for 1 hour so it cools and takes up the dressing before serving.
