# Oven-Baked BBQ Baby Back Ribs (3-2-1 method)

> Mr. Make It Happen's fall-off-the-bone ribs — a mustard-and-liquid-smoke bind, a heavy BBQ rub, then baked dry, foil-braised over butter/brown sugar/honey, and glazed with a homemade chipotle BBQ sauce.

**Servings:** 3–4 (1 rack) — *scale to ~2 racks for 5.5*

## Ingredients

### Rib prep & seasoning
- 1 full rack baby back ribs (or spare ribs)
- A few dashes liquid smoke
- 1 tbsp yellow mustard (binder)
- All-purpose seasoning (or a low-sodium garlic/onion/salt blend)
- BBQ rub (favourite low-sodium)

### The wrap (braise)
- 4–6 tbsp unsalted butter, sliced into pats
- ¼ cup light or dark brown sugar
- 2–3 tbsp honey
- ¼–⅓ cup liquid — apple/pineapple juice or chicken stock *(recipe uses light beer; use juice/stock for a no-alcohol braise)*

### Homemade chipotle BBQ sauce
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter
- ¼ cup brown sugar
- 1 cup ketchup
- A few dashes liquid smoke
- 2–3 tbsp adobo sauce (liquid only from chipotle in adobo — omit whole peppers to keep it mild)
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp molasses
- A pinch of AP seasoning / salt / black pepper

## Method

1. **Trim & bind:** Trim excess fat; remove the silver-skin membrane. Rub in a little liquid smoke, then coat both sides with yellow mustard.
2. **Season & dry bake:** Dust with AP seasoning, then a heavy BBQ rub. Bake uncovered, meat-side up, at 275°F (135°C) for 2½–3 hours until the bark sets.
3. **Foil braise:** On heavy foil lay half the butter, brown sugar, honey and the juice/stock. Place ribs meat-side down; top with the remaining butter/sugar/honey. Wrap tight and bake at 275°F another 1½–2 hours until a toothpick slides in with no resistance.
4. **Sauce:** Simmer the sauce ingredients until thick, dark and glossy.
5. **Glaze & set:** Unwrap, flip meat-side up, brush a heavy layer of sauce. Raise oven to 325°F (163°C) and bake uncovered 10–15 min until sticky and caramelised. Slice and serve.
