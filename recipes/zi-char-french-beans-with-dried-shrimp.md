# Singapore Zi Char Style French Beans with Dried Shrimp 虾米炒四季豆

> Shallow-fried French beans tossed in a light oyster-soy sauce with garlic and finely chopped dried shrimp, topped with slowly fried crispy dried shrimp.

**Servings:** Not specified in source (500g French beans, 40g dried shrimp)

## Ingredients

### Sauce

- 50ml water
- ½ tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- ½ tsp sugar
- ½ tbsp corn flour

### Main & aromatics

- Oil (quantity not specified)
- 500g French beans
- 40g dried shrimp, soaked in water for about 10 minutes — half chopped very small, half roughly chopped
- 5–6 cloves garlic, chopped

## Method

1. **Sauce:** In a bowl combine the water, oyster sauce, light soy sauce, sugar and corn flour. Mix thoroughly and set aside.
2. **Shallow-fry the beans:** Heat a wok of oil over medium heat (test with a bamboo chopstick until bubbles rise rapidly). Add the French beans and shallow-fry until the skin blisters and withers slightly. Lift out and set aside.
3. **Crispy dried shrimp topping:** Pour off most of the oil, leaving about 2–3 tbsp. Turn the heat to the lowest (or medium-low). Add the roughly chopped dried shrimp (about 20g) and fry slowly until the colour changes, the moisture is gone and the bubbling subsides. Remove and set aside.
4. **Garlic and chopped dried shrimp:** Leave about 1 tbsp of oil in the wok over medium heat. Add the finely chopped dried shrimp (about 20g) and the garlic and fry until fragrant.
5. **Combine beans and sauce:** Return the fried beans to the wok and mix well. Lower the heat slightly and gradually pour in the sauce, stirring constantly, to coat every bean evenly.
6. **High-heat toss:** Turn the heat to high to dry the sauce up slightly. Add the crispy dried shrimp, toss quickly, turn off the heat and serve.
