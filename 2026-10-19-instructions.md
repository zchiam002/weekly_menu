## Sunday (18 Oct)

```
*Sun 18 Oct — step by step* (dinner for 3)

*1. Thai green curry* (Dancing Chef packet. Follow the packet for the amount of paste. The video makes its own paste, so follow these steps instead)
You need:
- 3 chicken thighs
- 1 eggplant
- 1 small carrot
- 80g long beans
- 1 packet Dancing Chef green curry paste
- 100ml water
- 200ml Kara coconut milk
- 2 kaffir lime leaves
- ½ tbsp sugar
- A little oil
1. Cut the chicken into bite-size pieces. Cut the carrot thin and the eggplant into bite-size pieces
2. Cut the ends off the long beans and throw the ends away
3. Blanch the long beans whole in hot water, drain, then cut them into 4cm lengths
4. Sear the chicken in a hot pot with a little oil until browned on the outside
5. Add the eggplant, carrot and the blanched long beans and stir-fry for 1–2 minutes. Take the vegetables out onto a plate
6. Add the curry paste to the pot with the chicken and fry until fragrant
7. Pour in the water, then the coconut milk. Add the sugar and the kaffir lime leaves (centre stem removed, leaves twisted)
8. Simmer for 15 minutes
9. Put the vegetables back in for the last 10 minutes of the simmer only, until soft
10. Cut the biggest piece of chicken open: no pink

*2. Jasmine rice* (basic method, for 3 pax)
You need:
- 3 cups jasmine rice
1. Wash the rice until the water runs nearly clear
2. Cook in the rice cooker with water to the 3-cup line
```

## Monday (19 Oct)

```
*Mon 19 Oct — step by step*
Do ahead: measure the glutinous rice with the rice-cooker cup, then soak it for 2 hours. Soak the dried shrimp (about 20 min) and the dried mushrooms. Start the rice cooker about 1 hour before dinner

*1. Fragrant glutinous rice 腊味糯米饭* (for 5.5 pax)
You need:
- Glutinous rice for 5.5 pax
- 300g pork belly
- 2 Chinese sausages
- 60g dried shrimp
- 12 dried mushrooms
- 3 tbsp oyster sauce
- 3 tbsp premium dark soy sauce
- White pepper
- Water: 1:1 with the rice
- A little oil
1. Measure the rice with the rice-cooker cup before soaking. You will need the same number of cups of water later. Soak the rice for about 2 hours, then drain
2. Soak the dried shrimp for about 20 minutes, then drain. Soak the dried mushrooms until soft, then cut them into strips
3. Cut the pork belly into thin strips. Take the casing off the sausages and slice them thinly
4. Heat a little oil in a wok. Stir-fry the mushrooms, then the dried shrimp, until fragrant
5. Add the pork belly and stir-fry until it changes colour and some fat comes out
6. Add the sausage slices and stir-fry 1–2 minutes, without burning
7. Add the drained rice, oyster sauce, dark soy sauce and white pepper. Stir-fry 2–3 minutes until every grain is coated
8. Move everything to the rice cooker. Add water 1:1 with the rice (the same number of cups) and press everything down under the water
9. Cook on the normal rice setting, about 30–40 minutes
10. When it finishes, keep the lid closed and let it steam on keep-warm for another 15 minutes. If the middle is still hard, sprinkle 2–3 tbsp water, stir and cook or steam another 10–15 minutes
11. In all, about 45–55 minutes in the rice cooker
12. Fluff. The pork belly and sausage must be cooked through and hot. No extra salt, it is salty already

*2. Stir fried vegetables* (basic method)
You need:
- 2 bunches choy sum (about 1kg)
- 5 cloves garlic, chopped
- 2 tbsp oil
- Salt
1. Wash the choy sum well and cut into 5cm lengths
2. Heat the oil and fry the garlic until fragrant
3. Add the vegetables, a splash of water and a pinch of salt. Stir-fry on high heat 2–3 minutes until just tender. Cook in two batches if the wok is crowded
```

## Tuesday (20 Oct)

```
*Tue 20 Oct — step by step*
Do ahead: tonight, move the frozen chicken wings from the freezer to the fridge to thaw for Wednesday

*1. Thai style seabass 泰式蒸鱼* (one fish. The written recipe has no method, so these steps follow its ingredients and our steaming times. Check the video)
You need:
- 1 seabass (about 700g), descaled and cleaned
- 5 tbsp lime juice (2–3 limes)
- 50g garlic (about 10 cloves)
- 4–5 Thai chillies
- 3 coriander roots
- 3½ tbsp sugar
- 4 tbsp fish sauce
- 2 kaffir lime leaves
- A few lime slices
- Gloves
1. Wear gloves. Cut the chillies open and remove all the seeds
2. Finely chop the garlic, chillies and coriander roots. Mix them with the lime juice, sugar and fish sauce
3. Simmer the sauce for 1–2 minutes, then set it aside
4. Put the kaffir lime leaves on a plate and the fish on top
5. Steam over a rolling boil: about 10–11 minutes for a 700g fish (600g: 9–10 min, 800g: 11–12 min)
6. Check at the backbone: white all through and lifting off the bone
7. Pour the sauce over the fish and add the lime slices. No coriander leaves on top

*2. Si ji dou with dried shrimp sambal 叁巴虾米四季豆* (the sambal uses the technique in the recipe link at the bottom. That recipe is for okra, we use it for the beans)
You need:
- 2 packets of French beans
- About 3 tbsp oil
Dried shrimp sambal:
- 40g dried shrimp
- 8 cloves garlic
- 10 shallots (or ½ red onion)
- 2 red chillies
- 2 chilli padi (optional, for more heat)
- 2 heaped tbsp sambal belacan paste
- A pinch of salt
- 2 tbsp Shaoxing wine
- Gloves
1. Cut the ends off the beans and throw them away
2. Blanch the beans whole in hot water, drain, then cut them into short lengths
3. Soak the dried shrimp in about 200ml hot water for 20 minutes until soft. Keep the soaking water
4. Pound the soaked shrimp, garlic and shallots with the mortar and pestle into a rough paste
5. Wear gloves. Remove all the chilli seeds and slice the chillies thinly
6. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a wok. Stir-fry the beans until soft and blistered, then take them out
7. Add 1 tbsp oil. Fry the pounded paste until fragrant
8. Add the sambal belacan paste, the chillies, the salt and a few tbsp of the shrimp soaking water. Stir-fry 1–2 minutes
9. Return the beans and toss until coated and hot
10. Pour the Shaoxing wine down the hot sides of the wok and keep tossing for 2 minutes so it cooks off

*3. Steamed fish paste with minced pork* (family recipe)
You need:
- 2 tubs BoBo xi dao fish paste
- 2 small packets minced pork (about a third of the fish paste)
- Garlic paste, a quarter to a third of the fish paste (about 20–25 cloves, minced very fine)
- White pepper
- A little cooking oil
- No soy sauce, no salt
1. Put the fish paste, minced pork, garlic paste and white pepper in a bowl
2. Mix until sticky: stir in one direction for 2–3 minutes, then slap the mixture against the bowl until it holds together
3. Press it flat on a plate, about 2cm thick
4. Drizzle a little cooking oil on top
5. Steam over a rolling boil for 15 minutes
6. Cut into the centre to check: no pink, hot all the way through

*4. Steamed rice* (basic method)
You need:
- Jasmine rice for 5.5 pax
1. Wash the rice until the water runs nearly clear
2. Cook in the rice cooker with water to the line for that amount
```

## Wednesday (21 Oct)

```
*Wed 21 Oct — step by step*
Do ahead: early this morning, buy 16 fishballs and 1 pack of wawa vegetable at the market. The chicken wings should already be thawed (moved to the fridge last night): marinate them this morning, at least 2 hours. Soak the dried mushrooms and dried shrimp before the yam rice. Tonight, move the frozen threadfin from the freezer to the fridge for Thursday

*1. Yam rice with pork belly 芋头饭* (for 5.5 pax)
You need:
- Jasmine rice for 5.5 pax (measure it with the rice-cooker cup)
- 400g pork belly
- Half a yam
- 10 dried mushrooms
- 50g dried shrimp
- 3 shallots
- 6 cloves garlic
- 1½ tbsp light soy sauce
- 2¼ tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1½ tsp chicken stock powder
- ¾ tsp salt
- White pepper
- Water: 1:1 with the rice
- A little oil
- Gloves (for the yam)
1. Soak the dried mushrooms until soft, then cut them into strips. Soak the dried shrimp until soft, then drain
2. Wear gloves and peel the yam, or peel it under running water. Cut it into chunks
3. Cut the pork belly into thin slices. Thinly slice the shallots and chop the garlic
4. Wash the rice and drain it well
5. Heat a little oil in a wok. Fry the shallots until fragrant and lightly golden. Add the garlic, mushrooms and dried shrimp and stir-fry until fragrant
6. Add the pork belly and stir-fry until it changes colour. Season with the salt and white pepper
7. Add the yam and toss to coat
8. Stir in the light soy sauce, ¾ tbsp of the dark soy sauce and the stock powder
9. Add the rice and stir to coat every grain
10. Move everything to the rice cooker. Add water 1:1 with the rice and the other 1½ tbsp dark soy sauce. Stir once and level it
11. Cook on the normal rice setting. When it finishes, keep the lid closed on keep-warm for another 10 minutes and check the yam is soft
12. Fluff. Check a thick piece of pork belly: no pink

*2. Baked chicken wings 酱烧蚝油鸡翅* (2 per person)
You need:
- 12 chicken mid wings, fully thawed
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- ¼ tsp sugar
- ¼ tsp salt
- 2 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1 stalk spring onion, smashed
- White pepper
- Baking paper
1. Mix the marinade with the wings and massage well. Cover and marinate in the fridge for at least 2 hours
2. Let the wings rest at room temperature before baking. Throw away any leftover marinade
3. Preheat the oven to 200°C (top and bottom fan). Line a tray with baking paper and space the wings apart
4. Bake 20 minutes at 200°C, then about 10 minutes at 220°C to brown. Mid wings are small, so start checking at 20 minutes
5. Cut the thickest wing open at the bone: no pink, juices clear

*3. Fishball soup with wawa vegetable* (household recipe, for 5.5 pax)
You need:
- 16 fishballs (bought fresh this morning)
- 1 pack wawa vegetable
- 2L chicken broth
- 1–2 tsp anchovy powder
- A little oil
1. Wash the wawa vegetable well and cut it into pieces
2. Heat a little oil in a pot, pour in the broth and bring to a simmer
3. Add the anchovy powder a little at a time, tasting
4. Add the wawa vegetable and simmer until soft, about 5 minutes
5. Add the fishballs and simmer until they float and are hot all the way through, about 3–5 minutes
```

```
*Thu 22 Oct — step by step*
Do ahead: the threadfin should already be thawed (moved to the fridge last night). Early this morning, buy 1 bunch of green vegetable (e.g. choy sum) at the market. Thaw the pork slices in the fridge if they are frozen. Tonight, if the prawns for Friday are frozen, move them to the fridge

*1. Pan-fried threadfin with large spring onion (leek)* (household recipe, Jayzel knows this one)
You need:
- 1 whole threadfin (about 1.2kg), thawed
- 1 large leek
- Oil
- Salt
- White pepper
1. Pat the fish dry
2. Split the leek lengthways and wash it very well, grit hides between the layers. Slice it
3. Pan-fry the fish and the leek the way you normally do
4. Check at the backbone: white all through and lifting off the bone

*2. Enoki with pork wrap 日式金菇猪肉卷* (for 5.5 pax, 16 rolls)
You need:
- 500g thinly sliced pork (shabu-shabu)
- 300g enoki
- Oil
Sauce:
- 6 tbsp water
- 10g ginger, grated
- 2 red shallots, grated
- 2 cloves garlic, grated
- 4 tbsp mirin
- 5 tbsp Japanese soy sauce
- 2 tbsp honey
1. Cut the roots off the enoki, wash, pat dry and divide into 16 portions
2. Lay 2 slices of pork flat, overlapping by half. Put one portion of enoki near the end and roll up tightly
3. Mix the sauce in a bowl
4. Heat a pan with oil. Place the rolls seam-side down first and sear all sides until browned. Do 2 batches
5. Pour in the sauce and simmer, letting it reduce, until the enoki is soft and hot all through and the meat is glazed
6. Cut one roll open: no pink, and the enoki is soft. Never serve enoki raw or half-cooked

*3. Stir fried vegetable* (basic method)
You need:
- 1 bunch green vegetable (about 500g)
- 3 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1 tbsp oil
- Salt
1. Wash the vegetable well and cut into 5cm lengths
2. Heat the oil and fry the garlic until fragrant
3. Add the vegetable, a splash of water and a pinch of salt. Stir-fry on high heat 2–3 minutes until just tender

*4. Steamed rice* (basic method)
You need:
- Jasmine rice for 5.5 pax
1. Wash the rice until the water runs nearly clear
2. Cook in the rice cooker with water to the line for that amount
```

```
*Fri 23 Oct — step by step*
Do ahead: the prawns should already be thawed (moved to the fridge last night). Early this morning, buy 1 bunch of green vegetable (e.g. choy sum) at the market

*1. Thai basil minced pork (pad kra pao) 泰式打抛猪肉* (for 5.5 pax. The written recipe has ingredients only, so the amounts and steps are estimates. Check the video)
You need:
- 500g minced pork
- 10 cloves garlic
- 6 bird's eye chillies
- 2 mild red chillies
- 1 onion, diced
- 100g long beans
- 1 bunch holy basil (leaves only)
- 5 eggs (one fried egg each)
- Oil
- Gloves
Sauce:
- 1½ tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 4 tbsp water
1. Wear gloves. Cut the chillies open and remove all the seeds. Chop the chillies and garlic together
2. Cut the ends off the long beans and throw them away. Blanch them whole in hot water, drain, then cut them into 1cm pieces
3. Mix the sauce ingredients
4. Heat oil in a wok on high. Stir-fry the garlic and chillies for a few seconds until fragrant
5. Add the minced pork and break it up. Keep frying until the pork is *dark brown*, not just browned, with no pink left
6. Add the onion and long beans, then the sauce. Cook until the sauce coats the pork
7. Turn off the heat and fold in the holy basil leaves until just wilted
8. Fry the eggs in hot oil until the whites are crisp and the yolks are cooked firm, not runny
9. Serve over rice with an egg on top. No raw chilli fish sauce on the side

*2. Stir-fried eggs and prawns* (household recipe, basic method)
You need:
- 6 eggs
- 250g peeled prawns, thawed
- 2 stalks spring onion, cut short
- 2 tbsp oil
- White pepper
- A pinch of salt
1. Devein the prawns and pat them dry. Season with a pinch of salt and white pepper
2. Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt
3. Heat the oil and stir-fry the prawns until pink, opaque and curled, with no grey translucent flesh. Take them out
4. Pour in the eggs. When the edges start to set, add the prawns and spring onion
5. Stir until the eggs are cooked firm: set curds, no wet or glossy bits

*3. Stir fried vegetable* (basic method)
You need:
- 1 bunch green vegetable (about 500g)
- 3 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1 tbsp oil
- Salt
1. Wash the vegetable well and cut into 5cm lengths
2. Heat the oil and fry the garlic until fragrant
3. Add the vegetable, a splash of water and a pinch of salt. Stir-fry on high heat 2–3 minutes until just tender

*4. Steamed rice* (basic method)
You need:
- Jasmine rice for 5.5 pax
1. Wash the rice until the water runs nearly clear
2. Cook in the rice cooker with water to the line for that amount
```
