## Decisions on the record

- **The menu is the user's own (3 Oct).** Four blanks were filled by Claude and are picks, not
  the user's choices:
  - **Monday's soup ("no bamboo shoot") → Watercress pork rib soup** (exactly 21d, clears). Light
    and cooling beside a rich, salty pork belly. The other reading — winter melon and jin hua ham
    soup without the bamboo shoots — was passed over because Monday already has two salty
    cured/braised pork dishes, and cured ham would make three.
  - **Tuesday's "some steamed fish" → Steamed baby threadfin** (28d), Cantonese style, non-spicy.
    It is the child's dish that night and gentler beside spicy, fried fishcakes. Steamed pomfret
    is 2 days short; Thai-style steamed fish would make the whole night spicy.
  - **Wednesday's "fishball soup" → Fishball soup with cabbage** — never cooked, for variety, and
    cabbage holds up to day three off the shop. Fishball minced pork soup and fishball and baby
    spinach soup are both eligible alternatives.
  - **Friday's "mushroom soup" → the homemade Mushroom soup**, 10 days short of its 28-day
    cool-down; logged as an approved early repeat. Friday is rich (shepherd's pie under buttery
    mash), so leave the cream out of the soup — the recipe's own note says it stays creamy.
- **Egg as a dish is lifted** — the user asked for stir-fried eggs (3 Oct). The raw-egg rule
  still applies: cook the eggs firm, not soft or runny.
- **Monday is pork three ways** (belly, lap cheong, ribs) and the saltiest night of the week.
- **Shepherd's pie recipe is LAMB** — 1.5kg mince, serves 8, so ~1kg for 5.5. The household
  appears to have used beef last time (old inventory note). Either works.
- **Japanese curry rice** — the recipe's 2 chicken thighs for 6 servings is thin; use ~600g
  (estimate). One heaped tsp chilli powder: reduce or leave out for the child.
