# Market List — Week of 14 Sep 2026

**Mon 14 – Fri 18 Sep · 5 dinners · 6.5 pax all week · one shop, Sunday 13 Sep**

> Buta don and Taiwanese beef noodle soup are **already written at 6.5 pax** — their quantities
> are taken straight from the recipe, not scaled again. Everything else is scaled from its own
> servings line, and the five dishes with no recipe are sized by this list.

## Market

### Pork
⚠️ **TWO DIFFERENT CUTS, plus ribs. Flag it at the stall.**

- **Thinly sliced pork belly — 1.55kg.** One order covers two nights: **1.3kg Mon buta don +
  220g Thu tonjiru.** Ask for shabu-thin; ordinary "sliced" is too thick to sear in 3 minutes
- **Pork ribs — 1.2kg** (Mon watercress soup). Soup ribs, *not* prime ribs
- **Streaky pork — 600g** (Fri sweet and sour). ⚠️ Uplifted from the recipe's 325g at scale — that
  was ~50g a head before batter, too thin for the night's only meat

### Beef
- **Beef shank — 1.3–1.5kg**, cut into large cubes (Tue). Ask for shank with visible tendon; it is
  the whole point of the dish

### Chicken
- **Chicken, cut into pieces — 730g** (Wed curry). Bone-in is better in a curry
- **Minced chicken — 290g** (Thu meatballs, replacing the recipe's pork)

### Fish & seafood
- **Pomfret, whole — 1.4kg**, ideally 2 fish (Thu). Descaled, gutted
- **Threadfin, whole — ~1.4kg** (Fri). ⚠️ **Freeze on arrival Sunday, thaw Thursday night.**
  Sunday to Friday is five days — far past the chiller limit for whole fish
- **Prawns — ~450g shell-on / 290g peeled** (Thu meatballs)
- **Fuzhou fishballs — 600g** (Wed)

- **Bok choy — 6–7 heads** (Tue)
- **Tomatoes — 2** · **Apple — 1** (Tue broth)
- **Capsicum — 2** · **Fresh pineapple — half a small one** (Fri)
- **Onions — 3** (1½ Tue, 1 Thu) · **Shallots — 13** (6–7 Tue, 6 Thu)

## Do NOT buy

- **Raw egg yolks for the buta don.** The recipe offers them as an optional topping and they come
  off — raw egg is the week's one genuine avoid. If the yolk matters, use **pasteurised eggs**
- **Sesame oil** — Friday's sweet and sour marinade loses its ½ tbsp. The sesame rule was not
  lifted, unlike tomato and alcohol
- **Mushrooms of any kind** — zero instances this week, and two soups where shiitake creeps in
- **Kombu / kelp** — nothing needs it

## Cook ahead
- **Monday's watercress soup can be made Sunday** — cool it in shallow containers, and bring it
  back to a **full rolling boil**, not a warm-through
- **Tuesday's beef braise is the long one.** Blanch, braise, strain — start it early; the broth
  improves for sitting
