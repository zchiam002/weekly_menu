## Summary

**No AVOIDs this week.** Every finding is preparation-level.

**The four doneness checks that matter:**
1. **Cubano ham** — seared hot right through (the week's only listeria item)
2. **Hey zho** — cut one open after steaming
3. **Baked rice** — seafood from the *centre* of the dish, not the edge
4. **Seabass** — at the spine, steamed one fish at a time

**Week shape:**
- **Seafood-heavy Mon–Wed** (seabass, prawn rolls, salmon, then salmon + prawns + scallops), all
  low-mercury. Not a problem, just repetitive — salmon and prawns both appear two days running.
- **Iron is thin until Thursday.** The oxtail is the only strong haem-iron dish; the rib soups
  help a little. If the GP has flagged iron, Thursday matters most.
- **Two fried nights, not adjacent** (Tue hey zho + salmon; Thu oxtail is fried before braising).
- **Wednesday is the salty, rich night** — canned condensed soup beside a cheese bake.
- **Third trimester**: if heartburn has started, Tuesday (double fried) and Wednesday (cheese +
  cream soup) are the nights most likely to aggravate it.
