## Monday (21 Sep) — Chicken parm sandwich

  won't get there (Western)

  fried (Western)

## Wednesday (23 Sep) — Kong bak pau (braised pork buns)
Verdict: **CAUTION → the scale-down is the real issue, not the ingredients**

  once the braise has run its time. Check the thickest piece before serving (Western)
- **320ml Chinese rice wine goes in at the start of a 1.5–2hr braise** — fully cooked through,
  fine regardless of the lifted rule (Western)
- **⚠️ THIS IS A BIG-BATCH RECIPE — 3kg pork belly, serves 25 — scaled DOWN to ~6.5 pax means
  roughly ¾kg pork belly, not a straightforward fraction of everything.** Braising liquid, spice

  the child (pref)
