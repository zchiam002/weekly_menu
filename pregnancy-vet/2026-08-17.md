- **SODIUM is the quiet theme:** Wednesday (soy braise + salted soup), Thursday (2.5kg of soy-marinated wings and a soy gravy, **no soup to dilute it**), Tuesday's preserved vegetable. All three adjusted above.

**Net assessment:**
Nothing comes off, but this week's findings sit in an unusual place. **The cooking is unusually clean** — one written instance of alcohol against five last week, zero tomato, mostly braises and long simmers. **What needs watching is almost all at the serving end**: the prik nam pla beside the krapow, the raw cucumber plate with the satay, the communal dip bowl, basil scattered rather than wilted in. None of those appear as steps in any recipe, which is exactly why they're the ones worth writing down.

**Four things carry real weight.** Monday's **minced pork** — the one protein with bacteria distributed all the way through; 800g in a single wok steams and hides pink, so two batches is a safety instruction. Thursday's **wings at the bone**. Friday's **day-old rice**, with a trap specific to this week — Friday's rice is Thursday's rice, and Thursday's sits on keep-warm through dinner. And Friday's **satay**, where a palm-sugar marinade blackens in three minutes and reads as finished while the centre is still cold.

**Two findings the brief didn't anticipate.** The yam soup's cooking wine can simply be **deleted** rather than managed — blanching pork ribs does the 去腥 job better than wine did for the chicken, so the rib substitution resolves the week's only alcohol line for free. And the lotus root soup's **optional kelp should come out on iodine grounds** — excess iodine in pregnancy is a genuine thyroid concern, and four hours of simmering leaches a lot out of a cup of kombu.

**On heat and damp, counted honestly:** two deep-fry nights out of five, but the true picture is worse than that ratio and shouldn't be softened. Last week the two fries were separated by a steamed night. **This week Thursday and Friday fall back-to-back as the two dry nights** — both without a soup and both without a standalone vegetable — and Friday adds grill char on top of Thursday's deep-fry.
