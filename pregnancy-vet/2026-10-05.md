## Monday (5 Oct) — Watercress pork rib soup 西洋菜排骨汤
Verdict: **OK — wash it hard, cook it long**
- Scale ×1 (recipe serves 5–6): 1kg pork ribs, 500g watercress, 30g dried scallops, 10g goji,
  10g red dates, 2L water — as written (pref)
- **WASH THE WATERCRESS WELL** — soak, lift out, and repeat in fresh water two or three times;
  trim off the roots. Watercress grows in water and can carry grit and parasites. **Fully cooked
  watercress clears that** (Western)

  method)*, dark and soft. Do not drop it in late for colour (pref)
- **Blanch the ribs and discard that water.** Salt only at the very end, and taste first — the
  dried scallops give up their own salt, and Monday is already the saltiest night (pref)
- **Red dates 红枣 and goji 枸杞 with long-simmered rib — 补血 (blood-building), right for the
  third trimester**, and one of the week's two iron nights. No strong tea with this meal; the
  tannins block the iron in the plant foods (TCM/Western)
- **Watercress is cooling 寒凉** — this is a balance against the heavy pork belly, not a concern at

   **lamb mince with no pink** before it goes under the mash

**The storage plan (one shop, Sunday morning):** threadfin, curry chicken and lamb mince **into
the freezer on arrival** — threadfin down Monday evening, chicken Tuesday night, lamb Thursday
morning. Wings into the freezer too, down Monday morning. Fishballs stay frozen.

**Raw-garnish traps, three:** the threadfin's spring onion (smoking oil only, no coriander), the
mushroom soup's parsley and thyme (simmer them in), and spring onion over the eggs or japchae
(into the pan, not on top).

**No kombu or kelp this week** — no iodine note needed.

**Week shape:**
- **Protein spread:** beef (Sun, light), pork three ways plus eggs (Mon), fish and chicken (Tue),
  chicken and fishballs (Wed), lamb (Fri). Good variety; Monday is pork-heavy, and the 220g of pork
  belly is a modest share across 5.5 — the eggs and ribs carry the night.
- **Iron:** Friday's lamb is the one strong haem-iron dish. Monday's rib soup with red dates and
  goji helps. Sunday's beef is too light to count unless it goes up to 150–200g. If the GP has
  flagged iron, Friday matters most — and no strong tea at dinner on Monday or Friday.
- **Omega-3:** Tuesday's threadfin is the only source this week.
- **One fried night** — Tuesday's deep-fried wings. The pork belly is pan-fried before steaming,
  which barely counts.
- **Salt:** **Monday is clearly the saltiest night** (fermented bean curd, lap cheong, dried
  scallops, two soys). Tuesday is next (soy, salt and MSG in the wings, with no soup). Wednesday is
  moderate (curry stock powder and soy, anchovy powder in the soup). Friday's beef cubes,
  Worcestershire and Pecorino add up too. Adjustments are noted under each dish.
- **Child rule holds every day:** Sun japchae · Mon pork belly and eggs · Tue threadfin and wings ·
  **Wed curry only if the chilli powder comes out** (the fishball soup backs it up) · Fri shepherd's
  pie.
- **TCM balance:** Monday's warming five-spice belly against cooling watercress; Tuesday's heaty
  fried wings against a steamed fish and a vegetable; Friday's heaty lamb is the one to moderate if
  there are heat signs.
- **Third-trimester heartburn:** **Tuesday** (deep-fried, white pepper) is the most likely night,
  then **Monday** (fatty pork belly and lap cheong) and **Friday** (lamb under buttery mash).
  Smaller portions and eating early help.
