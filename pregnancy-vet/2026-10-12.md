**Week shape:**
- **Beef on Sunday and Thursday** are the week's iron dishes. Chicken Mon/Wed, pork Tue/Fri, fish
  only on Tuesday.
- **No soup this week**, and no seafood beyond the pomfret.
- **Rich nights back to back:** Tuesday (deep-fried rib king) and Wednesday (coconut curry and oily
  prata). In the third trimester these are the nights most likely to aggravate heartburn. Smaller
  portions of the rib king and prata would help.
- **Salt:** Tuesday and Friday are the salty nights.
- **Raw garnish:** skip the parsley on the hayashi and roast potatoes, and fold the basil into the
  hot vegetables.
