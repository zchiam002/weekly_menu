- **Fri mushrooms, herbs and corn** are day five. Keep mushrooms in a paper bag in the fridge, herbs
  wrapped in damp kitchen paper. If the corn has gone starchy, canned sweetcorn is the fallback.

**Dish notes**
- **Yam pork belly — 220g of pork is the file's own pre-scaled figure.** It reads light for 5.5,
  but Monday also has lap cheong and pork ribs. Not scaled again.
- **Fried chicken wings** — the recipe file lists ingredients only; the method (marinate, batter,
  deep-fry) is in its video. Salt cut to about 1 tsp in the marinade, MSG halved and white pepper
  moderate (Tuesday has no soup). Oil 160–170°C, 3–4 batches of 8–10 min; check the thickest wing
  at the joint.
- **Japanese curry** is a homemade roux (butter, flour, meat curry powder) — no packet curry cubes.
  Leave out the chilli powder so it stays the child's non-spicy meat dish on Wednesday.
- **Shepherd's pie mash uses butter (~70g) and milk (~70ml) — no cream.** Lamb or beef mince, about
  1kg either way. Rosemary and thyme are cooked into the filling. Bake at 180°C, 35–40 min, in a
  smaller dish than the recipe's 30 × 25cm.
- **Mushroom soup — the cream is left out this week** (the recipe's own note says it stays creamy).
  The parsley-and-thyme garnish is dropped: put all the thyme into the pot while it simmers, so
  **no parsley is bought**.
- **Steamed threadfin** — Cantonese style, non-spicy. The spring onion is shredded and goes under
  genuinely smoking oil, which is what cooks it.
- **Doneness:** threadfin opaque at the spine · eggs cooked firm, not soft · lap cheong fried until
  sizzling before the eggs go in · yam pork belly re-steamed piping hot · wings checked at the joint ·
  curry chicken no pink · mince browned with no pink · fishballs simmered right through.
- **Child rule:** Sun japchae beef · Mon yam pork belly / eggs · Tue wings and threadfin · Wed curry
  (chilli out) · Fri shepherd's pie — every cooking day has a non-spicy meat dish.
